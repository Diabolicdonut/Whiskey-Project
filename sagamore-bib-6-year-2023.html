The short version

A precisely identified six-year Maryland-made bonded rye. Producer flavor descriptions are promising, but independent reviews cannot yet be tied confidently to the 2023 bottle, so Quality and Value are intentionally unscored.

Exact identity: Sagamore Bottled in Bond Straight Rye Whiskey, third annual edition released November 2023, aged 6 years and bottled at 100 proof. Do not confuse with 2021 four-year, 2022 five-year, 2024 seven-year or 2026 eight-year bonded editions.

Background and history

Maryland rye heritage and Sagamore's own 2017-opened distillery are central to the annual bonded series.

The 2023 six-year edition follows shorter-aged annual bonded releases and precedes the separately catalogued 2024 seven-year edition. Its U.S. launch price was circa $60.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

Made entirely at Sagamore's Baltimore distillery and aged six years in new American oak at its Maryland rickhouse; a high-rye and low-rye blend from one bonded distillation season. Historical announcement dated October 2023; limited release November 2023.
