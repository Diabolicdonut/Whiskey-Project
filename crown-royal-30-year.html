The short version

A mature Coffey-still blend with substantive but conflicting judgments about sweetness, wood and finish. Its thirty-year label is authenticated; it does not make the USD 499 launch price a bargain.

Exact identity: Crown Royal Aged 30 Years Extra Rare, November 2023 Canadian blend, 46% ABV / 92 proof; the discovery ledger’s 80-proof lead is corrected.

Background and history

The November 1, 2023 primary announcement identifies the second Extra Rare Series release at USD 499 / 750 mL and 46% ABV. The exact producer photograph also reads 46%, resolving the seed’s incorrect 40% / 80-proof data.

Contemporary independent review and January 2024 tasting refer to this 30-year release. An extra year of label age does not imply that it is simply the previous 29-year blend aged one more year.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The primary November 1, 2023 launch states each component was distilled in the brand’s Coffey still. The producer product page separately identifies aging in used barrels at LaSalle. Neither record authenticates a numerical mash bill. This release’s source and oak account are not copied to the 29, 31 or 32 Year merely because they share the Extra Rare name.

Critical review synthesis

Christopher Null · Drinkhacker

Null finds considerable sweetness, butter-like texture and aged-grain fruit, but also char/polish aromas and a medicinal edge. He sees the mature wood approaching the limit of pleasant development.

Scope: November 29, 2023; exact 30-year / 92-proof release, B+; USD 499 reference.

Mark Gillespie · WhiskyCast

Gillespie finds rich flavor, well-managed sweetness and subtle spice, with a long and complex ending. This positive integration judgment is retained alongside the more critical sample.

Scope: January 2024; exact 30-year / 46% ABV release, 94 points.
