How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

New Riff Kentucky Straight Bourbon is 100 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: bread, cinnamon and brown sugar. The key question is whether that character persists as caramel with firm rye spice.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 100 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is vanilla and drying oak follow sustained warmth. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is caramel with firm rye spice. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 100 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of New Riff Kentucky Straight Bourbon.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from bread, cinnamon and brown sugar toward vanilla and drying oak follow sustained warmth. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
