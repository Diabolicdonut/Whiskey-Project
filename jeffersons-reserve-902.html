How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Jefferson’s Reserve — 90.2 proof is 90.2 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: nutty caramel, dried citrus and herbs lead to pineapple, licorice and pear. The key question is whether that character persists as pepper and roasted wood leave a lightly drying finish.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 90.2 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is pepper and roasted wood leave a lightly drying finish. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is pepper and roasted wood leave a lightly drying finish. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 90.2 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Jefferson’s Reserve — 90.2 proof.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from nutty caramel, dried citrus and herbs lead to pineapple, licorice and pear toward pepper and roasted wood leave a lightly drying finish. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
