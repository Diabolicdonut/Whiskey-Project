The short version

Classic bonded bourbon with good flavor and limited complexity.

Breaking Bourbon describes an initially alcoholic nose that opens into familiar sweet bourbon; Bourbon Culture finds fruit and useful balance without exceptional complexity. This clears the Maker’s/Bulleit midpoint, near Maker’s 46, while falling below the stronger mature or full-proof Taylor samples.

At the exact $54.95 NC standard price, the profile supports good value with a restrained premium over Buffalo Trace and Maker’s 46. Knob Creek 9 is a mature lower-cost alternative.

Scope: Standard batched 100-proof bonded bourbon. Small batch does not disclose a barrel count. 1
