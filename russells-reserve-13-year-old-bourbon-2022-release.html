How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: let fruit meet savory oak

Russell’s Reserve 13 Year Old Bourbon (2022) is 114.8 proof. Give apple and strawberry time to develop against mature wood. The point is the sweet-savory balance before the dry ending grows stronger.

Water: preserve the red fruit

Compare drops with a neat pour. Stop if fruit loses definition while the late dry oak stays insistent.

A maturity flight

Try Knob Creek 12 or the identified older Russell’s peer beside it. Match temperature and small pour size so different proof does not decide the comparison alone.

Rocks: watch the endpoint

A large cube may temper intensity, but cold can reduce the sugared-fruit impression. Taste before the finish becomes mostly dry oak.
