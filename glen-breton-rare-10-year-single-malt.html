Critical review synthesis

Adam, Kate, Meghan and Henry · Scotchology

Adam and Kate value the lively malt and fruit; Meghan is unmoved, and Henry finds the development confused with an excessively hot, dry wood-led ending. The group’s disagreement is retained rather than flattened into unanimous praise.

Scope: July 23, 2015, exact Rare 10 / 43%; one group tasting with four distinct voices.

Chris Goodrum · Whisky According to Chris Whiskyman

Goodrum finds a malt-led, earthy profile with more energy on the palate than the aroma and useful persistence. Rye-like spice describes flavour, not a rye recipe.

Scope: November 2016 tasting of ten-year / 43%, separate from the page’s Ice 10.
