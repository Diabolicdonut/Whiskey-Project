Production and identity

The blend combines nine-year Knob Creek, nine-year Basil Hayden’s, eleven-year Booker’s and twelve-year Baker’s stock. The Basil Hayden’s portion uses a higher-rye recipe than the other components. Twelve years is not the youngest age.
