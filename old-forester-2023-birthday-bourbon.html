History and line identity

The 2023 Birthday Bourbon was the 23rd annual release honoring Old Forester founder George Garvin Brown’s September 2 birthday. It was the first such blend under Melissa Rift after Jackie Zykan’s departure, making the year an important identity field.

Critical synthesis

The matched reviewer finds a bright fruit-and-buttercream aroma followed by much darker, less sweet fruit, leather and short tannic wood. That loss of vibrancy drives the restrained saved score. Its unusual profile adds distinction, but distinction is not sufficient drinking value at the premium price.
