Exact identity: The first, June 2025 New York-only Borough Blend edition. The independent review is Batch 2; the 2026 second edition retains a separate dossier.

Background and history

Launched June 9, 2025, Borough Blend connected Widow Jane’s Brooklyn blending operation with seven New York bartenders. Five barrels represented the city’s five boroughs; the initial allocation was 2,400 bottles.

Its limited New York distribution and recurring annual selection explain the separate 2025 and 2026 identities. Neither is an ordinary retailer single-barrel pick.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

A marriage of sourced straight bourbons, each aged at least ten years in new American oak, selected as a five-barrel blend. The producer documents non-chill filtration and Rosendale-water proofing at 45.5% ABV; individual recipes and blend percentages are undisclosed.

Critical review synthesis

Shane English · Whisky Advocate

English describes a floral, red-fruit aroma, a nuttier palate with creamy sweetness, and a finish that holds chocolate sweetness against pepper. The assessed sample is the 2025 Batch 2.

Scope: Winter 2025; 45.5% ABV, Borough Blend Batch 2.
