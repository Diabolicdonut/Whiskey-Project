How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Old Forester 1920 Prohibition Style Bourbon is 115 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: dark fruit and burnt sugar enrich chocolate. The key question is whether that character persists as caramel cushions bold char.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 115 proof, add a few drops to test whether the documented warmth releases more flavor rather than simply flattening it. The relevant finishing behavior is pepper gives way to sustained classic flavors without much new development. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is caramel cushions bold char. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. The extra proof leaves room for ice dilution, while bitters can frame the spice.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from dark fruit and burnt sugar enrich chocolate toward pepper gives way to sustained classic flavors without much new development. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
