# American rye floor resolution — v5.1

Research checked October 5, 2026. Continuation of the accepted v5 collection.

**No defensible stronger 1.0 floor was established. Keep the American rye scale provisional.** This is a finite research result, not a claim that no worse or better-documented rye exists.

The prior twelve Hudson accounts, same-batch counterevidence and eight alternative-candidate decisions remain authoritative. This pass adds seven candidate screens rather than redoing that work. “Young,” “boring,” “expensive” and one low numerical grade do not independently demonstrate catastrophic drinking failure.

The historical Manhattan Rye search also surfaced aggregate summaries. Those summaries were used only to locate original accounts; they were not counted as independent critical votes. Australian rye, Canadian rye, cross-country blends and unaged rye were excluded.

Hudson Maple Cask 2014 Batch 1, Bottle 2338 remains the **sample-limited provisional 1.0 marker**. All 16 existing numerical rye Quality scores remain provisional and unchanged; the eight unscored rye dossiers remain N/A. The established rye midpoint and ceiling are retained. A failed search does not justify lowering an ordinary whiskey to fill the floor.

A future replacement needs several independent, substantive accounts of a sufficiently identifiable release or era. Identical bottle numbers and unanimity are unnecessary, but severe off-notes must converge and contrary evidence must be addressed.
