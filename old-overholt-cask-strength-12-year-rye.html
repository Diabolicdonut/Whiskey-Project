How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow plum and raspberry

Old Overholt Cask Strength 12 Year Rye is 117 proof. Let the fruit enrich the sweet-char middle before the dry oak enters. The attraction is mature rye with fruit still present.

Water: keep the red-fruit center

A few drops can test clarity through the warmth. Stop when raspberry fades and the ending becomes mostly spice and dry wood.

Manhattan: moderate vermouth

The fruit-rich middle already supplies sweetness and depth. Begin with less vermouth so mature rye and sweet char remain distinct.

Rocks or an Overholt flight

Try a large cube separately, or compare the ten-year release neat. Cooling may shorten fruit definition; retain a room-temperature reference.
