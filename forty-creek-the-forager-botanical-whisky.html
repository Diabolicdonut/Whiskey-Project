The short version

A deliberately botanical whisky whose successful use may be closer to a gin alternative than a conventional neat blend. Assessments differ on texture and sipping appeal; direct botanical infusion is stated explicitly.

Exact identity: The Forager Botanical Whisky by Forty Creek, Canadian whisky infused with five wild-sourced botanicals, 40% ABV / 80 proof, 750 mL; 2020 general release with earlier prototype history.

Background and history

Davin de Kergommeaux’s historical account describes a prototype unveiled at the 2019 Whisky Weekend. The producer timeline and May 27, 2020 primary announcement authenticate the general Canadian release; March 2020 Dykstra review predates that broader announcement.

The current producer Ontario listing is CAD 33.95 / 750 mL, marked out of stock when checked October 15, 2026. The original launch quote was CAD 34.95. Neither is treated as U.S. retail or a stock guarantee.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

Critical review synthesis

Chip Dykstra · The Rum Howler Blog

Dykstra finds the whisky remains recognizable while subtle botanicals add useful bitter and cooling accents. He likes both sipping and citrus/ginger mixing, acknowledging his own affinity for gin.

Scope: March 15, 2020; exact 40% botanical expression; packaging points not converted into Quality.

André · Québec Whisky

André finds the citrus-and-forest direction interesting but the texture ordinary. He is unconvinced as a whisky drinker while recognizing potential for cocktails.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Try a small pour first to judge whether the botanical character suits your sipping preference.

Rocks / water

A cube can soften the botanical/whisky contrast; keep dilution moderate.

Old Fashioned / Manhattan

A small citrus-forward short cocktail is better supported than treating it as an ordinary rye substitute in a Manhattan.
