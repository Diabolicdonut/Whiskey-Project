The short version

A distinct wood-and-wine Canadian blend with divided execution evidence. Some accounts find rich, controlled development; others identify youthful heat, excess sweetness or a rough wine-tannin ending.

Exact identity: Forty Creek Victory, 2019 thirteenth Annual Limited Edition / Lot 013, 43% ABV / 86 proof, NAS; high-vanilla wood treatment plus Starboard wine addition.

Background and history

Victory is the 2019 annual release commemorating the Engagement at the Forty during the War of 1812. The commemorative narrative is not a whisky vintage or final age statement.

An exact dated U.S. reference is Michigan code 21350, eighty-six proof / 750 mL, minimum shelf USD 74.99 for August 1 through October 30, 2021, excluding sales tax. It is historical consumer shelf context, not a current NC offer or wholesale price.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The attributed production account describes treating part of the blend with high-vanilla staves and adding Starboard wine. Dykstra reports a four-year base subset and ten-year corn component; Blair Phillips’s direct Ashburn interview describes an elevated rye proportion and no remainder of Unity’s old-corn centerpiece. Those component accounts are not silently reconciled into one invented recipe. No complete-blend numeric age is established.

Critical review synthesis

Davin de Kergommeaux · Whisky Advocate

De Kergommeaux finds creamy, fruit-rich development with sweetness keeping drying wood in check and good peppery activity. It is the strongest favorable interpretation in the inspected set.

Scope: Fall 2019, exact Victory / 43%; source separately reports U.S. allocation.

Chip Dykstra · The Rum Howler Blog

Dykstra finds attractive sweet-spice elements but youthful astringency and an unexpectedly short ending. Ice improves comfort without fully resolving the heat; he questions the premium.

Scope: November 30, 2019; exact annual Victory, sensory sections rather than packaging points.

talexander · Whisky Connosr

The taster finds substantial sweet corn and body, but enveloping wine and a rough tannic ending reduce coherence. Water provides some improvement.

Scope: December 24, 2019; exact 43% release. The reviewer’s unsupported Chilean-wine remark is not adopted as provenance.
