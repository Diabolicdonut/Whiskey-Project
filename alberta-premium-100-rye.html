The short version

A competent, affordable Canadian 100%-rye reference with sweetness and grassier grain character, limited by a youthful/edgy finish.

Exact identity: Standard Alberta Premium NAS 100%-rye whisky, 40% ABV; the U.S. market launch documented in 2022 is not a new age-stated recipe.

Background and history

The producer dates Alberta Premium’s Calgary brand to 1958. Christopher Null documents the standard 80-proof bottle entering the U.S. in 2022, illustrating a later export chapter rather than Prohibition participation.

The everyday NAS expression is distinct from Golden Rye 9 Year and the limited old/cask-strength bottlings. Historical reports of five-year stocks do not create a current label age statement.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Critical review synthesis

Christopher Null · Drinkhacker

Null finds grassy oak and citrus, a much sweeter almond/caramel palate, and a slight industrial edge returning at the finish. He considers it useful as a rail option.

Scope: January 11, 2023 U.S. bottle, 80 proof.

Lew Bryson · Whisky Advocate

Bryson finds sweet grain/citrus and soft oak, but an angular mouthful and edgy, boozy finish. Water improves that ending; he regards it as good rather than outstanding.

Scope: Summer 2012 Canadian-market bottle, 40% ABV; historical corroboration, not proof that a 2023 batch is identical.

Published judgments are attributed to their reviewers. This dossier does not claim first-person tasting, and a sample review does not establish every batch.
