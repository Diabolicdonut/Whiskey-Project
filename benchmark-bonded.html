The short version

More texture and balance than the basic line, at a small price increase.

Breaking Bourbon, the Whiskey Shelf and Bourbon Obsessed find a fuller sweet-spice profile than the weakest Benchmark bottles, while retaining some youthful grain and limited finish. The result clears the Maker’s/Bulleit midpoint and approaches the useful Very Old Barton 100 tier. Bonded status supplies identity and a minimum age, not automatic quality points.

At $24.95, the added texture and 100-proof utility justify very good value. Evan Williams Bonded and Old Grand-Dad Bonded are strong peers; Wild Turkey 101 offers another inexpensive step in character.

Scope: The current named expression, rather than old No. 8 at a substituted strength. Proof alone does not establish a Quality advantage. 1
