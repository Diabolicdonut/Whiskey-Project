Background and history

Cinnamon Rush followed the brand’s successful caramel extension and was announced for U.S. sale in September 2013, with 1 L, 750 mL and 50 mL sizes.

It does not appear in the current official range inspected at recovery. That supports historical treatment, but no exact formal discontinuation date is claimed.
