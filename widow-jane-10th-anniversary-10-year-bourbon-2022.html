The short version

A higher-strength anniversary bourbon with a credible spicy, mature review record; the premium makes more sense for the flavor than for commemorative packaging.

Exact identity: 2022 tenth-anniversary blend of straight bourbon whiskeys, aged ten years and bottled at 100 proof. This is distinct from the standard 91-proof 10 Year.

Background and history

Widow Jane was founded in Red Hook in 2012. Its 2022 anniversary release celebrates the company’s tenth year; the ten-year age of the sourced bourbon is a separate claim from the distillery’s birthday.

The anniversary bottling belongs to the blending side of the business. The label identifies Kentucky, Tennessee and Indiana distillation, rather than claiming all the whiskey spent its first decade in Brooklyn.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The identified 2022 label states a blend of straight bourbon whiskeys from Kentucky, Tennessee and Indiana. It carries a ten-year age statement and 50% ABV. Source distilleries and component mash bills are not established. Proofing in New York does not make this a wholly Brooklyn-distilled bourbon.

Critical review synthesis

Mark Gillespie · WhiskyCast

Gillespie finds caramel and dried-apple aromas leading into pronounced pepper, dark fruit and tobacco, followed by a lengthy spicy ending. His 93-point judgment is strongly positive.

Scope: October 2022 anniversary release, 50% ABV.

One named professional tasting plus a dated consumer counterpoint. The assessment is provisional and applies to the 2022 100-proof expression; it does not replace the accepted Batch 224 score.

Community sentiment

The August 2024 r/bourbon bottle-kill account is more restrained: it reports a buttery, moderately oily profile and a medium sweet finish. It supports drinkability but does not reproduce the enthusiasm of WhiskyCast. Anonymous accounts do not establish a broad consensus.

Neat

Rocks / water

Old Fashioned

Use a restrained syrup measure so the mature dark-fruit notes remain audible.
