The short version

A wheat-led mixed-grain Canadian blend with several substantive positive reviews. The supported appeal is integration and depth; its award is context rather than an automatic anchor score.

Exact identity: Crown Royal Noble Collection Winter Wheat, sixth annual edition, 2021, 45% ABV / 90 proof, NAS.

Background and history

The sixth Noble edition was released in 2021, before the 2022 Canadian Whisky Awards recognition. Later review dates do not turn the bottle into a new 2022 edition.

Drew Beard’s January 21, 2022 full review gives a contemporary U.S. USD 70 reference. The historical 750 mL U.S. package is separately size-matched in the indexed October 2021 Virginia catalogue at USD 69.99; its endpoint is no longer retrievable, so no current state listing or stock is claimed.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The producer confirms the mixed-grain recipe and charred American white-oak maturation at 45% ABV. It publishes no numeric age. The blend’s legal label is Canadian, and its grain recipe does not make it American wheat whiskey or wheated bourbon. Component distillation and any unreported additions are not inferred.

Critical review synthesis

Chip Dykstra · The Rum Howler Blog

Dykstra finds substantial flavor, structure and a long sweet/bitter-spiced ending. He explains that the whisky impressed him in blind judging and in a later separate tasting.

Scope: April 15, 2022 review page; exact 2021 Winter Wheat, 45% sample; the award is not the only sensory evidence.

Drew Beard · Drinkhacker

Beard finds a rich aroma and layered palate with well-managed sweetness and oak, though the finish is thinner than the rest of the pour.

Scope: January 21, 2022; exact NAS 90-proof sixth edition, USD 70 context.

Aengus McCloud · Dramface

McCloud finds a controlled, rounded mixed-grain profile with useful structure and complexity. He favors the chosen strength over an assumption that more proof would improve it.

Scope: May 12, 2022; exact Winter Wheat section of a four-expression tasting; ambiguous dollar context is not used as U.S. pricing.
