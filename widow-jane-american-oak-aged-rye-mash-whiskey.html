The short version

A sweet, floral legacy rye-mash whiskey with a useful cocktail case. Its used-oak process and label identity deserve their own treatment rather than a straight-rye assumption.

Exact identity: Legacy American Oak Aged whiskey distilled from a rye mash, 91 proof; Whisky Monster reviewed batch 31. Used bourbon barrels plus fresh oak staves distinguish it from modern Paradigm straight rye.

Background and history

The legacy 91-proof rye-mash range predates the 2023 Paradigm launch. Its production description uses former bourbon barrels and added oak staves, a different identity from the later blend of straight ryes.

Whisky Monster’s 2020 batch-31 review provides a matched sensory record; the later brand range does not silently supersede that bottle.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

Published specifications describe a rye-dominant mash with a small barley component, at least two years in oak, and 45.5% ABV. Whisky Monster reports used bourbon barrels with fresh-cut oak staves. The label category is preserved as whiskey distilled from a rye mash; it is not assumed to satisfy American straight-rye new-charred-oak requirements.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Start neat to inspect the citrus/floral side reported in batch 31.

Rocks / water

Try a large cube after a neat sample; keep dilution moderate because the review describes limited breadth.

Old Fashioned

Use less syrup than a dry rye might need: the matched review already reports substantial sweetness.

Manhattan

Start with a relatively light vermouth measure so its floral-citrus profile remains apparent.
