How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

New Riff 8 Year Kentucky Bourbon is 100 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: fruit, custard and caramel. The key question is whether that character persists as berries and mature oak in a full body.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 100 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is cherry, leather and spice linger. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is berries and mature oak in a full body. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 100 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of New Riff 8 Year Kentucky Bourbon.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from fruit, custard and caramel toward cherry, leather and spice linger. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
