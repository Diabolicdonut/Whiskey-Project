How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: give the pepper time

Old Forester 2019 Birthday Bourbon is 105 proof. Track banana bread and vanilla through the first peppery impression. The sweet middle and prolonged spice matter more than the opening alone.

Water: balance spice against fruit

Use a few drops only after a neat sample. Stop if banana and vanilla fade while the leather-and-wood ending stays dominant.

A controlled annual-release flight

Compare 2018 with 2019 at equal temperatures and pour sizes. The aim is to hear each vintage’s texture and finish, not average their scores.

Old Fashioned: moderate the bitters

A light syrup dose can support banana and vanilla. Too much aromatic bitterness can turn the long woody-spicy close into the only remaining impression.
