How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: evaluate oak integration

Knob Creek 15 Year 2020 Limited Edition is 100 proof. Give dark fruit and caramel time to support the mature wood. The important distinction is rich leather beside sweetness versus oak taking over the whole sip.

Water: stop before sweetness thins

Compare only a few drops initially. A drier ending can become more obvious if dilution removes caramel faster than it softens the wood.

A family flight

Use equal small pours of Knob Creek 12 and 15 at the same temperature. Both are 100 proof, making mature-oak balance a clearer comparison than proof intensity.

Rocks: keep a neat benchmark

A large cube can soften warmth, but chilling may make the already dry finish seem more austere. Judge the change before adding more ice or sugar.
