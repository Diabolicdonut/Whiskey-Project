Production

Specifications without invented precision

Buffalo Trace describes a rye-and-malted-barley recipe without corn. No exact rye percentage or universal maturation age is published.

Recipe: R / MB · no corn; percentages undisclosed. Age: NAS · bonded minimum 4 years. Strength: 100 proof / 50% ABV. 1

C corn · R rye · W wheat · MB malted barley. Undisclosed proportions are not estimated from neighboring labels. A component’s oldest age is not the age of a blend. Proof is not a quality multiplier.

Critical evidence

The flavor evidence, with disagreement intact

A synthesis of credited critical accounts. Written notes paired with a YouTube episode and that episode count as one voice. Unavailable transcripts are not inferred from titles.

Nose

Oak, rye spice and a fresh mint or fruit accent.

Palate

Oily texture, sweet fruit and pepper/spice, with a composed progression.

Finish

Warm baking spice and herbs; development is good rather than elite.

The Whiskey Jar / Robb Gibb

The Whiskey Reviewer / S.D. Peters

Favorable original 100-proof corn-free rye account; older evidence is qualified. 6

Evidence limits and disagreement

Breaking Bourbon, The Whiskey Jar and The Whiskey Reviewer support an expressive, balanced 100-proof rye. The older accounts receive less temporal weight. They support the standard corn-free bonded expression, not the newer Barrel Proof Rye recipe.
