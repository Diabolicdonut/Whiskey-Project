History and line identity

Four Roses organizes bourbon around two mash recipes and five yeast strains, yielding ten named recipes. The B mash is the higher-rye 60/35/5 recipe; the E mash is 75/20/5. Recipe letters describe a production identity, not a quality rank. Core blends, bottled single barrels and annual Limited Editions therefore remain separate assessments, with proof and barrel scope attached to the tasted sample.

Start with a small neat pour and allow it to rest. Add water gradually if alcohol masks the documented fruit or grain. A larger dilution can help compare balance, but it can also weaken an already light texture.
