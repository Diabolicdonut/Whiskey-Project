Production

Specifications without invented precision

Residual chocolate-malted rye distillate carried over into the next standard bourbon run. The base is the ordinary bourbon recipe, with a trace from the preceding run; this is not the Chocolate Malted Rye mash bill. The 2025 release is bottled at 139.4 proof.

Recipe: C72 R18 MB10 base · trace chocolate-malted rye carryover; exact trace undisclosed. Age: NAS. Strength: 139.4 proof / 69.7% ABV. 1
