How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Old Forester Classic 86 Proof Bourbon is 86 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: vanilla and faint caramel accompany oak. The key question is whether that character persists as brown sugar meets light pepper.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 86 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is wood and gentle spice fade relatively quickly. Stop early if the already gentle body becomes less distinct.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is brown sugar meets light pepper. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 86 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Old Forester Classic 86 Proof Bourbon.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from vanilla and faint caramel accompany oak toward wood and gentle spice fade relatively quickly. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
