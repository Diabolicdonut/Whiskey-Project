The short version

A more expressive, fruitier Canadian Mist made for the U.S. market, with increased rye and sherry content. Its short finish and youthful character keep it primarily a versatile budget blend.

Exact identity: Canadian Mist Black Diamond Premium Select, original 2010 U.S. launch expression, 43% ABV / 86 proof.

Background and history

The producer announcement schedules initial release for late August 2010 in six U.S. markets at USD 14.99 / 750 mL, with further markets planned for 2011. Its original announcement date governs; a migrated page footer dated April 2010 is inconsistent with that text.

This is retained as a historical line extension. The exact cessation date is unverified. Brown-Forman’s July 31, 2020 sale transferred the Canadian Mist and Collingwood brands and production assets; that later ownership does not make the 2010 bottle a Sazerac production.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The August 5, 2010 producer announcement specifies increased rye and sherry content and 86 proof. The independent production record describes separately matured corn and mixed-grain streams blended after three years. Sherry is a blending contribution here; an ex-sherry-cask finish is not established. Three years is reported production age, not a verified age statement on the pictured front label.

Critical review synthesis

Davin de Kergommeaux · Canadian Whisky

De Kergommeaux finds fuller fruit, more body and brighter spice than original Canadian Mist, with useful citrus balance. He still sees youthful grain character, moderate complexity and a relatively short finish.

Scope: Exact 43% Black Diamond review; historical launch-era specification and USD 14.99 suggested price.

Chuck Cowdery · The Chuck Cowdery Blog

Cowdery finds the extra rye and sherry make a more substantial blend without adding wood age. He particularly values the added character in a Manhattan.

Scope: August 10, 2010; three-year production, 43% ABV, USD 14.99 / 750 mL.
