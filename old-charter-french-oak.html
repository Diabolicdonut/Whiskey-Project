Evidence limits and disagreement

Breaking Bourbon and The Whiskey Wash describe a layered, approachable 2021 bottle; Whiskey Consensus is more enthusiastic about its texture and long ending. All three match 92 proof and the 2021 release. Their differing age descriptions are kept qualified. The earlier nine-year release is not pooled.

Quality is limited to the reviewed 2021 92-proof edition. NAS labeling is retained; the reported twelve-year age is source context, not a universal French Oak age.
