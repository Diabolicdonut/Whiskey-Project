The short version

A whisky-and-rum blend whose integration attracts differing reactions. The twelve-year statement belongs to the rum component, and the treatment is a blending addition rather than a rum-cask finish.

Exact identity: Forty Creek Foxheart, recurring Canadian whisky blend with twelve-year Caribbean rum added, introduced July 2021, 40% ABV / 80 proof; no whole-blend age statement.

Background and history

The producer announced Foxheart in Ontario on July 6, 2021, with BC and further Canadian rollout planned. The name references Bill Ashburn’s champion wire-haired terrier breeding, not a separate production site.

The historical Canadian launch reference and the current Ontario producer page both give CAD 44.95, with the current page explicitly showing 750 mL. No exact dated U.S. regular price was authenticated.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The July 6, 2021 producer release authenticates twelve-year Caribbean rum blended into Canadian whisky. The current product page verifies 40% and 750 mL. De Kergommeaux reports less than 9.09% rum, while Drams states the full percentage; the producer does not publish an exact quantity, so none is fixed here. No whisky-base grain percentages, base age, rum distillery or separate rum-cask stage is established.

Critical review synthesis

Davin de Kergommeaux · Canadian Whisky

De Kergommeaux finds the rum broadens the familiar whisky profile without taking over, supporting a useful sweet/spicy balance and a sustained ending.

Scope: July 18, 2021; exact Foxheart, full whisky-only sensory account; wider legal comparisons are not adopted.

Drams of Whisky reviewer · Drams of Whisky

This taster finds a more obvious contest between rum and whisky, with a more successful palate than aroma or finish. It is interesting but not a bottle they would seek again.
