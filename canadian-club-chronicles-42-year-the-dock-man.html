The exact label confirms 42 years and 45% ABV. Critical review synthesis

Chip Dykstra · The Rum Howler Blog

Dykstra finds expanding grain, fruit and nutty sweetness, elegant balance and persistent spice. His earlier blind notes were already strongly positive, so the favorable assessment did not depend solely on seeing the age.

Scope: February 22, 2020, 42-year / 45% ABV; 94-point overall review includes presentation.

Coldorak · More Drams, Less Drama

The comparison finds the 42-year fuller and creamier than the 40-year, with more wood, pepper and caramel. It regards the added components as effective and judges the final whisky favorably.

Scope: July 25, 2023, separately reviewed exact 42-year sample; 87 points.

Courtney Kristjana · The Whiskey Wash

Kristjana finds attractive orchard fruit and citrus but very light texture and a missing or wood-obscured element. She enjoys the pour while reporting less body and completeness than the other records.

Scope: March 11, 2020, exact 90-proof / 750 mL record; review-sample source disclosed ambiguously by the publisher.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Begin neat with a small pour and allow time in the glass to evaluate wood, grain and fruit integration.

Rocks / water

Try a few drops of water before a cube; protect the documented texture and subtle mature flavors.

Old Fashioned

A small Manhattan or restrained Old Fashioned is possible, but taste neat before deciding to sweeten an expensive release.

Highball

Use a small measure of soda if desired; the old-stock premium does not automatically make it a better mixing whisky.

Serving suggestions are editorial starting points, grounded in documented strength and style; adjust to your taste.
