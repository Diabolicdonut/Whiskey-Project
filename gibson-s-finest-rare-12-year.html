Critical review synthesis

Robbie Raskin · Heatseeker Whisky

Raskin values the creamy texture and engaging acidity but finds the spice comparatively brash, the ending short and the refinement less complete than the age might suggest.

Scope: July 7, 2020, exact Rare 12 / 40%; CAD 38 for 750 mL.

Victor · Whisky Connosr

Victor finds an appealing aromatic opening and lively delivery, then simpler development and a bitter-to-sour ending. He prefers it more than expected, while retaining the finish limitation.

Scope: September 16, 2016, exact twelve-year sample; a separate NAS duty-free bottle is discussed, not merged.
