The short version

A gentle, fruity wheated bourbon; the short ending limits its reach.

Breaking Bourbon’s 2020 sample has soft fruit and creamy sweetness but limited persistence and a little bitter dryness. The Whiskey Shelf’s later bottle is fruitier and more expressive, comparing favorably with some Van Winkle samples. Together they support good bourbon above Maker’s ordinary midpoint, with less consistent depth than the stronger Weller range.

The $40 dated release reference supports good value for the reviewed style. It is not a verified current shelf offer; Weller Special Reserve and Maker’s 46 provide useful lower-cost checks before paying a scarcity premium.
