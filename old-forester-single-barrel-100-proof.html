How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Old Forester Single Barrel 100 Proof is 100 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: butterscotch and cherry enrich vanilla. The key question is whether that character persists as nuts and measured wood support sweetness.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 100 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is pepper and clove finish with a mildly dank accent. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is nuts and measured wood support sweetness. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 100 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Old Forester Single Barrel 100 Proof.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from butterscotch and cherry enrich vanilla toward pepper and clove finish with a mildly dank accent. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
