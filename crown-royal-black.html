Critical review synthesis

Christopher Null · Drinkhacker

Null finds intense wood, sweet fruit and spice, with a persistent ash-like ending. He sees an unusual workable balance but expects the oak to overwhelm some drinkers.

Scope: October 4, 2010; exact Black 90-proof sample, B+.

Richard Thomas · The Whiskey Reviewer

Thomas finds a surprisingly light texture and straightforward sweet/spicy flavors, despite a long warm finish. He is much less persuaded by the robust billing and its price.

Scope: July 2014; exact Black 45% ABV sample, C+.
