How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Old Elk Blended Straight Bourbon is 88 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: faint cinnamon and cocoa. The key question is whether that character persists as velvety brown sugar and maple.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 88 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is brief spice gives way to delicate sweetness. Stop early if the already gentle body becomes less distinct.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is velvety brown sugar and maple. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 88 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Old Elk Blended Straight Bourbon.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from faint cinnamon and cocoa toward brief spice gives way to delicate sweetness. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
