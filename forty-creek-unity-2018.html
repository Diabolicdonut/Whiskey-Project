Producer, grains, aging and finishing

Dykstra’s contemporary account describes a four-year-stock subset receiving further aging with high-mocha staves, then blending with ten-year corn whisky and a small amount of fifteen-year Portuguese-style Starboard wine. These are attributed component accounts, not a numeric whole-blend age. High-mocha describes a wood treatment, not a verified tree species, coffee infusion or a separately proven port-cask finish.
