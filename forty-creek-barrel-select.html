Background and history

The producer dates Barrel Select to 2000 and describes the current bottle as a new look for the same whisky. Niagara branding is not treated as a new formulation without supporting evidence.

Whiskeyfellow’s July 10, 2026 review explicitly lists USD 21.99 per 750 mL and describes a roughly USD 22 purchase. The NC catalogue’s generic Forty Creek 1.75 L entry is not specific enough to replace this exact-expression U.S. reference.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The producer states that grain whiskies are aged separately using seasoned ex-bourbon barrels and new American white oak. This is a Canadian blending approach, not a single American-style mash bill. The current producer page does not authenticate the six-to-ten-year component ages, grain-specific char levels or sherry-finishing recipe reported in some secondary descriptions; those are not asserted for the current bottle. The exact label is NAS.

Critical review synthesis

Jeffrey Schwartz · Whiskeyfellow

Schwartz finds a well-developed contrast between sweetness, fruit and spice with a medium finish. He considers its character persuasive at the paid price.

Scope: July 10, 2026; exact Barrel Select, 40%, purchased bottle, explicit 750 mL USD 21.99 reference; speculative current production details excluded.

Beverage Testing Institute panel · Tastings

The panel finds approachable rounded sweetness with useful spice and a medium ending. A candy-like impression is retained rather than omitted from the favorable account.

Scope: July 12, 2024 tasting, published 2024; exact Barrel Select at 40%; score not mechanically converted.

Nathan · The Scotch Noob

Nathan’s older blind sample is much sweeter and younger-seeming, with limited development despite a convincing dessert profile and an absence of bitterness.

Scope: April 11, 2016; blind sample later identified as Barrel Select, 40%; older era is not silently treated as the same batch.
