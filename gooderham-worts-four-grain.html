The short version

A lively but divisive four-grain blend. Some reviewers enjoy its sustained spice, while others find excessive sweetness, solvent-like notes and a thin or abrupt ending.

Exact identity: Gooderham & Worts Four Grain, modern 2015-revival NAS Canadian blend, 44.4% ABV / 88.8 proof, 750 mL; current exact producer photograph reads 44.4%.

Background and history

Four Grain returned in 2015 following earlier revivals of the historic name. The older Whisky Guild / Natural Small Batch bottling is not silently treated as the same 44.4% formulation.

The current U.S. Caskers offer shows USD 23.99 / 750 mL at 88.8 proof, observed October 15, 2026. An earlier exact U.S. reviewer purchase was USD 35 in June 2020; historical and current price contexts remain separate.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The modern blend combines separately made grain whiskies. Direct Livermore interview documentation confirms corn, rye, wheat and barley. Specialist accounts describe different distillation paths and new/ex-bourbon oak, but an exact whole-blend cask schedule and numeric age remain undisclosed. The current shop’s rounded 44% prose is not substituted for the clearly photographed 44.4% label. A blend number is not a whisky age or proof of a single barrel.

Critical review synthesis

Selfbuilt · Selfbuilt’s Whisky Analysis

Selfbuilt likes the persistent grain spice and comparatively satisfying ending, but notes a solvent-like initial aroma that improves with rest and limited visibility of some grains beneath the rye character.

Scope: January 13, 2016, exact Four Grain / 44.4%; direct notes, not the worldwide meta-score.

The Scotch Noob · The Scotch Noob

The reviewer finds the sweetness poorly integrated with simple grain/spice development and an abruptly fading, solvent-like ending. Water offers little improvement in this sample.

Scope: June 21, 2020, exact blend A.A1129 / 44.4%, personally purchased in California.
