Critical evidence

Critical synthesis

A synthesis of credited critical accounts. Written notes paired with a YouTube episode and that episode count as one voice. Unavailable transcripts are not inferred from titles.

Nose

Oak, caramel or butterscotch, dried fruit and vanilla, with ethanol varying by sample.

Palate

Rich wheated sweetness meets toasted wood, fruit and spice; some accounts find bitter or gritty wood stronger than expected.

Finish

Dry oak and pepper or cocoa; integration is the main disagreement.

Evidence limits and disagreement

Breaking Bourbon praises the sweet-oak balance; Brian Dawson finds bitterness, rough texture and dominant wood. Michael Veach likes the caramel character but finds less complexity than varied Private Selections. His 107.8-proof bottle is comparative edition context, not an authenticated match to the 108.2-proof row.
