Background

The name, the era and the limits of the story

Justin and Cara King founded King’s Family Distillery in 2018 in the Sevierville area of Tennessee. The business combines sourcing, blending, selection and finishing. A Tennessee bottling or finishing address does not by itself make the liquid Tennessee Whiskey, nor establish the distiller of every sourced batch. 2

Identify, then taste

Editorial method: check the actual proof, age, recipe and barrel label against this page, then begin with a small neat sample. An unresolved label is not a basis for an expensive full-bottle ranking.

Controlled water comparison
