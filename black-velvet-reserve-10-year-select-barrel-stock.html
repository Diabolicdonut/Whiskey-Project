Critical review synthesis

Beverage Testing Institute panel · Tastings

The July panel describes dry woody aromas, modest body, a tingling short ending and suitability for sweet cocktails. It recommends the sample at Bronze level rather than treating age as excellence.

Scope: July 12, 2024 exact 10 Year Select Barrel Stock, 40% ABV.

TAG judging panel · TAG Global Spirits Awards

The TAG panel finds richer fruit-and-chocolate development, balanced medium body and a sustained ending. Its detailed notes and maturity praise contrast substantially with the other panel.

Serving recommendations

Rocks / water

Try one cube to moderate sweetness and spice; avoid masking an overly dry finish.

The less enthusiastic panel still favors sweet cocktails; reduce syrup until balance is checked.
