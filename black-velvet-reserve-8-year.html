Critical review synthesis

Davin de Kergommeaux · Canadian Whisky

The specialist finds creamy caramel, fruit and vigorous pepper, with wood mostly held in balance. Complexity remains modest and the finish is moderate to brief despite lingering spice.

Scope: October 2017 page, exact eight-year / 40% ABV.

Josh · Sipology

Josh finds more developed sweet fruit, nuts and oak than in the core blend and praises the balance. His sample’s ending persists, though he wishes for a stronger presentation.

Scope: August 2014 eight-year Reserve head-to-head.

Johannes Lindblom · WhiskyRant

Lindblom finds a sharp aroma, heat and a rapidly thinning finish. He regards the sample as merely decent among cheap blends and less pleasant than ordinary Black Velvet.

Scope: 2015-era exact eight-year / 40% ABV sample.
