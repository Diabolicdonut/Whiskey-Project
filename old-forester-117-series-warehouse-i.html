Critical synthesis

The nose promises familiar banana, vanilla and caramel, but the review finds less weight in the middle and a relatively brief close. The warehouse story explains selection intent; it does not erase that imbalance. The preserved assessment favors pleasant familiarity over exceptional concentration.
