The short version

A balanced classic single barrel, carrying a meaningful brand premium.

Breaking Bourbon, the Whiskey Shelf and Bourbon Obsessed converge on an approachable fruit, caramel and spice profile, while finding limits to depth and persistence. This is clearly above the ordinary bourbon midpoint and near Maker’s 46, rather than near the category ceiling. Individual barrel variation remains material.

At $82.95 NC, the reviewed quality is harder to justify against Maker’s 46 at $39.95, Eagle Rare 10 at $44.95 and Weller Full Proof at $64.95. The bottle presentation adds no drinking-quality points.

Production

Specifications without invented precision

Selected Buffalo Trace barrels mature in metal-clad Warehouse H. The original brand debuted in 1984 under Elmer T. Lee; brand history does not guarantee every barrel has equal depth.
