How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: test the body

Traveller Whiskey Blend No. 40 (90 proof): The light fruit and sweet grain are easy to identify; assess whether creamy dessert notes have enough weight to carry the short sweet-and-spicy close.

Water: little room to spare

Traveller Whiskey Blend No. 40: Start undiluted at 90 proof. A sample already perceived as light can lose definition with water; compare a few drops only after establishing its texture.

A short highball

Traveller Whiskey Blend No. 40: Use modest soda and ice so the light fruit remains detectable. A long pour of mixer is likely to make the drink primarily soda.

Old Fashioned: keep sugar modest

Traveller Whiskey Blend No. 40: Begin with bitters and a small amount of syrup. The soft grain sweetness needs room, while this blend lacks the body to absorb heavy sugar and dilution.
