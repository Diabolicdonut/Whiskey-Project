How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Knob Creek Small Batch 12 Year Bourbon is 100 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: caramel and aged oak frame dark fruit. The key question is whether that character persists as sweetness and spice retain body.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 100 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is seasoned wood continues through a sustained warm finish. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is sweetness and spice retain body. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 100 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Knob Creek Small Batch 12 Year Bourbon.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from caramel and aged oak frame dark fruit toward seasoned wood continues through a sustained warm finish. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
