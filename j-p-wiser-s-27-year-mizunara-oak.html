Stephen Beaumont · Beaumont Drinks

Serving recommendations

Neat

Begin with a small rested pour; assess sweetness, texture and the drying finish before choosing a serving.

Water / rocks

Compare neat with water added a few drops at a time: the matched review specifically finds dilution helpful. Preserve enough strength to assess texture.

Old Fashioned

Use restrained syrup and bitters. A recurring Canadian blend is the cost comparison before mixing a limited bottle.

Manhattan / highball

Try a small serve with restrained vermouth or soda; a softer whisky can lose definition under assertive mixers.
