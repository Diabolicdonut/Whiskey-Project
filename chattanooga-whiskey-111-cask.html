How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Chattanooga Whiskey 111 Cask is 111 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: raisin, toffee and toasted malt. The key question is whether that character persists as creamy honeyed caramel.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 111 proof, add a few drops to test whether the documented warmth releases more flavor rather than simply flattening it. The relevant finishing behavior is bakery spice follows a textured, moderately long sip. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is creamy honeyed caramel. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. The extra proof leaves room for ice dilution, while bitters can frame the spice.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from raisin, toffee and toasted malt toward bakery spice follows a textured, moderately long sip. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
