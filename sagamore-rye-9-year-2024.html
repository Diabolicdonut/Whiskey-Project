Background and history

Sagamore Spirit revived the Maryland-style rye tradition, using blends of two rye mash bill profiles and gradually transitioning from sourced to Maryland-distilled stock.

2024 Reserve Series 9-Year Straight Rye, Batch 1A, 112.6 proof (56.3% ABV), matured at least nine years in new charred American oak; distinct from 2025 10-year release.

Sagamore's official Reserve Series identifies a minimum of nine years in new charred American white oak, bottled at 112.6 proof. Exact matched Batch 1A is documented separately by critics; do not assume this age indicates its liquid was wholly Maryland-distilled.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Preferred way to evaluate the long pepper, citrus and mint finish at 112.6 proof.

Rocks

Add a few drops rather than a large cube first; high proof can support some dilution.

Old Fashioned

Use a notably light sugar dose to avoid wasting layered aged rye.

Manhattan

A spirit-forward Manhattan works; choose vermouth that does not overwhelm citrus and oak.

Highball

A tall rye highball is plausible, but this relatively mature/limited bottle is most informative neat before mixing.
