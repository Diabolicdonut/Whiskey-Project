The short version

The better barrels have real depth; other samples are more ordinary.

Breaking Bourbon finds richer fruit and sweetness; the Whiskey Shelf’s two separate barrels show meaningful variation from excellent to more ordinary drinking. Very good bourbon near Weller Full Proof is a bounded assessment of those samples, rather than a promise attached to the single-barrel label.
