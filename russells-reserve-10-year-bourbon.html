How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Russell’s Reserve 10 Year Bourbon is 90 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: vanilla and caramel introduce leather and toast. The key question is whether that character persists as tobacco and oak anchor sweetness.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 90 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is rye spice builds before tapering. Stop early if the already gentle body becomes less distinct.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is tobacco and oak anchor sweetness. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 90 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Russell’s Reserve 10 Year Bourbon.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from vanilla and caramel introduce leather and toast toward rye spice builds before tapering. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
