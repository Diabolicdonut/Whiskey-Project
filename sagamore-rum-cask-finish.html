The short version

An expressive 98-proof rum-finished rye combining molasses, dark fruit and toasted coconut with fresh herbal rye spice. Independent critics find it fun and layered, although the rum lifts off the nose faster than the palate.

Exact identity: Nationwide March 2022 Rum Cask Finish Reserve Series, 98 proof, 5- and 6-year straight ryes divided into Jamaican and South American rum casks for 10 months, then reblended. Not the 2019 one-cask distillery-exclusive rum edition.

Background and history

Sagamore had made a limited 2019 Jamaican rum cask whiskey at its Baltimore distillery; its reception prompted the more broadly distributed 2022 two-rum-region edition.

The March 2022 launch was a Reserve Series product, not necessarily found in present 2026 state-control retail stock.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The 2022 edition was built from separately matured 5- and 6-year-old straight rye whiskey, then transferred to Jamaican and South American rum barrels for a further ten months. It was blended back at 98 proof and released nationally, unlike the 2019 distillery-only Jamaican high-ester rum cask experiment.

Critical review synthesis

Christopher Null · Drinkhacker

Reviews precisely identified October 2022 Batch 1A: finds the nose somewhat erratic and grassy, with pepper and wood overtaking early rum esters; praises a more deftly integrated palate of buttery sweetness, cocoa, vanilla, cherry, toast and late orange peel. The sample improved with time.

Scope: 2022 98-proof Reserve Series, identified reviewer bottle Batch 1A. Not the 2019 release.

Danielle · Coffee Black Whiskey Neat

Describes rich cinnamon loaf, molasses and coconut on the nose, tropical punch, coconut and starfruit on the palate, then banana and cayenne. Enthusiastic but does not provide unambiguous full publication year; identity supported by 98 proof and the two-region 10-month finish.

Scope: Two-region rum-finishing version at 98 proof; archive does not show exact calendar year of sample; secondary supporting source only.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Highly recommended neat with a rested second sip; note whether the fruity rum nose gives way to grassy rye while the palate remains complex.

Rocks

A large cube calms herbal heat but may soften the intricate brown-sugar and coconut flavors.

Old Fashioned

Minimal syrup, orange peel and bitters emphasize caramel and rye without burying the natural rum sweetness.

Manhattan

A spirit-heavy Manhattan with dry-ish vermouth can work, but rich sweet vermouth may compound rum molasses notes.

Highball

Possible for mixing, but the limited bottle's special finishing deserves an initial neat comparison before dilution.

Serving suggestions are editorial starting points, grounded in documented strength and style; adjust to your taste.
