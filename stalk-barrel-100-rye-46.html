The short version

A distinctive all-rye craft whisky with real herbal and pepper complexity, but youth shows through as heat, drying oak and an awkward citrus edge.

Exact identity: Stalk & Barrel 100% Rye at 46% ABV / 92 proof, the individually bottled single-cask strength-reduced release; cask-strength Stalk & Barrel Rye is a different expression.

Background and history

Still Waters was founded in 2009 and built its Stalk & Barrel craft range around in-house malt and rye distillates rather than treating Canadian rye as only a flavoring component.

The 46% rye sits beside separately bottled cask-strength rye. Reviews of the latter are not imported into this dossier.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

Independent reviews quoting the producer identify 100% locally grown Ontario rye, hand-distilled in small batches on a copper pot still and matured in ex-bourbon casks for a minimum of three years. The 46% release was bottled as individual single casks; cask-to-cask variation therefore matters.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Start neat, then add a few drops of water to separate rye herbs from alcohol heat.

Rocks

A small cube can tame the hot, drying side, but too much dilution will flatten the grain.

Highball

Its pepper, herbs and citrus should carry well in a dry highball.

Old Fashioned

Use restrained sugar and aromatic bitters; the rye already supplies plenty of spice.

Manhattan

Use a moderately rich vermouth and keep the whisky-forward ratio.

Serving suggestions are editorial starting points, grounded in documented strength and style; adjust to your taste.
