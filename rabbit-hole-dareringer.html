How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Rabbit Hole Dareringer is 93 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: rich wine fruit and caramel. The key question is whether that character persists as sherry sweetness in a good body.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 93 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is raisin, spice and dry oak resolve gradually. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is sherry sweetness in a good body. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 93 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Rabbit Hole Dareringer.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from rich wine fruit and caramel toward raisin, spice and dry oak resolve gradually. For a Sour, keep syrup modest so the cask-derived sweetness is not doubled.
