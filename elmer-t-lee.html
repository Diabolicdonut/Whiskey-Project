The short version

Pleasant classic fruit and balance, priced well beyond its reviewed depth.

Breaking Bourbon’s 2026 bottle and its earlier review provide continuity, while the Whiskey Shelf supplies an independent light-fruit assessment. The profile is good and approachable above the ordinary midpoint, without the richness or persistence of Weller Full Proof. Two articles from Breaking Bourbon count as one publisher.

At $89.95 NC, the standard single-barrel premium makes this poor value beside Buffalo Trace, Eagle Rare 10 and Maker’s 46. Rarity and the commemorated distiller do not raise Quality.

Scope: Standard 90-proof single barrel. The 2026 review is relevant; old commemorative 93- or 100-proof editions are excluded. 1
