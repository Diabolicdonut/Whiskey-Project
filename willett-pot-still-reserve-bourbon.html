How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Willett Pot Still Reserve Bourbon is 94 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: cherry and citrus with vanilla. The key question is whether that character persists as honeyed caramel in a light body.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 94 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is approachable spice and oak. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is honeyed caramel in a light body. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 94 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Willett Pot Still Reserve Bourbon.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from cherry and citrus with vanilla toward approachable spice and oak. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
