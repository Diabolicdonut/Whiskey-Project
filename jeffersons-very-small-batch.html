How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Jefferson's Very Small Batch is 82.3 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: corn and vanilla frame faint plum. The key question is whether that character persists as light sweet wood forms a simple middle.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 82.3 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is only brief vanilla and oak remain late. Stop early if the already gentle body becomes less distinct.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is light sweet wood forms a simple middle. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 82.3 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Jefferson's Very Small Batch.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from corn and vanilla frame faint plum toward only brief vanilla and oak remain late. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
