How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Russell’s Reserve Single Barrel Bourbon is 110 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: caramel and toast frame substantial oak. The key question is whether that character persists as leather and tobacco join sweetness and rye spice.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 110 proof, add a few drops to test whether the documented warmth releases more flavor rather than simply flattening it. The relevant finishing behavior is char and warm spice persist. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is leather and tobacco join sweetness and rye spice. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. The extra proof leaves room for ice dilution, while bitters can frame the spice.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from caramel and toast frame substantial oak toward char and warm spice persist. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
