Critical review synthesis

Christopher Null · Drinkhacker

Null finds better refinement than the prior ultra-aged samples, with developing citrus, floral and nut-like notes. He reports a sharper palate than the aroma suggests but a clean, substantial ending, with earlier creosote-like character largely absent.
