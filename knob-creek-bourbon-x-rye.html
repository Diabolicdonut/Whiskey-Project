History and line identity

Bourbon x Rye brings nine-year Knob Creek bourbon and seven-year Knob Creek rye into a 113-proof blend. The July 2024 source reports a 30% bourbon and 70% rye composition; those blend fractions are not a single mash bill or an overall age statement. This is a deliberate way to balance bourbon sweetness with a rye-led middle and finish, and should be compared with both its component lines and other bourbon–rye blends.
