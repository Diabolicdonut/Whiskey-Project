Exact identity: Modern 93-proof Apple Wood blended straight rye; scoring evidence is BTI batch 28 tasted July 2024. This is not the older 91-proof rye-mash whiskey.

Background and history

Widow Jane’s applewood treatment spans two differently documented identities. The modern 93-proof blend uses the Paradigm rye base; the legacy 91-proof label described whiskey distilled from a rye mash.

That transition warrants separate dossiers. It does not warrant one entry for every ordinary recurring batch.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

Distiller describes a base of Widow Jane’s own straight rye and sourced Kentucky/Indiana straight ryes, finished with new applewood staves. The matched BTI review establishes 46.5% ABV. Mash percentages and blend proportions remain unknown.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Try neat to separate the applewood influence from the underlying rye spice.

Rocks / water

Add a few drops first; a large cube may make the fruit sweeter while reducing its medium body.

Old Fashioned

Keep syrup restrained and use a citrus peel rather than adding an apple liqueur that obscures the stave effect.

Manhattan

Start two-to-one with sweet vermouth; test a small pour before letting richer vermouth dominate the apple notes.

Serving suggestions are editorial starting points, grounded in documented strength and style; adjust to your taste.
