Serving recommendations

Neat

Neat is the best way to learn if extended cask contact improves the old release; the 2025 version is not independently appraised.

Rocks

One cube can soften 103-proof spice, but the precise 2025 textural result is unverified.

Old Fashioned

Very cautious sugar addition avoids doubling the producer-described cherry and bitters cues.

Manhattan

Start with half the usual vermouth and bitters, then adjust; a finished-for-Manhattan rye can be overwhelmed by a fully flavored cocktail.

Highball

A highball is viable, but less efficient than a less expensive, unfancied rye if the finish is what warrants the premium.

Serving suggestions are editorial starting points, grounded in documented strength and style; adjust to your taste.
