Specifications without invented precision

Critical evidence

The flavor evidence, with disagreement intact

A synthesis of credited critical accounts. Written notes paired with a YouTube episode and that episode count as one voice. Unavailable transcripts are not inferred from titles.

Nose

Cocoa, vanilla, grain and light oak.

Palate

Roasted grain and dark chocolate dominate; novelty is clearer than breadth.

Finish

Dry cocoa and wood, with disagreement about whether that dryness invites another sip.

Breaking Bourbon / Jordan Moskal

Gentle entry and an enjoyable long, dry chocolate finish. 2

Adventures in Whiskey / Bobby

Finds the cocoa-led palate too dominant to recommend; supplied sample disclosed. 4

Bourbon Obsessed / Gus

Roasted malt, fruit and wood make a distinctive experiment; enthusiasm is qualified. 5

Evidence limits and disagreement

Breaking Bourbon enjoys the long chocolate-led finish. Adventures in Whiskey cannot recommend the chocolate-dominant balance. Bourbon Obsessed describes an interesting roasted-grain experiment with more qualified repeat-pour appeal. The title refers to the rye ingredient in a bourbon, not a rye-category whiskey.
