Critical review synthesis

Selfbuilt · Selfbuilt’s Whisky Analysis

Selfbuilt finds familiar corn sweetness, dry rye and earthy notes with little burn, but restrained complexity and lingering artificial-sweetness/tannic impressions. He sees a recognizable, useful traditional blend rather than a standout.

The Gentleman’s Flavor panel · The Gentleman’s Flavor

The panel finds an agreeable fruit-and-maple nose but a thin palate and an almost immediate falloff on the finish. This reinforces the texture and persistence limitations.
