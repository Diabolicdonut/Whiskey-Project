The short version

A mature, grain-led Canadian blend with unusually well-documented red-oak treatment. Strong independent praise is tempered by disagreements over sharpness, body and finish length.

Exact identity: Gooderham & Worts 49 Wellington, 2019 Northern Border Collection Rare Release, nineteen-year Canadian blend at 49% ABV / 98 proof.

Background and history

The 2019 expression honors Toronto’s historic address at 49 Wellington. Its name and 49% strength reflect the address, not a forty-nine-year age. The modern blend comes from Windsor, not a reopened Toronto distillery.

Eric Burke records an exact historical purchase at Total Wine, Burnsville, Minnesota: USD 85.99 / 750 mL, in his May 26, 2022 review. This is historical consumer context rather than a current NC quote.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

In a direct 2019 interview, Don Livermore identifies red-winter-wheat whisky and mature whisky placed in oak vessels fitted with red-oak inserts for a further six years. This is an additional stage for treated stock within the nineteen-year release, not nineteen plus six years for the complete blend. Wheat is the source grain of a whisky component, not the wood from which barrels are made. Exact blend proportions and whether every component saw the inserts remain unverified.

Critical review synthesis

Mark Gillespie · WhiskyCast

Gillespie finds a warmly integrated, gently spicy whisky with sweetness and soft wood supporting a persistent finish. It is the most uniformly favorable reading in the inspected set.

Scope: January 2020, exact 2019 release / 49% / nineteen years.

Mark Bylok · Whisky Buzz

Bylok especially values the sustained finish and layered development, while finding the whisky somewhat sharp, sweet and lighter in body than the flavor range suggests.

Scope: October 10, 2019; exact release. Corby supplied samples and accommodations, as disclosed by the author.

Eric Burke · BOURBON GUY

Burke likes its silky delivery and growing spice but finds a comparatively modest finish length. His enthusiasm remains a discretionary splurge rather than proof of universal bargain status.

Scope: May 26, 2022; purchased exact 49% / nineteen-year / 750 mL bottle.
