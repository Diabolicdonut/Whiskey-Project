The short version

An eight-plus-year Classic Yukon malt with fruit, caramel and persistent spice in positive reviews, but sharper grain and tartness in independent counterpoints.

Exact identity: Two Brewers Classic Release No. 35, 46% ABV, issued in 2022; Canadian and export packaging sizes vary.

Background and history

Two Brewers' numbered Classic series includes release-by-release differences in maturity and assembly.

Release 35 is distinguished from the heavily PX-influenced Release 33 and Innovative sour-fermented Release 22.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

A detailed independent review describes fourteen malted-barley mashes aged eight years or longer, combined from ex-bourbon and virgin-oak maturation. Exact reviewed Release 35 appears as a 46% 2022 Canadian/export bottling.
