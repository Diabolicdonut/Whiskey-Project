The short version

A rye-recipe bourbon with fruit and spice, sometimes slow to open.

Breaking Bourbon and the Whiskey Shelf find familiar bourbon sweetness and spice rather than an exceptional departure. Amongst the Whiskey reports a muted initial glass that develops fruit and toffee after resting, with limited texture. The combined evidence supports good bourbon above the ordinary midpoint but below the fuller Weller Full Proof. This is bourbon, not rye whiskey.
