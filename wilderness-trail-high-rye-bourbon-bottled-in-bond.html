Wilderness Trail High Rye Bourbon Bottled in Bond is 100 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: caramel, oak and baking spice. The key question is whether that character persists as viscous buttery sweetness.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 100 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is savory pepper and wood contrast the sugar. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

The documented middle is viscous buttery sweetness. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 100 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Wilderness Trail High Rye Bourbon Bottled in Bond.
