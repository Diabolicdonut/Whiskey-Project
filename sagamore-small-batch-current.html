The short version

The approachable 93-proof Maryland house rye: warm apple/cherry fruit, honey-caramel sweetness, mild oak and moderate pepper, designed for everyday rye drinking and cocktails.

Exact identity: Sagamore Small Batch Rye Whiskey, standard 93-proof 100% Maryland-distilled core release introduced May 2024. General core expression, not the distinct individual documented Batch 2R currently published.

Background and history

Earlier Sagamore brands relied partly on Indiana contract-distilled rye while Sagamore's Baltimore distillery, opened in 2017, matured its own stock.

Small Batch entered nationwide/worldwide core distribution in May 2024 as a 93-proof Maryland-made rye; the ongoing core expression may vary between 20-barrel batches. Existing Batch 2R research remains a preserved, distinct sample.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

Sagamore's first fully Maryland-distilled widely distributed core rye, launched May 2024. A non-chill-filtered blend of triple-distilled high-rye and low-rye whiskeys aged four to six years, assembled in 20-barrel batches; the producer lists 93 proof.

Critical review synthesis

Amanda Schuster · Distiller

Describes a cherry-cola or root-beer-style nose, a gently herbal, cinnamon-and-butterscotch palate, and spice-forward finish with light body. Considers it particularly useful for cocktails.

Scope: Core 93-proof Maryland-made Small Batch introduced May 2024, no explicit batch number.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Start neat to establish the fruit-and-honey baseline with 93-proof accessibility and measure whether spice persists.

Rocks

A single cube can work, though it will soften a comparatively light texture.

Old Fashioned

An excellent practical Old Fashioned choice: moderate sweetness lets cherry/apple and baking spice remain visible.

Manhattan

Strong general-purpose Manhattan component; dry vermouth choice and citrus bitters can emphasize underlying grain spice.

Highball

Useful in a highball because the relatively modest price makes mixing less wasteful than with rare reserves.

Serving suggestions are editorial starting points, grounded in documented strength and style; adjust to your taste.
