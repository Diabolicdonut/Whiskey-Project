How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Peerless Small Batch Barrel Proof Bourbon is 109.5 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: tobacco, ripe fruit and caramel. The key question is whether that character persists as chewy buttered sweetness and spice.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 109.5 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is persistent warmth and seasoned oak. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is chewy buttered sweetness and spice. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 109.5 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Peerless Small Batch Barrel Proof Bourbon.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from tobacco, ripe fruit and caramel toward persistent warmth and seasoned oak. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
