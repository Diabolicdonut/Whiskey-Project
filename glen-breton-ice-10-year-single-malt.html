Critical review synthesis

Charles · Whisky Magazine

The taster finds a relatively inviting aroma but rubber-like, solvent-like and astringent delivery that remains difficult to interpret after dilution. These are reported sensory impressions, not a chemical contamination finding.

Scope: Exact 57.2%, 2015 bottling field; the visible Charles account is used, not an assumed second tab.

Chris Goodrum · Whisky According to Chris Whiskyman

Goodrum’s earlier assessment finds excessive wood and weak integration under water. The July 2015 retaste is more receptive to the lively oily character, while retaining dryness and limited complexity.

Scope: Exact 57.2% notes labelled August 2010 and July 2015. One taster with two assessments, not two independent votes.
