How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: separate wine fruit from rye

At 90 proof, Templeton Midnight Rye needs an undiluted reference first. Follow berry pastry and dried fruit into the cream and spice, then judge whether the short sweet close leaves the rye clearly defined. Port is added directly to this blend.

Water: use a separate trial

Add only a few drops to a second pour of Templeton Midnight Rye. Compare the sugared fruit with the grain and rye spice; stop if dilution makes the middle less coherent or the already short finish disappear.

A Manhattan variation: start with less vermouth

Templeton Midnight Rye already contains port wine and contributes berry sweetness. Begin a small 90-proof whiskey-and-bitters trial with a restrained measure of vermouth, tasting before adding more. The objective is clear rye character rather than stacking sweet wine flavors.

Rocks: monitor the quick fade

Try Templeton Midnight Rye on one large cube while keeping the neat pour beside it. Cooling may make the sweet berries pleasant, but check whether vanilla and spice retain enough definition as the 90-proof blend dilutes.
