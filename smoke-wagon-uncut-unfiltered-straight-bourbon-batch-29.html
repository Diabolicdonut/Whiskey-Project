How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Smoke Wagon Uncut Unfiltered Straight Bourbon (Batch 29) is 115.78 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: caramel, citrus and ginger. The key question is whether that character persists as buttery cherry, chocolate and leather.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 115.78 proof, add a few drops to test whether the documented warmth releases more flavor rather than simply flattening it. The relevant finishing behavior is dry cigar and oak remain. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is buttery cherry, chocolate and leather. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. The extra proof leaves room for ice dilution, while bitters can frame the spice.
