History and line identity

Lessons Honored, released in August 2020, is Little Book Chapter 4. Its combination of brown-rice bourbon, rye and conventional bourbon makes it straight whiskey rather than an all-bourbon blend. A grassy opening distinguishes it from the preceding chapter.
