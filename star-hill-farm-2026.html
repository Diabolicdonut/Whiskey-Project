How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow cream into dry oak

Star Hill Farm Wheat Whisky (2026) (116.4 proof): The cream, nut and fruit detail provides the opening attraction. Follow the late citrus, char and tannin before judging the whole pour by its first sweetness.

Water: retain the creamy middle

Star Hill Farm Wheat Whisky (2026): At 116.4 proof, add drops gradually and compare with a neat sample. Stop when body thins or the dry finish becomes the main remaining flavor.

A wheat-whiskey flight

Star Hill Farm Wheat Whisky (2026): Compare with the inaugural release or Bernheim Barrel Proof. The useful questions concern grain development and texture, rather than which bottle carries a newer date.

Avoid a dessert-heavy mixer

Star Hill Farm Wheat Whisky (2026): Custard and fruit already occupy the sweet side. Heavy syrup can obscure the grain while leaving the tannic close unchanged; establish the neat balance first.
