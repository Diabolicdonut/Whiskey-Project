Critical review synthesis

Chip Dykstra · The Rum Howler Blog

In a blind comparison with Crown Royal Regal Apple, Dykstra prefers Wiser’s crisper fruit and more evident whisky character. He finds the sweetness excessive, with medium body and a middling-length sugary/spicy finish.
