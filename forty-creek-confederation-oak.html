Critical review synthesis

talexander · Whisky Connosr

This Lot 1867-M sample is rich, distinctive and fruit-led, with a buttery presentation and useful spice. It provides a favorable counterpoint to the less successful lots.

Scope: May 15, 2021; explicitly Lot 1867-M, 40%; one detailed community account.

Jason Hambrey · Whisky Connosr

Hambrey’s comparison finds the 2022 sample raw, poorly balanced and unpleasant at the ending, unlike his cleaner 2015 Lot F. His tasting-based production suspicions are not adopted as facts.

Scope: February 3, 2023; explicit 2022 versus 2015 Confederation Oak samples; not all products discussed on this page are this whisky.

Nathan · The Scotch Noob

Nathan’s Lot D is silky but simple, with distracting alcohol aromas and a bitter, drying ending. His experience challenges an assumption that all older lots were excellent.

Scope: December 30, 2019; 2015-purchased 750 mL Lot 1867-D, California USD 55; historical bottle, not current packaging.
