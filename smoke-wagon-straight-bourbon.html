How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Smoke Wagon Straight Bourbon is 92.5 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: caramel, honey and toasted oak. The key question is whether that character persists as toffee, nutmeg and tobacco with good body.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 92.5 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is rye spice follows fading sweetness. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is toffee, nutmeg and tobacco with good body. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 92.5 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Smoke Wagon Straight Bourbon.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from caramel, honey and toasted oak toward rye spice follows fading sweetness. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
