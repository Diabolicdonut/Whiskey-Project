Critical review synthesis

Aengus McCloud · Dramface

McCloud finds vanilla, fruit candy, wintergreen and oak, a thinner-than-expected mouthfeel, and a long sweet/cocoa ending. He regards it as good at full strength but finds its charm falls away with more dilution.
