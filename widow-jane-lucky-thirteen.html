Critical review synthesis

Mark Pringle · The People’s Bourbon Review

Pringle finds limited aroma, a simple sweet-oak palate and a short ending, judging the price disproportionate to the richness of his sample.

Scope: 2021 batch 12, bottle 128; 93-proof blend.

Beverage Testing Institute tasting panel · Tastings / BTI

The panel describes nut-oil and banana-pastry aromas, a medium-to-full body, and a longer fruit-and-marzipan ending. This later sample is materially richer than Pringle’s sparse 2021 bottle.

Scope: July 2025 blend assessment; not the 2021 batch 12.

Different dated recurring batches disagree. The number is provisional; no retailer-pick score is generalized to every bottle.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Inspect neat first; the record is too divided to assume a rich profile in every batch.

Rocks / water

Use drops before a large cube when a sample seems restrained.

Old Fashioned

A small trial can show whether its oak-led profile survives bitters and syrup; a weak batch may be easily obscured.

Manhattan

Try a less assertive vermouth before a full cocktail, especially with a batch like Pringle’s.

Serving suggestions are editorial starting points, grounded in documented strength and style; adjust to your taste.
