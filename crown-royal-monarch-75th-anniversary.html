The short version

A delicate mature-tasting Canadian blend with sharply opposed published assessments. The anniversary number describes the brand; it is not a seventy-five-year age statement.

Exact identity: Crown Royal Monarch 75th Anniversary Blend, the individually numbered 2014 NAS limited release, 40% ABV / 80 proof.

Background and history

The June 12, 2014 producer announcement marks the brand’s 75th anniversary with a limited, individually numbered release at USD 75 / 750 mL. It is a separate blend from recurring Deluxe.

Independent reviewers disagree materially: one praises restrained mature balance, while another records an artificial note and drying ending. Both are retained rather than allowing commemorative status to decide the score.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The producer credits master blender Andrew MacKay and selected stocks including Coffey Rye from Gimli. It supplies no age statement or separate finishing specification. Mature-oak impressions in a tasting do not authenticate an age.

Critical review synthesis

Christopher Null · Drinkhacker

Null finds an agreeable texture and some balance but limited development, an artificial aroma and an astringent ending; air improves the objectionable character.

Scope: July 19, 2014; exact Monarch 80 proof; B assessment.

Geoff Kleinman · Drink Spirits

Kleinman finds mature oak, restrained sweetness and spice well integrated in a light, deliberate style, with a clean, moderately persistent ending.

Scope: June 23, 2014; exact Monarch 75th Anniversary 40%, NAS; much more favorable than Null.
