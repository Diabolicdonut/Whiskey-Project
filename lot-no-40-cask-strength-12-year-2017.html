The short version

A full-bodied, layered inaugural cask-strength release with strong balance and persistent rye/fruit character. It is scored for execution within Canadian all-rye, not its collector reputation.

Exact identity: Lot No. 40 Cask Strength First Edition, 2017 Northern Border Rare Release, twelve years, 55% ABV / 110 proof, 750 mL; 4,968 numbered bottles.

Background and history

The inaugural 2017 Northern Border Collection Rare Release was announced alongside three other distinct Canadian whiskies. Its producer original price was CAD 69.95 / 750 mL.

Later eleven-year 2018 and NAS 2019 versions remain separate. The name First Edition is not evidence that no stronger private samples previously existed.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

Corby’s September 19, 2017 announcement authenticates twelve years, 55%, 100% rye and new white oak, with October Canadian availability. Matched technical reporting describes column distillation followed by copper-pot distillation. The public release is separated from earlier unbottled distillery samples. No second cask finish is documented.

Serving recommendations

Neat
