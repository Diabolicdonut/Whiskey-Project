How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: judge the finish

Widow Jane Decadence (2025) (91 proof): Follow maple and buttered syrup into the shorter peppery close; the question is whether sweetness stays integrated.

Water: preserve the middle

Widow Jane Decadence (2025) (91 proof): At this moderate strength, begin undiluted. A few drops are a separate experiment because the gentler body and short ending can lose definition.

One cube, separately

Widow Jane Decadence (2025) (91 proof): Compare chilled and neat samples before committing a whole pour. Cooling may mute the syrup aroma without supplying more finishing depth.

Keep dessert drinks restrained

Widow Jane Decadence (2025) (91 proof): Use little added syrup in an Old Fashioned trial: maple finishing already supplies the sweet premise. Avoid treating the finish as permission to double the sugar.
