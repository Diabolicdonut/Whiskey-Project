History and line identity

The 117 Series began in 2021 as Old Forester’s small experimental release line. Its first bonded rye, Batch 001, arrived in March 2025, extending a rye program better known for the regular 100-proof bottle. The batch designation matters: this record describes that inaugural release, not every future bonded rye.

Critical synthesis

The matched review’s strongest feature is the fragrant, sweeter opening. Grain spice becomes more conventional as the sip moves into leathery wood, and the brief close limits the premium case. This is a refinement of a familiar rye style rather than evidence that bonded status by itself improves flavor.

How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: examine the opening

Old Forester The 117 Series: Bottled in Bond Rye (Batch No. 001) at 100 proof. Use a tulip glass to compare the fruit aroma with the more woody middle; the short close is the important qualification.

A little water

Try a few drops in a second pour and check whether lemon and molasses persist. Avoid chasing a lower proof at the expense of body.

A restrained Manhattan

Use less vermouth initially so melon, rye and leather remain perceptible. This is an inferred use, not a tested recipe.

A rye flight
