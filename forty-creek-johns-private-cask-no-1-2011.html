Critical review synthesis

Davin de Kergommeaux · Canadian Whisky

De Kergommeaux finds powerful spice and dark-fruit development with substantial body and exceptional persistence for the bottling strength. It is an assertive rather than merely gentle profile.

Scope: August 14, 2011; exact original No. 1, 40%; barrel-selection information is attributed to his Hall interview.

Victor · Whisky Connosr

Victor finds coherent rye, wood and wine-like flavors with good variety, while wanting more aromatic strength and better refinement of the wine-like element.

Scope: January 17, 2013; explicit Lot 2011, bottle 3486, opened one month; separate category-relative scores not mechanically converted.

Whisky Magazine taster · Whisky Magazine

The accessible taster finds a fuller palate than the aroma suggests, developed fruit and spice, and an increasingly dry finish. This is a more restrained assessment than the strongest specialist praise.

Scope: Exact year-bottled 2011 John’s Private Cask, 40%, NAS; accessible complete taster section, no hidden second review inferred.
