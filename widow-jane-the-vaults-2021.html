Background and history

The Vaults 2021 belongs to the annual old-and-rare series begun in 2019. Its documented finishing wood and minimum age define this edition, independently of handwritten ordinary batch numbers.

This 2021 release uses adirondack oak finishing casks; 42-month air-seasoned staves, heavy toast and 3.5-level char. Wood-air-seasoning time describes the staves before barrel assembly; it is not extra years of whiskey age.

Critical review synthesis

Jake Emen · Distiller

Emen reports strong sweet vanilla/treacle aromas with mint and cinnamon, rich fudge beside tannic oak and clove, and a long syrupy finish with cedar and char.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Start neat after a short rest to inspect the year-specific mature wood and sweetness.

Rocks / water

Test drops before a large cube; the documented finish/wood balance matters more than the proof alone.

Old Fashioned

Use a small, low-syrup trial if mixing; the finishing treatment deserves a neat assessment first.

Manhattan

Try a restrained vermouth measure so the older wood and finish retain their distinction.
