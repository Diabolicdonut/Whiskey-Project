Critical review synthesis

Chip Dykstra · The Rum Howler Blog

Dykstra finds layered toffee, fruit and spice, favors ice for its chocolate-like development, and regards the fifteen-year as subtler than the eighteen. A grain-spice sting slightly limits the finish.

Scope: December 1, 2017, exact fifteen-year / 40%; his 2018 announcement repeats this review rather than adding another tasting.

The Muskox · Malt Runners

The Muskox appreciates the fragrant nose and sticky brown-sugar character but finds the body thin, the middle grainy and metallic-green, and the finish short. This is substantive counterevidence to uniformly smooth marketing.

Scope: February 17, 2022, official fifteen-year / 40% miniature; duplicate Reddit posting is not another reviewer.

Stephen Beaumont · Beaumont Drinks

Beaumont values balance and gentle approachability, with caramel, dried fruit and a drying peppery ending. He finds it too soft for assertive mixing and recommends trying it neat.

Scope: Exact fifteen-year / 40%, reviewer-purchased bottle; article’s Canadian price range is separate from the dated U.S. offer.
