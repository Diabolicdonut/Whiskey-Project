The short version

A solid Vancouver Island single malt: honey, vanilla, grain and toasted sweetness with enough tannin and pepper to stay structured, but less refinement than the separate age-stated release.

Exact identity: Shelter Point Classic Single Malt, core NAS expression at 46% ABV / 92 proof; the exact early-2023 NAS review is kept distinct from the separately age-stated 7 Year Classic.

Background and history

Shelter Point began distilling on Vancouver Island in 2011 and now operates Forsyths wash and spirit copper pot stills on the coastal farm.

The Classic name continues the distillery’s core single-malt lineage; the exact NAS bottle reviewed by Dramface is not merged with the later 7 Year Classic.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Start neat at room temperature to assess the honeyed malt and tannin balance.

Rocks

One small cube can soften pepper and tannin without erasing the grain character.

Highball

A restrained soda highball should preserve the bright malt and coastal freshness.

Old Fashioned

Use very little sugar; the whisky already carries honeyed and vanilla sweetness.

Manhattan

Choose a lighter vermouth so the malt is not buried.

Serving suggestions are editorial starting points, grounded in documented strength and style; adjust to your taste.
