Background and history

Released in June 2018 to commemorate the Treaty of 1818 boundary anniversary. The issuer distinguishes it from the prior One Fifty bottle and simultaneous Seasoned Oak release.

Contemporary specialist accounts report 16,000 Canadian-market bottles. NAS is retained: an inferred component age and the commemorative date are not a bottle age statement.

Critical review synthesis

Selfbuilt · Selfbuilt’s Whisky Analysis

Selfbuilt finds a clean, representative Canadian corn-led profile with acceptable body and a medium, mildly bitter finish, but little depth or surprise. His competent-average conclusion tempers stronger praise.

Scope: July 14, 2018 exact 43.4% Canada 2018; faint solvent-like sensory language is not a contamination claim.

Davin de Kergommeaux · Canadian Whisky

The favorable account emphasizes wood, caramel, fruit and warming spice, with a long pepper-to-citrus ending. That stronger finish impression differs from Selfbuilt.

Scope: June 30, 2018 named Canada 2018 review; its 43.3% specification is explicitly disputed.

Neat
