Critical review synthesis

Chip Dykstra · The Rum Howler Blog

Dykstra finds a coherent grain-and-wood profile with enough development to offset some youth, and useful flavors emerge over ice. He considers it a successful return to balance.

Scope: December 15, 2016 stated publication date; exact Founder’s Reserve at 45%, producer specifications quoted separately from tasting.

Bryan Vanderkruk · Toronto Whisky Society

Vanderkruk finds a harsh aroma and young, relatively ordinary palate, with the advertised barley character less distinctive than expected. He notes that the distillery sample glass may have limited the experience.

Scope: May 21, 2017; exact Founder’s Reserve section at 45%, distillery sample; sibling sections not counted as this whisky’s evidence.
