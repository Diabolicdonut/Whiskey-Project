Understanding the Quality Scale

5.0 is the true category midpoint: ordinary, competent whiskey. Worst and best are the strongest-supported practical extremes found in a broad published-critical survey. A curated shelf can cluster above 5; there is no target distribution and empty integer bands are allowed.

Quality scores are category-relative. A 9.0 Bourbon means elite within Bourbon; a 9.0 Rye means elite within Rye. The numbers are not claims that unlike whiskey styles taste equivalent.
