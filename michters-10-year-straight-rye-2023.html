Critical synthesis

Creamy confectionery aromas lead to a buttery, fruit-and-pastry middle. The close preserves some sweetness before mildly tannic oak takes over. That small late qualification keeps the favorable synthesis grounded; the calibrated score is not the source’s sweeping category superlative.
