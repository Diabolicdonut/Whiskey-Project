Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The photographed front label authenticates The Town Collection, Double Barreled, 45% ABV and 750 mL. Distiller describes initial white-oak maturation, recasking into new heavily toasted white oak, then the brand’s maplewood marrying-vat treatment. The 2017 reviewer describes the second oak as heavily toasted/lightly charred; the indexed LCBO description calls it heavily charred. Precise char level, aging duration and time in each stage remain unresolved, without changing the authenticated expression identity.

Critical review synthesis

TOModera · TOModera Whisky Reviews

TOModera enjoys sweet fruit and the added oak, but reports a varnish-like aroma and raw-corn character, with uneven palate integration. He prefers it to Original while wishing for more spice.

Scope: November 27, 2017; exact 45% ABV brown-label Town Collection bottle; CAD 44.95 LCBO, 76 points.

Blair Phillips · Distiller

Phillips finds developed fruit, honey and wood, with sour/spicy balance and a persistent tangy ending. His more enthusiastic integration judgment is retained alongside the less favorable sample.

Scope: Exact 45% ABV Town Collection Double Barrel expert tasting; 92 points.
