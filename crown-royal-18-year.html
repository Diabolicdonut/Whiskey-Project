Background and history

A December 14, 2021 launch announcement and the later November 2022 producer announcement establish the 2021 introduction. Reviews describing it as new for 2022 reflect their review era, not a separate 2022 expression.

This age-stated identity is not conflated with XR Waterloo Red or XR LaSalle Blue. NC’s August–October 2026 regular list specifies 18Y, 80 proof and USD 144.95 / 750 mL, code 37-915.

Critical review synthesis

Kara Newman · Wine Enthusiast

Newman finds layered pastry-like sweetness, fruit and several baking-spice notes, ending warm. Her reading gives the whisky greater complexity than the quieter second record.

Scope: December 31, 2022 issue; exact 18-year / 40% ABV / 750 mL, 96 points.

David Savona · Whisky Advocate

Savona finds good balance and easy drinkability, with restrained caramel, citrus and wood. He judges the flavors pleasant but not particularly complex.

Scope: Summer 2022; exact 18-year / 40% ABV identity, 89 points. Substantive indexed review text inspected; direct page timed out.
