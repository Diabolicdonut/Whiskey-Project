The short version

The blue-packaged XR is a separate fruit-rich historical blend, with favorable complexity evidence and a credible contrary integration critique. It is not the original Waterloo edition.

Exact identity: Crown Royal XR Blue — LaSalle, second Extra Rare edition introduced in June 2012, including remaining LaSalle stocks in a Canadian blend, 40% ABV / 80 proof, NAS.

Background and history

The 2012 producer release expressly distinguishes the new edition from Waterloo XR introduced in 2006. Blue packaging marks the LaSalle blend; Canadian availability of old red stock overlapped the U.S. blue rollout.

The official U.S. introductory reference is USD 129.99 / 750 mL. Exact final bottling and cessation dates remain unresolved; modern residual listings do not establish ongoing production.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The May 16, 2012 producer release authenticates the second XR edition and its remaining LaSalle stocks, with U.S. availability beginning early June. The specialist account identifies old rye flavoring stocks blended with Gimli whisky. It is not entirely distilled at LaSalle and is not an age-stated single-distillery whisky. Closure dates do not establish the youngest component’s age.

Critical review synthesis

Davin de Kergommeaux · Canadian Whisky

De Kergommeaux finds a broad fruit-led profile with substantial texture and an extended spicy ending, while noting less crisp wood definition than the earlier XR.

Scope: August 4, 2012; exact LaSalle edition, directly distinguished from Waterloo.

Stephanie Moreno · Distiller

Moreno instead finds an aggressive, young-grain impression and an ending that turns overly drying, raising a material integration concern.

Scope: Professional tasting section explicitly identifies blue LaSalle; mixed user reviews of red and blue are not aggregated.

Bobby · Adventures in Whiskey

Bobby finds worthwhile complexity and a longer sweet/spicy ending than XO, although the grain-led entry is initially sharp.

Scope: February 3, 2016; LaSalle XR section, 40%, disclosed brand sample; XO evidence kept separate.
