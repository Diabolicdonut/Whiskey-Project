The short version

A historically distinct, well-supported mature Canadian blend with strong structure and rye-style character. It remains NAS, and its historical price record is not silently treated as a current 750 mL quote.

Exact identity: Crown Royal XR Red — Waterloo, original Extra Rare blend introduced in 2006, including surviving Waterloo stocks, 40% ABV / 80 proof, NAS.

Background and history

The red Waterloo XR preceded the blue LaSalle edition. A later museum object date of 2007 describes that collection bottle, not the first release year. A royal presentation Heritage Blend is not used as a substitute identity.

Wine Enthusiast’s October 2007 record quotes USD 150 and the L.A. society’s July 2012 record also shows USD 150. Neither inspected record states a matched bottle size. These are dated U.S. context, not a size-authenticated numeric Cost or current price; Value remains N/A.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The 2012 producer announcement dates the original Waterloo edition to 2006. De Kergommeaux describes Waterloo whisky blended with Gimli stocks, rather than an entirely Waterloo-distilled bottle. Closure/final-distillation accounts differ: the museum cites 1990, while the specialist reports last barrels filled in November 1992. Neither date creates a label age or a minimum age for the complete blend.

Critical review synthesis

Davin de Kergommeaux · Canadian Whisky

De Kergommeaux finds unusually coherent structure, substantial body and an extended finish, with mature wood supporting rather than swamping the grain and fruit.

Scope: February 14, 2012; original Waterloo XR, exact red-label photo; not the later LaSalle blend.

P.P. (Paul Pacult) · Wine Enthusiast

Pacult finds a refined, well-developed and lively whisky, with a textured middle and an elegant, drier ending.

Scope: October 1, 2007; pre-LaSalle original XR at 40%; original score band not converted mechanically.

Sku · L.A. Whisk(E)y Society

Sku finds a notably rye-like profile and a sweet, thick palate that works together, but considers the asking price difficult to justify.

Scope: Database entry added July 16, 2012 explicitly named XR Waterloo; one substantive member account, not a representative community consensus.
