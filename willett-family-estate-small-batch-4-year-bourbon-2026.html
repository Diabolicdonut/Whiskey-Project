How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Willett Family Estate Small Batch 4 Year Bourbon (2026) is 125.2 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: popcorn, vanilla and citrus pastry. The key question is whether that character persists as caramel corn and cream.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 125.2 proof, add a few drops to test whether the documented warmth releases more flavor rather than simply flattening it. The relevant finishing behavior is hot pepper, grain and dry oak. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is caramel corn and cream. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. The extra proof leaves room for ice dilution, while bitters can frame the spice.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from popcorn, vanilla and citrus pastry toward hot pepper, grain and dry oak. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
