Exact identity: Pike Creek ten-year Port Barrel / Vintage Port Finish, Canadian 2012 revival-era domestic release, 40% ABV / 80 proof; excludes the younger NAS U.S. export and later rum finish.

Background and history

Revived in late 2012 after the earlier Canadian Whisky Guild range disappeared. The Canadian ten-year / 40% bottle was followed by a separate NAS export, then the rum-finished ten-year in 2016.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The contemporary ten-year account identifies small copper-column double distillation, initial first-use ex-bourbon casks, then vintage port pipes. First-use bourbon here means once previously used for bourbon, not virgin oak. The ten-year Canadian statement is distinct from a seven/eight-year stock estimate for the NAS U.S. export. Finish duration and any direct port addition are unverified.

Critical review synthesis

Davin de Kergommeaux · Canadian Whisky

The ten-year domestic bottle is fruit-led and gently spicy, with rising warmth and a long citrus-edged finish.

Scope: March 6, 2013 ten-year / 40% scope; NAS export account excluded.

Selfbuilt · Selfbuilt’s Whisky Analysis
