Critical review synthesis

Stephen Beaumont · Beaumont Drinks

Beaumont finds developed nut/citrus aromas, intense chocolate/vanilla and a long coffee/pepper finish. He calls it elegant at strength but finds dilution sacrifices some complexity.

Scope: June 17, 2026; ten-year 67% ABV sample supplied by producer.

Robert K. Stephen · Set the Bar

Stephen reports rye/fruit alongside a cement-like impression and pronounced burning when neat. Ice reduces the heat but strengthens that mineral aroma; his response is ambivalent rather than an endorsement.

Scope: May 27, 2026; ten-year 67% ABV release.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Start with a very small neat sample; published reactions to the 67% integration conflict substantially.

Rocks / water

Add drops gradually, comparing with neat; Beaumont finds some complexity lost, while Stephen finds ice more approachable.

Old Fashioned

A small measured drink can use its strength without assuming the burn is a virtue.
