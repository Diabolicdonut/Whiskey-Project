How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Russell’s Reserve 6 Year Rye is 90 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: hay and vanilla carry light sweetness. The key question is whether that character persists as caramel and candy-like fruit form a gentle middle.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 90 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is pepper and leather add brief warmth. Stop early if the already gentle body becomes less distinct.

Manhattan: let the grain lead

Russell’s Reserve 6 Year Rye brings caramel and candy-like fruit form a gentle middle. Start with restrained sweet vermouth and bitters; the stated strength calls for a light hand with vermouth so the grain character remains visible.

Old Fashioned or a small highball

Use little sugar first: hay and vanilla carry light sweetness already provides part of the sweet impression. A short soda highball is the gentler option; too much water can erase 90 proof.
