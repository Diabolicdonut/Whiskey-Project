Critical review synthesis

Stephanie Moreno · Distiller

Moreno finds sweetness near the limit, with rye adding needed dimension. Bitter char and a slick texture remain; she favors it over ice or in mixed drinks more than neat.

Scope: Exact 1858 / 40% ABV entry, 78-point published judgment.

Thirty-One Whiskey tasting record · Thirty-One Whiskey

The review reports a solvent-like neat aroma but pleasant sugar, citrus and pepper on the palate. Ice reduces the bitterness, and cocktails perform better than an unmodified pour.
