How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Old Grand-Dad Bourbon is 80 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: restrained vanilla and cinnamon open. The key question is whether that character persists as light caramel forms the middle.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 80 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is modest brown sugar and apricot linger briefly. Stop early if the already gentle body becomes less distinct.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is light caramel forms the middle. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 80 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Old Grand-Dad Bourbon.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from restrained vanilla and cinnamon open toward modest brown sugar and apricot linger briefly. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
