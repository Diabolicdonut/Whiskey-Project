Background and history

Eleven Souls is the 2018 annual release, linked to the family story of William Gooderham’s care for eleven orphaned children. The current modern whisky is a Windsor blend; the heritage name does not establish Toronto production.

Corby’s 2019 annual report confirms Eleven Souls in the second Rare Range series. No exact regular dated U.S. offer was authenticated; Canadian personal-price reports and mixed-lot auctions are not U.S. consumer Value evidence.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The documentation describes eleven separately developed whisky components using different distillation and oak regimes, including new white oak, ex-bourbon and used Canadian-whisky vessels. It is a blend of component streams rather than one whisky finished sequentially through eleven barrels. Some sources call these five grains while listing varieties and malted states of rye/barley; the documented cereal families and processing forms are stated explicitly instead of inventing a fifth cereal or a fixed recipe.

Critical review synthesis

Blair Phillips · Distiller

Phillips praises the coherence of distinct grain voices, a developed fruit-and-spice core and energetic complexity. His high recommendation is used as sensory evidence, not converted into a native category endpoint.

Scope: Exact 2018 Eleven Souls / 49% / NAS review.

Chip Dykstra · The Rum Howler Blog

Dykstra finds richness, repeated balance and a comfortable substantial texture through the ending. His packaging dissatisfaction is separate from the liquid assessment, and rum-like flavor does not prove rum addition.

Scope: September 24, 2019, exact 2018 release; sensory sections rather than presentation points.

Drams of Whisky reviewer · Drams of Whisky

The taster finds an appealing creamy, sweet-spicy blend that fills a useful place in the collection, but personally prefers other bottles in the 2018 series. This more measured reaction is retained.

Scope: Tasted November 8, 2018; exact 49% Eleven Souls, not an assumed Little Trinity review.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Water / rocks
