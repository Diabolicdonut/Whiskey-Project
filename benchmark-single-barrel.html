The short version

A useful mid-proof selection; the barrel matters more than the name.

Four publishers supply barrel-level evidence of caramel, fruit and spice with a more approachable delivery than Full Proof. The samples vary in polish and finish, so a good-category position is better supported than a premium single-barrel ranking. It remains above No. 8 and below the richer Blanton’s samples.

At $29.95, it is a good buy for this accessible style. Bonded costs $5 less with comparable reviewed quality, while the fullness of Full Proof costs less too. The individual barrel is the reason to choose it.
