The short version

A sherry-treated Canadian blend with generous fruit and texture, offset by disagreement about spice integration and finish length. The newer Small Batch label does not establish an eight-year age statement.

Exact identity: Canadian Club Small Batch Sherry Cask, newer flask-bottle formulation reviewed in 2014–2015, 41.3% ABV / 82.6 proof; distinct from Invitation Series No. 1 Sherry Cask 15 Year.

Background and history

Eric Burke documents a Small Batch bottle, lot C12-232, purchased in Illinois and reviewed July 22, 2014. Dykstra’s January 25, 2015 review explicitly distinguishes the newer flask bottle from the earlier Sherry Cask presentation.

The accepted Invitation Series 15 Year is a different age, strength and named programme. It remains separately indexed. A precise first-release date and discontinuation date for the newer Small Batch are not established.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

Chip Dykstra describes the family’s blend-before-aging process and reports six to eight years in white oak before further maturation in Jerez sherry casks. That account supplies production context, not an authenticated label age or a precise finishing duration for every Small Batch lot. The pictured newer label authenticates 41.3% ABV and carries no age statement.

Critical review synthesis

Chip Dykstra · The Rum Howler Blog

Dykstra praises depth and a soft mouthfeel, but finds the sherry fruit and rye spice poorly reconciled. He also finds the ending shorter than the palate suggests.

Scope: January 25, 2015; exact newer Small Batch 41.3% ABV identity, 85.5 points.

Eric Burke · Bourbon Guy

Burke finds a substantial sweet palate and a fruit-led, drying finish. He likes it as a relaxed pour and prefers it to core Canadian Club, while questioning whether it earns twice the price.

Scope: July 22, 2014; 41.3% ABV, lot C12-232; USD 29.99 for 750 mL at Binny’s, Bloomington, Illinois.
