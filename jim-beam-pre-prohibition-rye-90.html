Jim Beam Pre-Prohibition Style Rye — 90 proof is 90 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: caramel and rye grain introduce vanilla and roasted nuts. The key question is whether that character persists as oak takes over late, leaving a medium, drying spice finish.

At 90 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is oak takes over late, leaving a medium, drying spice finish. Stop early if the already gentle body becomes less distinct.
