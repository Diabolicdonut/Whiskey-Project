The producer timeline places the 102 bottles under 2019, the award year. The inspected label’s Lot 2018 and Ashburn’s stated 2018 bottling precede that award. The existing candidate ID ending 2019 remains unchanged; no duplicate is created.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The producer archive and master-blender timeline identify the twenty-two-year all-rye release and 102 bottles. The exact photograph shows Lot 2018; matched reviews corroborate 43% ABV. Ashburn’s interview confirms bottling in 2018. A 1989 distillation account in that interview conflicts with the twenty-two-year chronology and the contemporary 1996 account. A single vintage is therefore not asserted. Exact barrel count and char specification remain unverified.
