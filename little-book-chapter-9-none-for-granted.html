Critical synthesis

The aroma has more fruit variety than the simpler butterscotch-and-wood middle. A longer, more complex close adds clove and char but also exposes warmth and tannin. Its quality case is patient development; drinkers sensitive to dry oak may find the saved premium Value optimistic for their preferences.
