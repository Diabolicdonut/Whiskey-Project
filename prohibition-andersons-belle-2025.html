Evidence limits and disagreement

VinePair and InsideHook enjoy the approachable wheated sip. Tasting Table also likes it but finds a tart, bitter finish and less body than stronger components. These event-based tastings have less depth than repeated full-bottle reviews.

Three independent publishers, but two brief media-event narratives. Confidence is medium, not a multi-bottle consensus.
