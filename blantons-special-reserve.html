The short version

Easy-going fruit and sweetness, with too little texture in the weaker sample.

Breaking Bourbon finds watery delivery and a finish vulnerable to dilution; Bourbon Culture is more positive about the fruit while still finding the result underwhelming. Slightly below the Maker’s/Bulleit ordinary midpoint is supported; this is not the defect-heavy poor end of bourbon.

The historical $48/700 mL reference equals $51.43 per 750 mL. That is below-fair value beside standard Maker’s, Buffalo Trace and Maker’s 46. It is a dated reference, not an authenticated current US price.

Critical evidence

The flavor evidence, with disagreement intact

This is a synthesis of credited written accounts, not an unperformed first-person tasting. Producer flavor claims are not counted as independent criticism.

Nose

Light fruit and caramel.

Palate

Gentle sweetness, with limited body in the weaker sample.

Finish

Short oak and spice; dilution can strip the profile.

Breaking Bourbon finds watery delivery and a finish vulnerable to dilution; Bourbon Culture is more positive about the fruit while still finding the result underwhelming. Slightly below the Maker’s/Bulleit ordinary midpoint is supported; this is not the defect-heavy poor end of bourbon.
