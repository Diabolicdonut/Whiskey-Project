History & identity

This dossier covers the historical 2011 Old Fashioned Sour Mash Taylor release. The process experiment is distinct from ordinary Taylor small batch and from other historical wood experiments. The accepted critics disagree about distinctiveness and finish duration; no individual Value or present stock claim is created.

Evidence limits and disagreement

BourbonBlog is enthusiastic about rich fruit and oak. Sipology finds it enjoyable but less special, with a short finish and only a mild recommendation. Two independent historical accounts support a tentative ‡ score; cost-driven reservations are not used as defects in Quality.
