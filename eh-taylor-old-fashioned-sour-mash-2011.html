Evidence limits and disagreement

BourbonBlog is enthusiastic about rich fruit and oak. Sipology finds it enjoyable but less special, with a short finish and only a mild recommendation. Two independent historical accounts support a tentative ‡ score; cost-driven reservations are not used as defects in Quality.

Two independent critical publishers only; tentative individual assessment (§).

2 cited tasting article(s), 2 independent publisher(s). Multiple writers, a video and written notes from the same outlet count once. The research aim is 3–6 meaningful independent critics; thin but sufficient matched evidence carries §. No personal tasting or blind panel is claimed.
