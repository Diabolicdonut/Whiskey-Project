Critical synthesis

For 23A0044, jammy dark fruit and oily texture support substantial old wood, leather and tobacco. The finish still brings dry oak and an ash-like edge, so richness is not the same as freedom from tannin. The lighter second barrel’s notes and score are kept out of this synthesis.

Nose

Toffee, brown sugar, blackberry, fig, plum, dusty wood and tobacco.

Palate

Oily oak, leather, jammy fruit, citrus and honey with some ash-like dryness.

Finish

Dry wood, leather and cigar-ash character, cushioned by residual texture.
