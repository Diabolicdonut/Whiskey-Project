Specifications without invented precision

Critical evidence

The flavor evidence, with disagreement intact

A synthesis of credited critical accounts. Written notes paired with a YouTube episode and that episode count as one voice. Unavailable transcripts are not inferred from titles.

Nose

Oat cereal, cocoa and baking spice.

Palate

Savory grain and confectionery sweetness rather than a conventional vanilla/oak profile.

Finish

Dry oats, spice and cocoa persist.

Breaking Bourbon

Baking spice, toasted grain and a drying, flavorful finish. 2

The Daily Pour / house critic

Own tasting: savory oats and cocoa with a long finish; supplied sample disclosed. 4

Evidence limits and disagreement

Breaking Bourbon likes the baking-spice progression. The Daily Pour’s house critic finds an unusually grain-led, savory experiment and prefers it to Whisper. Their agreement supports an individual score, but only two outlets make it tentative ‡. Aggregated quotations on The Daily Pour are not counted again.

Two independent critical publishers only; tentative individual assessment (§).
