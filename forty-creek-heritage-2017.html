Critical review synthesis

Blair Phillips · Distiller

Phillips finds layered fruit, creamy texture and nutty/spicy development, with a more tart wood-led ending.

Scope: Professional exact Heritage section, 40%, NAS; production and sensory sections distinguished.

Chip Dykstra · The Rum Howler Blog

Dykstra finds a successful balance of sweet grain, wood and spice, with substantial texture and a satisfying ending. His strong judgment is not mechanically converted.

Scope: December 14, 2017; exact 2017 Heritage, full independent review.

paddockjudge · Whisky Connosr

This taster finds improved maturity balance and a chewy texture, while a sharp spirit note and warm exit limit the claim of seamlessness.

Scope: October 29, 2017; Lot 011, bottle 14777, 40%; one detailed community sample, not evidence of every bottle.
