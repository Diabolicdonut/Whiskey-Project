History and line identity

Triple Char was released in July 2026 as the first 117 bourbon made entirely at the reopened Whiskey Row distillery. The Louisville site reopened in 2018, making this a useful milestone in the line’s move from barrel experiments to a specifically documented distilling location.

Critical synthesis

The review’s char is supported by plum and cooked-fruit sweetness rather than described as simple ash. The ending remains woody, but less punishingly dry than the name might suggest. That balance makes it a stronger drinking proposition than Extra Extra Old, without implying that more char is universally better.
