Critical review synthesis

Selfbuilt · Selfbuilt’s Whisky Analysis

Selfbuilt finds a remarkably rum-like profile, gentle sweet corn, fruit and soft spice without burn. He finds it easy to enjoy but too sweet for his preference, with a faint saccharine edge at the end.

Scope: November 12, 2019, exact ten-year / 40% Lafleur; personal preference is kept distinct from demonstrated execution.
