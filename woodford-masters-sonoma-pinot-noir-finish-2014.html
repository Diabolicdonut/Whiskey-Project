Critical evidence

The flavor evidence, with disagreement intact

A synthesis of credited critical accounts. Written notes paired with a YouTube episode and that episode count as one voice. Unavailable transcripts are not inferred from titles.

Nose

Wine-led red fruit and earthy wood.

Palate

Fruit and tannin obscure the bourbon; balance is the repeated concern.

Finish

Dry, wine-heavy and sometimes funky or astringent.

Breaking Bourbon / Eric Hasman

Wine overwhelms the bourbon and produces an unbalanced, lingering aftertaste. 2

Bourbon Guy / Eric Burke

Earthy wine influence and a short, warm ending weaken the overall experience. 4

Whisky Saga / Thomas Øhrbom

Aromatic appeal is a counterpoint, though palate dryness remains evident. 5

Evidence limits and disagreement

Breaking Bourbon and Bourbon Guy find the wine influence poorly integrated. Whisky Saga supplies a more favorable aromatic account but also notes a dry, nearly astringent palate. This is a flawed historical release, not a verdict on Sonoma Triple Finish.
