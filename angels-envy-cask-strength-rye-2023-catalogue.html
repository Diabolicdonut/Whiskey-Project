Five exact-edition independent accounts agree this 2023 114.4-proof rye succeeds at integrating substantial rye spice with Sauternes sweetness and toasted-oak structure. Paste stresses rye-grain intensity and impressive heat control; The Whiskey Wash notes a creamier and dessert-like profile; InsideHook finds a drier, more herbal character. How to drink this particular 2023 cask-strength rye

Neat

Best first choice for its balance of Sauternes sweetness, textured rye spice and toasted oak. Begin small at 114.4 proof.

A little water

Especially useful for readers seeking less ethanol edge: Forbes observed the softer vanilla and toffee emerging.

One large cube

Works if you favor a slower, cooler sip; a softer nose and muted fruit are plausible consequences of chilling, not tested claims.

Manhattan or Old Fashioned

The rye strength and finishing can stand up to spirit-forward builds, but a $270 bottle is an expensive mixer; use Old Forester 100 Rye or Knob Creek Rye instead.

Avoid heavily sweet liqueurs or syrups until you know the exact bottle neat: the Sauternes cask already contributes substantial sweetness.
