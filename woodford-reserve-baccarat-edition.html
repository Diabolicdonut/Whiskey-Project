The short version

Good Cognac-finished bourbon; the crystal accounts for a large premium.

Breaking Bourbon finds fig, berry fruit and useful mouth coating from the Cognac finish. Bourbon Culture is less persuaded by earthy, mothball-like notes and a short ending. Very good bourbon below the elite ceiling is a bounded assessment of the liquid; the decanter and its price play no part in Quality.

At the historical $2,000/750 mL reference, this is at the practical poor end of drinking Value. The expensive crystal presentation cannot compensate for modestly premium, contested whiskey compared with bottles costing a fraction as much.

Scope: Core Baccarat expression, distinct from the unique Derby 150 American-whiskey blend. Price and crystal presentation do not score the liquid. 1
