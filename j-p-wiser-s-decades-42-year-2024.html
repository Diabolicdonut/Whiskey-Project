Critical review synthesis

David Tao · Drinkhacker

Tao finds a sharp smoky/savory nose, a sweeter tea-and-nut palate and a fruity, gently woody finish. He enjoys the development with time, but criticizes thin texture and a potentially divisive mismatch between aroma and palate.
