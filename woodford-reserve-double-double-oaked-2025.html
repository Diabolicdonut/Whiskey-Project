The short version

A divisive oak-saturated bourbon at a difficult current price.

Breaking Bourbon finds a strong nose followed by thinness, bitter dryness and excessive wood; Bourbon Culture finds richer oily dessert flavors but an increasingly one-dimensional oak finish. Good bourbon near ordinary-to-good references, below more consistently integrated double-oaked alternatives, is a conservative resolution of this major disagreement.

The current producer shop price is $199.99/700 mL, or $214.28 per 750 mL. Mixed integration and strong cheaper Double Oaked, Maker’s 46 and full-proof alternatives make this extremely poor value at that offer.

Scope: National 700 mL release introduced in 2025. Older 375 mL distillery editions remain historical context; current producer-shop price is $199.99, not launch MSRP. 1

Production

Specifications without invented precision

Longer residence in the second, heavily toasted and lightly charred barrel distinguishes Double Double Oaked. It uses two barrels in total. Producer language about an additional two years and total secondary maturation is ambiguous; it is not claimed to have a third barrel or a guaranteed overall age.

Recipe: C72 R18 MB10. Age: NAS · additional 2-year second-barrel maturation. Strength: 90.4 proof / 45.2% ABV. 1
