How to approach this expression

Barrell Seagrass is 118.4 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: herbal candy and ripe fruit. The key question is whether that character persists as creamy fruit layers and spice.

At 118.4 proof, add a few drops to test whether the documented warmth releases more flavor rather than simply flattening it. The relevant finishing behavior is warm dry oak provides a less seamless ending. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.
