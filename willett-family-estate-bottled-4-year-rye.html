How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Willett Family Estate Bottled 4 Year Rye is 111.2 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: floral herbs and caramel. The key question is whether that character persists as raisin, tea and menthol with citrus.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 111.2 proof, add a few drops to test whether the documented warmth releases more flavor rather than simply flattening it. The relevant finishing behavior is pepper and grain carry noticeable heat. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is raisin, tea and menthol with citrus. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. The extra proof leaves room for ice dilution, while bitters can frame the spice.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from floral herbs and caramel toward pepper and grain carry noticeable heat. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
