The short version

An approachable, fruit-led rye blend with a credible middle-ground drinking case. The abrupt finish is a real limit, even when the producer’s mission is accessibility.

Exact identity: Paradigm blend of straight ryes launched August 2023, 93 proof. Eric Hasman’s detailed review is batch 28; recipe proportions and source distillery names remain undisclosed.

Background and history

Paradigm marks the documented modern blend of Widow Jane’s own rye and sourced Kentucky/Indiana straight ryes. It is not simply a renamed legacy used-oak rye-mash bottle.

The launch explains the modern base later used for Apple Wood Rye. Different finishes keep their own sensory records.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The producer launch and matched review identify a blend of Brooklyn, Kentucky and Indiana straight ryes, non-chill filtered and proofed to 46.5% ABV. Mash bills and blend proportions are undisclosed. The NAS label is preserved; a legal minimum is not turned into an age statement.

Critical review synthesis

Eric Hasman · Breaking Bourbon

Hasman describes citrus, mint and anise aromas, controlled melon/raisin sweetness with butter and rye spice, then familiar pepper/oak. He likes the accessible balance while identifying a fast drop in finish intensity.

Scope: Batch 28, November 6, 2023; 93 proof.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Start neat to notice the fruit and citrus beside the moderate rye spice.

Rocks / water

Try drops first; its documented quick finish argues against excessive dilution.

Old Fashioned

Keep bitters and syrup restrained so the unusual fruit remains visible.

Manhattan

Use a lighter sweet vermouth trial; a strong modifier can obscure the easygoing base.
