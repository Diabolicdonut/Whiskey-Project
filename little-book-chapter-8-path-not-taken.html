History and line identity

Chapter 8 moved Little Book toward rye in May 2024. Freddie Noe combined six rye whiskeys with one older high-rye bourbon, making the rye-led result a blended straight whiskey rather than a single straight rye expression.

Critical synthesis

The review describes a coherent, full-bodied rye profile despite the varied ages. Bread, caramel and herbs lead into a long sweet-spicy close without obvious old-wood dominance. Its limitation is narrower stylistic range than some bourbon-led chapters, not lack of rye intensity.
