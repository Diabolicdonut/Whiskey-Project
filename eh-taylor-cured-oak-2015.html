Evidence limits and disagreement

Drinkhacker and Adventures in Whiskey favor the original 17-year release’s sweet/wood balance. signde drinks finds the flavor comparatively muted and the ending musty. That less enthusiastic specialist account limits the score rather than disappearing behind the release’s reputation.
