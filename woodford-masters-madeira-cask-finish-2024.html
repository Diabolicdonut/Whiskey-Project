Production

Specifications without invented precision

The blend includes Madeira-finished bourbon, rye and wheat whiskey plus unfinished wheat whiskey. It is not scored as ordinary bourbon.

Recipe: Bourbon + rye + wheat whiskeys · component ratios undisclosed. Age: NAS. Strength: 90.4 proof / 45.2% ABV. 1

C corn · R rye · W wheat · MB malted barley. Undisclosed proportions are not estimated from neighboring labels. A component’s oldest age is not the age of a blend. Proof is not a quality multiplier.

Evidence limits and disagreement

VinePair finds creamy development and an intriguing fruit-led sip. Mostly Bourbon praises the aroma but finds thin texture and a short finish. Drinkhacker strongly dislikes the oxidized wine, savory grain and poorly integrated wood. Their disagreement is retained.
