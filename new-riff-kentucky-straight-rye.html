How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

New Riff Kentucky Straight Rye is 100 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: charred citrus and toast. The key question is whether that character persists as silky honeyed orange with pepper.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 100 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is grain spice and heat persist. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Manhattan: let the grain lead

New Riff Kentucky Straight Rye brings silky honeyed orange with pepper. Start with restrained sweet vermouth and bitters; the stated strength calls for a light hand with vermouth so the grain character remains visible.

Old Fashioned or a small highball
