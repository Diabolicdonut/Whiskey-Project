Critical review synthesis

Chip Dykstra · The Rum Howler Blog

Dykstra describes a gently fruity, caramel-and-spice profile that mixes easily and avoids major rough edges. He also finds the flavor light and less full than expected, with only a modest sweet/spicy ending.
