History & identity

The name and the identified release

Woodford Reserve’s core grain recipes, Master’s Collection and Distillery Series serve different purposes. A named wood or grain experiment is not just a stronger version of the core bourbon. Annual editions and private selections require their own bottle and proof checks.

Producer announcement identifies 119.5 proof as the 2025 edition. A shop description calls it 2024; that conflict is recorded, and 121.2-proof 2024 is not merged.

Primary release and catalog context. 1

Production

Specifications without invented precision

A batch of Woodford’s bourbon recipe is combined and bottled at its resultant barrel strength. It is a batch release, not one private barrel.

Recipe: C72 R18 MB10. Age: NAS. Strength: 119.5 proof / 59.75% ABV. 1

C corn · R rye · W wheat · MB malted barley. Undisclosed proportions are not estimated from neighboring labels. A component’s oldest age is not the age of a blend. Proof is not a quality multiplier.

Critical evidence

The flavor evidence, with disagreement intact

A synthesis of credited critical accounts. Written notes paired with a YouTube episode and that episode count as one voice. Unavailable transcripts are not inferred from titles.

Nose

Caramel, vanilla, barrel char and baking spice.

Palate

Sweet oak and spice, with a more substantial texture than ordinary Woodford.

Finish

Pepper, caramel and warm wood linger; depth remains less than the best limited bourbons.

Breaking Bourbon

Matched 119.5 proof: good body and a pleasing, warm caramel/spice progression. 2

VinePair / David Tao

Matched 119.5 proof: balanced and lingering; less punch and complexity than some alternatives. 5

Evidence limits and disagreement

Breaking Bourbon and VinePair find a pleasing, composed 119.5-proof sip. Both describe it as arriving in late 2024; the producer markets 119.5 proof as the 2025 edition. The strength is matched, the labeling conflict stays explicit, and 121.2-proof 2024 evidence is excluded. Only two voices make this tentative ‡.

Two independent critical publishers only; tentative individual assessment (§).

2 cited tasting article(s), 2 independent publisher(s). Multiple writers, a video and written notes from the same outlet count once. The research aim is 3–6 meaningful independent critics; thin but sufficient matched evidence carries §. No personal tasting or blind panel is claimed.
