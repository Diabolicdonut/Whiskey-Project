Background and history

The May 2012 contemporary release record opens bottle reservations May 7, with September distillery pickup/general sales. It expressly calls this the second release and announces 6,600 bottles at CAD 69.95.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The contemporary announcement reproduces Hall’s account of separately aged grain whiskies blended and given another two years in casks previously holding his Niagara-grown vintage port-style wine. The heavy-char white-oak wine vessels are documented; no exact whole-blend age follows from that final stage. A sensory resemblance to bourbon does not change its Canadian blend category.

Critical review synthesis

Davin de Kergommeaux · Whisky Advocate

De Kergommeaux finds broad, developed dark-fruit and savory complexity with sweet tobacco and spice. His favorable assessment explicitly belongs to the 2012 edition.

Scope: Spring 2013 issue, 2012 Edition / 45%; C$70 is Canadian price context.

Ruben Luyten · WhiskyNotes

Luyten likes the evolving aroma but finds the wine-led palate less convincing and the long ending insufficiently defined.

Scope: May 30, 2013; explicit OB 2012 / 45% / 6,600 bottles.

TOModera · Malt Runners

TOModera enjoys the changing aroma and simple balanced palate but reports a very weak, brief finish. That directly conflicts with the longer-ending accounts.

Scope: Original September 29, 2014 review, later migrated; explicitly 2012 rather than 2009, personally purchased at release.
