The short version

An early Brooklyn grain experiment with a defensible named tasting record. Attractive roasted flavors are limited by a thin body and a short ending.

Exact identity: The one-year, 91-proof Chocolate Malt Whiskey reviewed in February 2017: Batch 2, Bottle 125, 375 mL. “Chocolate malt” describes roasted barley, not added chocolate.

Background and history

Under Cacao Prieto, Widow Jane explored its own pot-distilled grain recipes alongside sourced bourbon. The 2017 Chocolate Malt sample belongs to that early in-house work.

The reviewer found it through specialty gift sets containing several grain experiments. A set price does not establish an individual 375 mL bottle price.

Critical review synthesis

Randall H. Borkus · The Whiskey Reviewer

Borkus reports pronounced roasted/coffee-like sweetness and nuts, a warm but thin mouthfeel, and a pleasant dry ending that fades quickly. His C+ judgment supplies context; it is not converted mechanically into the project score.

Scope: February 2017; one year, 91 proof, Batch 2 Bottle 125 / 375 mL.
