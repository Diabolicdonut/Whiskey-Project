Critical review synthesis

Stephanie Moreno · Distiller

Moreno finds tart berry flavor with modest grain and oak-spice definition. She regards cocktails as the best use and notes a somewhat slick texture.

Scope: Exact 35% ABV Blackberry expert tasting; 79 points.

Whiskey Wench · The Whiskey Wench

The reviewer finds sweet fruit and vanilla balanced by tartness, preferring the expression with lemonade rather than neat. The independent purchased-bottle report supplies specific mixed-drink evidence.
