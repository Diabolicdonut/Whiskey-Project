Research dated 2026-10-05. **150 additions; 244 total dossiers.**

Current producer-listed whiskey expressions and programs, identified annual editions and supported named historical releases. This is not a claim to document every vintage, 192 Single Oak barrel, discontinued market label or individual private selection. Older Master’s Collection names without sufficient identity / sensory evidence are mapped in the heritage overview, not assigned invented specifications or scores. Non-whiskey vodka, cream liqueur, new make, rum and soft drinks are outside the dossier categories.
