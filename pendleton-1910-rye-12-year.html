The short version

A gentle, mature all-rye with recent support for texture and balanced spice, qualified by an older account of muted development. Its age is not a Quality bonus.

Exact identity: Pendleton 1910 Rye, twelve years, 100% Canadian rye, 40% ABV / 80 proof, 750 mL; excludes Pendleton 1910 ten-year American bourbon.

Background and history

Launched in 2011 as the 1910 Rye extension, named for the rodeo’s 1910 beginning. The name is not a distillation vintage.

The separately marketed ten-year 1910 Bourbon is an American expression and is outside this dossier.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.
