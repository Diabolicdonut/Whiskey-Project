History and line identity

The December 2023 Scotch Cask Finish references George Garvin Brown’s Scottish heritage. Its casks come from Speyside sherry- and wine-matured stock. That region and cask history do not imply an intensely peated bourbon, and restrained smoke is judged on its own terms.
