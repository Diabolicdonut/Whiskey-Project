How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: hear the change in direction

Old Forester The 117 Series (Batch No. 002) is 110 proof. Let sweet pear and honey give way to pepper, cinnamon and cherry. The contrast is the interest; the dry ending remains the limitation.

Water: keep cherry beside pepper

Use a few drops in a comparison glass. Stop if fruit recedes faster than tobacco and dry wood.

Old Fashioned: avoid extra pepper

Start with a restrained bitter dose. The bourbon already supplies substantial spice; more bitters need not add more useful structure.

A same-strength series flight

Pour Batch 001 and Batch 002 equally. Compare caramel-and-tobacco depth with Warehouse K’s sweeter opening and more assertive spicy middle.
