The short version

A full-barley Canadian whisky with sharply opposed reviews. It is not single malt: the producer confirms mostly unmalted barley.

Exact identity: Crown Royal Noble Collection Barley Edition, seventh and final edition, 2023, 45% ABV / 90 proof; a blend of unmalted- and malted-barley whiskies.

Background and history

The April 3, 2023 producer announcement identifies Barley Edition as the seventh and final Noble Collection release, at USD 79.99 / 750 mL. The established 2016 Cornerstone start is retained despite inaccurate chronology in some secondary coverage.

Producer figures resolve a reversed malted/unmalted ratio in one trade preview. Native single-malt or Scotch anchors are not applied to this mostly unmalted-barley blend.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Critical review synthesis

Davin de Kergommeaux · Canadian Whisky

De Kergommeaux finds developed grain character, layered fruit/herbal detail and confident balance. He strongly recommends it as an expressive barley whisky.

Scope: May 18, 2023; exact 2023 Barley Edition, full independent sensory account.

Aengus McCloud · Dramface

McCloud enjoys the texture but finds the palate disappointing after the aroma, with artificial/rubbery notes and more harshness after dilution. He considers it average.

Scope: July 6, 2023; self-purchased exact 45% Barley Edition at USD 82; 5/10 on that publisher’s own scale.

Editorial interpretation of the attributed published samples, not first-person tasting. Opposed judgments and sample-era limitations remain explicit; source grades are not mechanically converted to the project’s full-spectrum scale.
