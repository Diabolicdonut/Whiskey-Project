Production

Specifications without invented precision

The producer selects whiskeys from its broader network and combines them for Traveller. The 121-proof edition is a separately defined release; its ingredients cannot be inferred from the ordinary 90-proof blend.

Recipe: Blend of whiskeys · component grains and ratios undisclosed. Age: NAS. Strength: 121 proof / 60.5% ABV. 1
