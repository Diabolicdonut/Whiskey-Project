The short version

A light, ordinary bourbon with a useful budget price.

Breaking Bourbon finds a simple sweet profile and limited finish; the Whiskey Shelf and Bourbon Obsessed add usable fruit and spice rather than persistent defects. This fits the Maker’s/Bulleit ordinary midpoint. It is a clear step above No. 8’s recurring thin grain and bitterness, without the depth of Wild Turkey 101.

At $19.95, a competent low-proof bourbon is good value for simple sipping and mixed drinks. Evan Williams Bonded costs only a little more and supplies greater weight; that keeps this below the strongest budget buys.

Scope: The current named expression, rather than old No. 8 at a substituted strength. Proof alone does not establish a Quality advantage. 1
