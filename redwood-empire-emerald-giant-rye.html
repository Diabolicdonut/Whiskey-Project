How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Redwood Empire Emerald Giant Rye is 90 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: pine, anise and dill. The key question is whether that character persists as sweet peppery rye.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 90 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is leather, tobacco and lingering warmth. Stop early if the already gentle body becomes less distinct.

Manhattan: let the grain lead

Redwood Empire Emerald Giant Rye brings sweet peppery rye. Start with restrained sweet vermouth and bitters; the stated strength calls for a light hand with vermouth so the grain character remains visible.

Old Fashioned or a small highball

Use little sugar first: pine, anise and dill already provides part of the sweet impression. A short soda highball is the gentler option; too much water can erase 90 proof.
