History and line identity

Old Grand-Dad 16 was released in October 2024 as a mature limited extension of Beam’s longstanding high-rye line. It retains 100-proof strength while moving far beyond the ordinary 80-, bonded- and 114-proof range in age and price.

Critical synthesis

Cherry and berry sweetness support aged wood throughout the source account, with a creamy middle and lively cherry-cola close. Slight mustiness and rye spice supply mature structure without overwhelming the fruit. The quality case is this balance; the much higher price remains a separate question from everyday Old Grand-Dad value.
