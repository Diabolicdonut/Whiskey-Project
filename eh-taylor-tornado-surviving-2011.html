Evidence limits and disagreement

The Bourbon Babe finds rich texture without excessive oak. The Whiskey Reviewer finds more assertive wood but good balance. Whiskey Wonka likes the sooty, nutty character while separating a price complaint from flavor. Bourbonfool adds a favorable later bar tasting of the same historical release.
