How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: grain, fruit and tannin

Star Hill Farm Wheat Whisky (2025) (114.7 proof): Follow the berry and custard opening into the drying oak. The buttery texture praised by several sources should be judged alongside the shorter, tannic account.

Water: preserve the dissent

Star Hill Farm Wheat Whisky (2025): Try drops in a separate pour. Dramface reported weaker structure with water, so the higher proof is not a reason to assume dilution improves this release.

A two-year flight

Star Hill Farm Wheat Whisky (2025): Compare a small 2025 pour with 2026 at equal temperature. Assess grain, body and late tannin separately; neither a new vintage nor estate farming settles the result.

Ice: a brief comparison

Star Hill Farm Wheat Whisky (2025): Retain a neat reference if using a large cube. Cooling can hide the bakery fruit and make the astringent ending feel more dominant.
