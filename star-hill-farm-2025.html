The short version

Quality 7.2†/10; Value 5.4/10 at the stated reference.

Very good wheat-whiskey execution: expressive fruit and bakery development, body and distinctive grain are supported across several accounts. Dry oak, cleaner-like associations in one review and a short, tannic finish in Dramface prevent an excellent or elite whole-sip conclusion. 4 2 5 6 7

History & identity

The name and the identified release

Maker’s Mark’s wheated bourbon is the base for its standard strength, cask-strength and stave-finished ranges. Cellar Aged extends maturation in the limestone cellar, while Star Hill Farm is a separate wheat-whisky project. Shared branding does not transfer a bourbon score to that new category.

Critical evidence

The flavor evidence, with disagreement intact

Editorial synthesis of the credited independent tasting narratives. Producer intent and specifications are separate evidence. Paired channel/article notes count once; inaccessible video transcripts are not inferred.

Nose

Berry fruit, vanilla, baked grain and spice.

Palate

Fruit, custard and bakery notes with good body in favorable accounts; oak dryness remains material.

Finish

Long sweet spice for some, shorter tannic persistence for Dramface.

Breaking Bourbon / Eric Hasman

Admired fruit-and-dessert development and buttery body are curtailed by astringent oak in the finish. 4

Bourbon Culture / Mike & Mike

Reports oily body and lengthy fruit/spice, alongside cleaner-like wood and slight solvent associations. 2

Dramface / Murdo McAtear

Material dissent: spice dominates, the finish is short and tannic, and water weakens the structure. 5

Beverage Testing Institute / Tastings

Blind panel notes support creamy full body and sustained grain, citrus and spice. The medal/grade is not converted. 6

Artfully Bourbon / Scott Oetjen

YouTube-paired written notes find rich fruit, creamy grain and a long warming ending. 7
