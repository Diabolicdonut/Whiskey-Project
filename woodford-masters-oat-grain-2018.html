History & identity

The 2018 Oat Grain bourbon replaces part of the flavoring-grain recipe with oats. Its history and drinking evidence remain separate from the later toasted edition. An individual-bottle price and Value are not authenticated here, so neither is filled for presentation consistency.

How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: oats and texture

Woodford Reserve Master’s Collection Oat Grain (2018) (90.4 proof): Follow grain, nuts and molasses into the chewy oat character. The central question is whether that texture compensates for the raw-grain reservations.

Water: preserve the cereal middle

Woodford Reserve Master’s Collection Oat Grain (2018): At 90.4 proof, taste neat first. A few-drop comparison should retain the sweet nuts and oat body rather than leave the simple grain finish alone.

An oat-release comparison

Woodford Reserve Master’s Collection Oat Grain (2018): Compare small pours with the toasted 2022 edition if available. Keep original grain selection separate from the later oak treatment.

A modest Old Fashioned trial

Woodford Reserve Master’s Collection Oat Grain (2018): Use little syrup and measured bitters so molasses-like sweetness and cereal remain evident. No cocktail test or improved maturity is claimed.
