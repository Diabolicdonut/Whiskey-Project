How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Nelson Brothers Classic Bourbon is 93.3 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: apple, cherry and citrus enrich caramel. The key question is whether that character persists as cake-like sweetness gains light tropical fruit.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 93.3 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is pepper and mint outlast the sweeter notes. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is cake-like sweetness gains light tropical fruit. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 93.3 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Nelson Brothers Classic Bourbon.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from apple, cherry and citrus enrich caramel toward pepper and mint outlast the sweeter notes. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
