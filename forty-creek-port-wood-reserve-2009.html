Exact identity: Forty Creek Port Wood Reserve, first 2009 release / Lot 060, 45% ABV / 90 proof, NAS; separate from 2012 Lot 061.

Background and history

The first Port Wood release followed the 2007 Small Batch and 2008 Double Barrel editions. Dykstra’s December 29, 2009 review explicitly identifies his purchased first edition; the database records Lot 060 and 3,000 bottles.

The current archive reuses a Lot 061 photograph under both Port Wood years. This dossier instead embeds the contemporary first-edition reviewer photograph with visible Lot 060. No later edition’s price or sensory reputation is transferred.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The producer’s archive and a later brand-manager interview describe two-year finishing in vintage port wood for the 2009 and 2012 editions. This is a secondary cask period, not a two-year whole-whisky age. Niagara port-style wine is not proof of Portuguese Port provenance. No exact complete-blend age is authenticated.

Critical review synthesis

Chip Dykstra · The Rum Howler Blog

Dykstra finds evolving berry-and-spice flavors, elegant integration and a lightly oily, sustained finish. He notes day-to-day flavor variation and a small bitter-grape note, while remaining strongly positive.

Scope: December 29, 2009; personally purchased first edition, 45%; sensory sections used without converting his packaging-inclusive score.

MaltMartin · Whiskybase

MaltMartin’s matched bottle develops from ginger and dried fruit into a syrupy, bittersweet palate, followed by a medium-long fruit-and-spice ending. This offers a less extravagant persistence claim.

Scope: August 21, 2016 review attached specifically to 2009 Lot 060 / 45%; pooled rating not used.
