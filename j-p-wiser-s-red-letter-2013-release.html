Background and history

The 2013 reintroduction follows the 2007 original. The plain numbered decanter without the original heavy wooden enclosure provides a useful presentation distinction.

The 2015 rebrand, a separately dated 2014 sample account and later age-stated 2025 Red Letter are not silently merged into a single invariant recipe. Matched 2013-labeled samples anchor this dossier.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The exact 2013 record and sample photographs verify 45%, non-chill filtration and virgin-white-oak finishing. Dykstra’s 2014 review distinguishes its corn-led, silky style from the 2007 original. Critical review synthesis

Chip Dykstra · The Rum Howler Blog

Dykstra finds a silky delivery under assertive orange and wood spice, with persistent chocolate-like richness. Sharp heat remains explicit despite his very favorable conclusion.

Scope: December 24, 2014 exact 2013 release, bottle 0693 / bond 0006075; packaging score excluded.

TOModera · TOModera Whisky Reviews

TOModera finds the 45% 2013 sample gentle and balanced but light on aroma and limited in complexity. Creamy development makes the finish its strongest feature.

Scope: August 5, 2016, sample 1052 / bond 0006075. Separate cask-strength sample in the same article not scored here.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Rest a small neat pour to assess fruit, spice, texture and the drying finish in the published accounts.

Water / rocks

Add drops gradually or one small cube, comparing aroma and texture as it dilutes.

Old Fashioned

Use restrained syrup and bitters. A recurring Canadian blend is the cost comparison before mixing a limited bottle.

Manhattan / highball

Try a small serve with restrained vermouth or soda; a softer whisky can lose definition under assertive mixers.
