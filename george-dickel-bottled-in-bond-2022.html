Serving guidance

Start with a small neat pour and allow it to rest. Add water gradually if alcohol masks the documented fruit or grain. A larger dilution can help compare balance, but it can also weaken an already light texture. A simple highball or spirit-forward cocktail is a practical alternative when the cited profile suits it; the Quality score does not automatically increase because a bottle works as a mixer.
