## Coverage boundaries

Booker’s recurring batches and Little Book chapters/Infinite editions: named researched releases only; no pooled score.
Four Roses: ten recipe identities; specific barrel proofs remain sample-specific; no ten-recipe average.
Jack Daniel’s Distillery Series: numbered programme and independently evidenced named selections; no score inherited by an unreviewed number.
Old Forester Birthday and 117 Series: editions and 375 mL sizes stay distinct.
WhistlePig Boss Hog: I–XIII covered with specific reviewed samples; no guarantee for every barrel.
Private Selection, Reserve and fill-your-own programmes require an authenticated barrel for numeric performance.

Packaging aliases are not new liquids: Knob Creek Trailmaster, commemorative Overholt presentation, Angel’s Envy Volume 3 trilogy, and WhistlePig large-format bottles. Actual sizes remain in priced dossiers.

Adjacent catalogued lines include Jim Beam Apple, Pineapple, Peach, Honey, Black Cherry/Red Stag, Orange, Vanilla and Kentucky Fire; Jack Daniel’s flavored liqueurs and ready-to-drink products; Evan Williams flavored/cream products; Wild Turkey American Honey; and whiskey-brand canned/malt drinks. Their producer catalogues are retained in the source list. They are outside straight whiskey drinking comparisons; a liqueur is not silently scored as its base bourbon.
