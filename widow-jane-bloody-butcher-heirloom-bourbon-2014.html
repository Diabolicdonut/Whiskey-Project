Background and history

The January 2014 founder announcement introduces three separate grain experiments: Wapsie Valley, Bloody Butcher and Bloody Butcher High Rye. They document Widow Jane’s own distillation ambitions alongside its sourced-bourbon business.

This expression is preserved because its documented grain composition is distinct. The announcement’s enthusiastic producer notes and endorsements are not an independent critical consensus.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The founder announcement identifies New York-grown heirloom grains and Brooklyn farm-to-bottle production. Its documented recipe is 85% Organic Bloody Butcher corn; 15% heirloom barley. Neither a complete release-specific label-age record nor exact proof was authenticated; those remain N/A rather than borrowing the modern family blend’s 91 proof.
