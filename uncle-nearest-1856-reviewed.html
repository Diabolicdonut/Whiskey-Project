How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Uncle Nearest 1856 — 100-proof reviewed expression is 100 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: cherry, citrus and brown sugar meet mild spice and leather. The key question is whether that character persists as vanilla gives way to dry, lightly bitter wood.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 100 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is vanilla gives way to dry, lightly bitter wood. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is vanilla gives way to dry, lightly bitter wood. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 100 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Uncle Nearest 1856 — 100-proof reviewed expression.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from cherry, citrus and brown sugar meet mild spice and leather toward vanilla gives way to dry, lightly bitter wood. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
