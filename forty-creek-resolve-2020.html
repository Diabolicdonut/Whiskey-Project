Critical review synthesis

Davin de Kergommeaux · Canadian Whisky

De Kergommeaux finds generous fruit and spice with substantial texture and developed complexity. His review describes the body changing through the sip into a peppery, moderately persistent ending.

Scope: August 13, 2020; exact annual Resolve. His separate Advocate review is the same reviewer, not an independent second vote.

Chip Dykstra · The Rum Howler Blog

Dykstra praises the aroma and flavor range but finds the wine addition in conflict with the whisky. Ice improves the balance, and he favors an Old Fashioned. He allows that personal preference may influence that criticism.

Scope: December 5, 2020; exact 43% Resolve; sensory sections used without mechanically converting packaging points.
