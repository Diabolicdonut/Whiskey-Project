Critical evidence

Critical synthesis

A synthesis of credited critical accounts. Written notes paired with a YouTube episode and that episode count as one voice. Unavailable transcripts are not inferred from titles.

Nose

Pastry sweetness, caramel and nuts, with some fruit and gently toasted wood.

Palate

Creamy, oily or buttery texture leads; vanilla, caramel and nuts are clearer than a complex fruit progression.

Finish

Wheaty warmth and sweet nuts, with light wood or spice.

Evidence limits and disagreement

Tortured Bourbon’s paired episode notes favor its dessert texture; Brian Dawson is also positive. The Right Spirit finds texture stronger than flavor, and Breaking Bourbon finds the French oak and sharp spice slightly off balance, below FAE-01. These contrary accounts remain visible.
