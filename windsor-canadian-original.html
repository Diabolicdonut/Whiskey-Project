The short version

A gentle, fruity, rye-spiced Canadian mixer with modest body and a short, pleasant finish, supported by a detailed historical rather than current-production tasting.

Exact identity: Windsor Canadian Original, standard 80-proof blended Canadian whisky, distinguished from the 93.2-proof Windsor Reserve.

Background and history

Introduced in the 1960s as Windsor Supreme, the brand passed through several corporate owners.

The principal independent tasting was based on a 2011 sample and later rescored; formulation stability through 2026 is not assumed.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The standard 40% Windsor Canadian is a lighter Canadian blend with documented rye influence but no authenticated exact mash percentages. Published historical tasting emphasizes mixed citrus, caramel and spice.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Start neat at room temperature to assess the documented malt/rye character, oak and spirit integration.

Rocks

One large cube can soften alcohol intensity; compare before and after to avoid masking release-specific flavors.

Highball

Use restrained soda and fresh citrus to explore its grain and spice.

Old Fashioned

Use a minimal sugar dose so its distinguishing character remains perceptible.

Manhattan

Try a half-size vermouth build to judge whether the whisky retains sufficient spice and structure.
