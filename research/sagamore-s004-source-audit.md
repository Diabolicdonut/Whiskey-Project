## Exact primary sources and differentiated critic scope

### Standard Double Oak (general current edition)

- Official specifications: https://sagamorespirit.com/spirits/double-oak-rye-whiskey/ — 96.6 proof, 4–5 year starting rye and 18-month toasted wave-stave finish; never report just "4–5 years total."
- Official dated pickup price: https://shop.sagamorespirit.com/products/sagamore-spirit-double-oak — $59 listed, **not NC ABC retail**.
- State control list: https://www.abc.nc.gov/pricing/february-2026-quarterly-price-list/open — item **22-547**, Sagamore Double Oak Rye, 96.6 proof 0.75 L, listed **$59.95** (tax and date handled separately). Retailer stock not established.
- Independent 2026 batch-specific review: https://breakingbourbon.com/review/sagamore-rye-double-oak-batch-9a — Batch 9A is already a protected published dossier; use only as contextual batch differentiation and do not copy its same score/content.
- Independent 2026 Batch 8AP: https://maltrunners.com/2026/06/23/sagamore-rye-double-oak/ — dated reviewer identifies 8AP bottle, 96.6 proof and heavy sweet-oak notes.
- Independent 2024 Batch 8AF: https://secretwhiskeysociety.com/sagamore-spirit-rye-double-oak-review/ — author criticizes heavy oak covering rye and describes one-dimensional balance.
- Independent 2026 general-bottle review: https://openpourwhiskey.com/reviews/sagamore-double-oak-rye/ — reports chocolate, sweet oak, rye-pepper finish and a favorable overall opinion; exact numbered batch not declared.
- **Reviewer disagreement matters:** This should not be scored as the same as Batch 9A, and do not imply all Double Oak is either perfectly balanced or universally too oak-dominant.

### Manhattan Finish — 2024 vs 2025

- Official 2024 release via company announcement: https://www.prweb.com/releases/sagamore-spirit-launches-second-release-of-manhattan-finish-rye-whiskey-302098547.html — March 26, 2024, 103 proof, four-year rye with at least 20-month finish, three separately seasoned barrel types.
- 2024 expert tasting: https://distiller.com/spirits/sagamore-spirit-manhattan-finish-rye-whiskey — expert review labeled second March 2024 release; more drying and tannic than producer marketing would suggest.
- 2024 independent review: https://thedailypour.com/spirit/sagamore-spirit-manhattan-finish-rye-whiskey-2024/ — David Morrow notes cherry, vermouth and considerable baking-spice influence; publisher discloses producer-provided sample. Date and exact 2024 identity match.
- Additional 2024 appraisal with ambiguous "year 2023" details: https://www.bourbonbanter.com/sagamore-spirts-manhattan-finished-rye-whiskey-review/ — June 2024 article; do not silently infer exact bottling year or same SKU from title alone.
- Producer 2025 listing: https://sagamorespirit.com/spirits/manhattan-finish/ — explicitly labeled **2025 limited release** with **30 months** in the three barrel types; 103 proof; not the 2024 batch.
- 2025 exact critic gap: user ratings at https://distiller.com/spirits/sagamore-spirit-manhattan-finish-rye-whiskey/tastes span years without securely confirming which of the 2024/2025 liquid revisions was in each bottle. Avoid inventing a 2025 exact independent tasting score.
- Michigan 2025 market reference: https://www.miramw.org/common/Uploaded%20files/Liquor%20Book/LB2025Q1_web.pdf — a generic Manhattan 103 proof SKU at $79.99 **does not establish it is the 2025 30-month edition**. Do not treat as confirmed 2025 release MSRP.
