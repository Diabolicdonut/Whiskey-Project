Critical synthesis

An earthy, cinnamon-and-wood opening leads to oily corn sweetness and pepper. The finish remains dry and woody rather than supplying a richer payoff. This is a distinctive batch account, but its force and viscosity do not by themselves warrant the premium over stronger-balanced alternatives.
