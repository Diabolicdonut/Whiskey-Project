The short version

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The producer explicitly identifies corn and rye aged for ten years in new white oak, once-used American bourbon and used Canadian-whisky casks. Triple Barrel denotes the three vessel regimes; it does not establish three distillations, three sequential finishes or three separate ten-year periods. The shop’s phrase triple-barrel distilled is not adopted literally, and a retailer’s high-rye wording is not a verified mash percentage.
