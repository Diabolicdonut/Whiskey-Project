Critical review synthesis

Murray Cohen · Scotch Malt Whisky

Cohen describes nut-and-fruit aromatics, a creamy palate, well-developed spice and an enduring earthy finish. He sees polished maturity and judges the numbered 2016 sample very positively.

Scope: November 9, 2016, bottle 303965, 40% ABV.

Davin de Kergommeaux · Whisky Advocate

The 2018 review finds toffee, prune-like fruit and pepper within a clearly woody framework, with crisp grain and restrained tannin. Its 89-point judgment is positive rather than a declaration of reference-class status.

Scope: Summer 2018 twenty-year / 40% ABV; Canada-only distribution noted.

Bryan Vanderkruk · Toronto Whisky Society

Vanderkruk’s 2017 tasting finds alcohol-led sweetness, very thin texture and a short light ending. It is a substantial negative counterpoint to the mature, creamy descriptions elsewhere.

Scope: April 3, 2017 twenty-year / 40% ABV tour sample.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Start neat to determine whether your bottle matches the rich or thin published profile.

Rocks / water

Try a small amount of water before a cube; do not assume maturity compensates for dilution.

Old Fashioned

Use restrained vermouth or syrup so mature wood and grain remain audible in the drink.

Highball

Soda is optional, but the negative texture evidence argues for a low mixer ratio.
