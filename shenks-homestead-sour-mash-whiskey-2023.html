How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: allow the opening to develop

Shenk’s Homestead Sour Mash Whiskey (2023) is 91.2 proof. Give the unusual caramel-and-wood aroma time in the glass. A quick first sniff can miss the creamy leather middle and later honeyed sweetness.

Water: preserve the creaminess

Try neat before a very small comparison addition. At this gentle proof, texture can disappear before the earthy wood becomes any easier to follow.

A Shenk’s year-to-year flight

Compare 2024 at the same 91.2 proof. Keep the two reports separate: the question is how earthiness, sweetness and texture develop within each vintage.

Cocktails: keep the unusual close

A short, lightly sweetened Old Fashioned can frame the honeyed finish. Strong mixers can hide the receding earthiness and creamy character that distinguish this release.
