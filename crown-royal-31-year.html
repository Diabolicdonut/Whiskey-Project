Critical review synthesis

Richard Thomas · The Whiskey Reviewer

Thomas finds subdued aroma but more developed fruit, silky sweetness and herbal/wood nuances on the palate. He considers it a sophisticated pour despite its high cost.

Scope: Exact 31-year / 46% release; article path is November 2024, but current page date furniture is inconsistent. A-.

Christopher Null · Drinkhacker

Null reports toasted-nut and asphalt-like aromas, bitter oak and a strongly industrial ending. Late fruit and nut notes do not resolve his concerns about barrel dominance.

Scope: January 10, 2025; exact 2024 31-year / 92-proof release, B-. The adjacent Reserve 12 and ready-to-drink sour reviews are excluded.
