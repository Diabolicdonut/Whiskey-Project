Critical review synthesis

Geoff Kleinman · Drink Spirits

Kleinman finds an aromatic rye character, a softer entry and a controlled progression toward a dry, spiced finish. He values its approachable style rather than endorsing a universal best-whisky claim.

Scope: February 15, 2016; exact 45% Northern Harvest Rye, USD 29.99 context.

Curt · All Things Whisky

Curt enjoys the aroma but finds sharper wood on the palate and a thin, brief ending. His criticism provides an important check on the more enthusiastic account.

Scope: November 25, 2015; exact 45% Northern Harvest Rye; full sensory criticism is distinguished from award debate.
