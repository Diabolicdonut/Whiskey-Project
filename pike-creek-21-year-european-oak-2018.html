Producer, grains, aging and finishing

Distiller’s exact record describes a blend of corn-whisky streams: 50% ex-bourbon, 25% French oak and 25% Hungarian oak, at twenty-one years and 45%. The shares are attributed technical reporting. Those cask portions do not prove three sequential whole-blend finishes. Corby’s 2019 results confirm the named 2018 European Oak release. The established Canadian Single Grain group is appropriate for the documented corn identity.
