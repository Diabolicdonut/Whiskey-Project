The short version

An unusually mature all-malted-rye Canadian whisky whose published samples emphasize gentle texture, wood and herbal detail. It is a historical one-time release, not the current Collingwood blend.

Exact identity: Collingwood Rye 21 Year Old, one-time November 2013 release from 100% malted rye, 40% ABV / 80 proof.

Background and history

Collingwood announced the one-time release for November 2013 in Ontario and sixteen U.S. markets. The official USD 69.99 / 750 mL MSRP is a dated historical quote.

Eric Burke records a USD 54.99 / 750 mL purchase at Surdyk’s in Minneapolis on December 14, 2013, demonstrating that actual launch-era retail could be below MSRP. Neither quote establishes present stock or collector pricing.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The November 7, 2013 producer announcement authenticates lot 41-06-91, 100% malted rye, 21 years in seasoned oak, followed by resting with toasted maplewood. Independent accounts report the maplewood vat rest as about one year, but the primary announcement does not fix that duration. The age statement remains 21 years; no new 22-year label age is invented.

Critical review synthesis

Eric Burke · Bourbon Guy

Burke repeatedly buys the release and favors its gentle floral, vanilla and maple-like profile. His sample has a delicate tea/mint ending rather than aggressive rye heat.

Scope: 2015 article; exact 21-year / 40% ABV identity and documented December 14, 2013 purchase.

Stephanie Moreno · Distiller

Moreno finds aromatic wood and herb/spice character with a dry palate that avoids harsh tannins. Fruit and floral notes are relatively subdued in her sample.

Scope: Exact 21-year all-malted-rye one-time bottling, 40% ABV; expert score 85.
