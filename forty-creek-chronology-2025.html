Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The November 2025 producer release describes thirty-one-year barley malt, twenty-year corn, ten-year rye and a younger corn component. The inspected front packaging displays 1992, 2000, 2014 and 2020, while the independent review uses calendar-derived ages. These are not substituted for authenticated time in cask: date subtraction alone cannot establish continuous maturation. The final bottle is NAS; exact cask specifications, blend proportions and youngest-component age remain unresolved.

Critical review synthesis

Kurt Bradley · The Gentleman’s Flavor

Bradley finds a successful interplay of mature wood, sweeter components and sustained spice, with a moderately persistent, drying ending. His sample supports a sophisticated blend, but is only one detailed account.
