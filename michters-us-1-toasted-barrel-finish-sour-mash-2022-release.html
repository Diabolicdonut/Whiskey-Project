How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: give apricot room

Michter’s US*1 Toasted Barrel Finish Sour Mash (2022 Release) is 86 proof. Sip without additions first. At this gentle strength, floral notes and dried fruit are easier to lose than the toasted wood.

Water: use a separate trial

If experimenting, add just a few drops to a second pour. Stop when butterscotch and delicate fruit no longer support the toast.

A sour-mash comparison

Compare the ordinary 86-proof Sour Mash at the same temperature. The useful difference is the added wood treatment, rather than assuming toast must improve quality.

Cocktails: keep them short

Use little sugar and modest dilution in a small Old Fashioned. Long drinks and syrup-heavy recipes can hide the delicate middle before the leather-and-spice finish arrives.
