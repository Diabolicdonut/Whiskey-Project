Nose

The source finds port-led raspberry and dried cherry with caramel, floral detail and growing cedar or oak. Orange peel and rye spice become more evident as the wine aroma recedes.

Palate

Wine-rich fruit and dark caramel continue through a moderately sweet middle, with rye spice still discernible. Mild tannin supplies structure rather than being hidden by the finishing sweetness.

Finish

The close becomes tangier, drier and more peppery, with persistent oak and rye spice. The documented long, moderately warm finish keeps the port influence recognizable without claiming that wine removes the wood’s drying effect.
