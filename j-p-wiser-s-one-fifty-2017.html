Background and history

Released nationally in May 2017 for Canada’s Confederation sesquicentennial. Its 7,827 differently dated labels represent weeks, rather than 7,827 different liquid recipes.

This One Fifty is distinct from Canada 2018 and the 2007 Red Letter distillery-anniversary release. A stray Double Rye phrase in a review is not adopted as its expression name.

Critical review synthesis

TOModera · TOModera Whisky Reviews

TOModera finds some aromatic growth with air but excessive sweetness and a late arrival of spice. A rough cardboard-like note weakens the finish.

Scope: July 21, 2017 supplied TWS bottle 7793 / 43.4%; sensory cardboard is not a material contamination finding.

talexander · Whisky Connosr

The reviewer enjoys a creamy sweet/spicy profile but finds the spice forceful and the sweetness nearly cloying, with a medium finish.

Scope: June 29, 2017 exact One Fifty / 43.4%.

WhiskyNeighbour · Whisky Neighbour

The written account is more favorable, praising fullness, a longer ending and balanced oak/spice; its collecting-uniqueness bonus is excluded from Quality.

Scope: 2017 written review; a correction acknowledges new oak. Producer’s two-cask account remains the production authority.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Rest a small neat pour to assess fruit, spice, texture and the drying finish in the published accounts.

Water / rocks

Add drops gradually or one small cube, comparing aroma and texture as it dilutes.

Old Fashioned

Use restrained syrup and bitters. A recurring Canadian blend is the cost comparison before mixing a limited bottle.

Manhattan / highball

Try a small serve with restrained vermouth or soda; a softer whisky can lose definition under assertive mixers.
