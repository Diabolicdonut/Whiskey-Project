Background and history

Sagamore Spirit revived the Maryland-style rye tradition, using blends of two rye mash bill profiles and gradually transitioning from sourced to Maryland-distilled stock.

Sagamore 2024 Bottled-in-Bond Straight Rye, 7-year, distilled fall 2017, 100 proof, distinct from earlier 5-/6-year and later 8-year releases.

Sagamore's distillery describes a fall-2017 Maryland distillation, aging for seven years and 100 proof bottled-in-bond rye made from a high-rye and a low-rye mash bill; source identity not the same as 5-year review.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Best starting point: explore dessert aromatics and whether the tannic finish is balanced.

Rocks

One large cube can reduce drying heat but may suppress lighter fruit notes.

Old Fashioned

A restrained Old Fashioned should preserve bonded rye structure and sweet spice.

Manhattan

A rye-forward Manhattan complements cocoa/cherry notes; use moderate vermouth sweetness.

Highball

A tall rye highball is plausible, but this relatively mature/limited bottle is most informative neat before mixing.
