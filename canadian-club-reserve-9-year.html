Critical review synthesis

Stephanie Moreno · Distiller

Moreno finds a harsh aroma, straightforward sweet rye/vanilla flavors and a tingling finish. Limited complexity leads her to prefer mixed drinks.

Scope: Exact Reserve 9 / 40% ABV named review, 75 points.

Josh · Sipology

The direct tasting finds substantial body, caramel and assertive spice, preferring Reserve 9 to the thinner Classic 12. It recommends the expression while wishing for more bottling strength.

Scope: April 18, 2014 exact nine-year / 40% ABV comparison; sensory observations are not borrowed from Reserve 10.
