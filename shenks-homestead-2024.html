How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: give the cocoa detail time

Shenk’s Homestead Sour Mash — 2024 is 91.2 proof. Let marshmallow and graham-like sweetness develop through tobacco and cherry. The interest is the amount of flavor retained at this relatively gentle strength.

Water: stop before the sweetness fades

Try only a few drops in a comparison sample. Cocoa and dry wood can seem more bitter if the supporting caramel thins.

A 91.2-proof Shenk’s flight

Compare 2023 at equal temperature and small pour size. The newer account’s chocolate richness should be heard beside the earlier creamy earthiness, not used to rewrite it.

Old Fashioned: start with little sugar

Dessert-like aromas do not require dessert-level syrup. Keep dilution and bitters modest so cherry, citrus and cocoa stay distinct.
