Background

The first Millennium edition is a 99-proof blend with disclosed component vintages, rather than a single age-stated Weller bourbon. The 2024 evidence describes rich mature aromas with a less uniformly compelling whole sip; presentation and collector demand do not enter its drinking Quality.

The name, the era and the limits of the story

The Weller name honors William Larue Weller and the brand’s historic association with wheated bourbon. Current Weller whiskey is made at Buffalo Trace in Frankfort, Kentucky. A historic connection to Stitzel-Weller is not evidence that a modern bottle contains old Stitzel-Weller stock. 1

Specifications without invented precision

The first blend uses barrels distilled in 2000, 2003, 2005 and 2006, at disclosed shares of 3%, 50%, 40% and 7%. Those shares describe vintage, not the bourbon-versus-wheat proportion. Distillation years do not establish bottling age without complete maturation and storage dates. 2

Published percentages are reproduced only where disclosed. A distiller’s address, a retailer’s style field, an award entry and the actual bottle label answer different questions; unresolved differences are stated in the scope note rather than combined into a fictional recipe.

Vintage component percentages describe when whiskey was distilled, not how much corn or wheat is in the total recipe. Complete cask / tank storage history and bourbon-versus-wheat proportions are not published, so no average age or blended grain percentage is calculated.

How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: aroma versus full sip

Weller Millennium (2024) (99 proof): Use a small room-temperature pour. Follow the layered mature sweets beyond the nose to test whether drying wood reduces the vividness of the middle and finish.

Water: a separate experiment

Weller Millennium (2024): At 99 proof there is no automatic dilution need. Compare only a few drops and stop if the composed fruit gives way to an increasingly wood-led ending.

A release-specific flight

Weller Millennium (2024): Keep the 2024 and 2025 samples separately identified. Differences in development can be informative, but two editions are not a controlled test of every component.

Avoid mixing away the evidence
