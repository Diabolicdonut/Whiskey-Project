Background and history

The Vaults 2020 belongs to the annual old-and-rare series begun in 2019. Its documented finishing wood and minimum age define this edition, independently of handwritten ordinary batch numbers.

This 2020 release uses appalachian oak finishing casks; five-year air-seasoned staves. Wood-air-seasoning time describes the staves before barrel assembly; it is not extra years of whiskey age.

Critical review synthesis

Michael Veach · Bourbon Veach

Veach describes caramel, dried fruit, tobacco and baking spice, then a long dry oak/spice ending with only limited tannic bitterness. He recommends time in the glass.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Start neat after a short rest to inspect the year-specific mature wood and sweetness.

Rocks / water

Test drops before a large cube; the documented finish/wood balance matters more than the proof alone.

Old Fashioned

Use a small, low-syrup trial if mixing; the finishing treatment deserves a neat assessment first.

Manhattan

Try a restrained vermouth measure so the older wood and finish retain their distinction.
