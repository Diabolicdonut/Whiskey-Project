The short version

An approachable, lightly sweet Canadian blend whose modest complexity suits ice and highballs. The recurring identity is distinct from Reserve and limited age-stated editions.

Exact identity: Crown Royal Fine De Luxe / Deluxe, the recurring 40% ABV / 80-proof Canadian blend.

Background and history

The brand originated in 1939 for the Canadian royal tour. Dykstra records U.S. availability from 1964. This is brand chronology, not a claim that today’s blend is unchanged since 1939.

The current NC entry explicitly named Deluxe is the 1 L bottle at USD 34.95, code 37-885. Its 750 mL cost equivalent is USD 26.21; displayed package size remains 1 L. Gift boxes and other sizes are not separate recipes.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Critical review synthesis

Chip Dykstra · The Rum Howler Blog

Dykstra finds appealing sweet/spicy balance and a soft mouthfeel, but a short ending with some heat. He most often prefers it with ice, ginger ale or cola.

Scope: February 4, 2016; exact De Luxe 40% sample; explicitly compares its character with earlier versions.

Richard Thomas · The Whiskey Reviewer

Thomas finds a pleasant, fairly simple blend with light sweetness, modest oak and rye accents. He regards it as competent for neat drinking and practical mixing.

Scope: January 2014 article; flagship Crown Royal at 40%, not Reserve.

An editorial interpretation of attributed published samples, not a first-person tasting. Formulation, sample era and single-barrel variation limit generalization; reviewer grades are not mechanically converted to this project’s scale.
