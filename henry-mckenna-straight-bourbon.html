How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Henry McKenna Straight Bourbon is 80 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: faint vanilla and caramel. The key question is whether that character persists as light pecan and oak with a thin body.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 80 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is a gentle char-and-leather close. Stop early if the already gentle body becomes less distinct.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is light pecan and oak with a thin body. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 80 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Henry McKenna Straight Bourbon.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from faint vanilla and caramel toward a gentle char-and-leather close. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
