How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Michter’s US*1 Single Barrel Straight Rye is 84.8 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: cherry and apple brighten caramel. The key question is whether that character persists as cinnamon-like sweetness gains gentle body.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 84.8 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is gingerbread yields to increasingly dry oak and leather. Stop early if the already gentle body becomes less distinct.

Manhattan: let the grain lead

Michter’s US*1 Single Barrel Straight Rye brings cinnamon-like sweetness gains gentle body. Start with restrained sweet vermouth and bitters; the stated strength calls for a light hand with vermouth so the grain character remains visible.

Old Fashioned or a small highball

Use little sugar first: cherry and apple brighten caramel already provides part of the sweet impression. A short soda highball is the gentler option; too much water can erase 84.8 proof.
