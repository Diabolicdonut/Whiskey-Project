The short version

An interesting experiment whose grain and wood do not integrate consistently.

Breaking Bourbon and Bourbon Culture find an unfocused profile, including raw or damp grain and cardboard-like wood alongside occasional fruit and chocolate. Below-average bourbon near the weaker conventional budget samples fits the lack of integration, far above Cleveland’s catastrophic defect pattern. Novel smoke treatment does not earn extra points.

At the original $100 reference, the inconsistent profile is extremely poor approaching poor value against ordinary Woodford and stronger cheaper bourbon. The historical experiment may interest collectors, but that does not improve drinking Value.

Scope: Identified 2017 Master’s Collection release. Current catalog chronology does not prove this older edition is still in production or available at original MSRP. 1

Production

Specifications without invented precision
