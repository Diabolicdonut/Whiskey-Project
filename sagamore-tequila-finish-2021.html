Critical synthesis

Bourbon Obsessed finds honeyed sweetness quickly giving way to pepper, with subtle tequila and citrus through the close. Its reservation is that the treatment subdues both sides rather than producing stronger integration. That is a sensory judgment, not a chemical neutralization claim.
