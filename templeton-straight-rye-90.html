How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Templeton Straight Rye — 90-proof formulation is 90 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: honey, pear and tropical fruit meet licorice and toasted oak. The key question is whether that character persists as grapefruit peel and earthy rye grains taper gently.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 90 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is grapefruit peel and earthy rye grains taper gently. Stop early if the already gentle body becomes less distinct.

Manhattan: let the grain lead

Templeton Straight Rye — 90-proof formulation brings grapefruit peel and earthy rye grains taper gently. Start with restrained sweet vermouth and bitters; the stated strength calls for a light hand with vermouth so the grain character remains visible.

Old Fashioned or a small highball

Use little sugar first: honey, pear and tropical fruit meet licorice and toasted oak already provides part of the sweet impression. A short soda highball is the gentler option; too much water can erase 90 proof.
