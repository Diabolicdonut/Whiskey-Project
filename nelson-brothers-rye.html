How to approach this expression

Nelson Brothers Rye is 92.5 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: flowers and orange peel introduce mint, pepper and sandalwood. The key question is whether that character persists as spearmint and clove carry a medium, lightly bitter wood finish.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 92.5 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is spearmint and clove carry a medium, lightly bitter wood finish. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Nelson Brothers Rye brings spearmint and clove carry a medium, lightly bitter wood finish. Start with restrained sweet vermouth and bitters; the stated strength calls for a light hand with vermouth so the grain character remains visible.
