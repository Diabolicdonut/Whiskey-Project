Critical review synthesis

Davin de Kergommeaux · Canadian Whisky

De Kergommeaux finds mature wood supporting an active, varied grain profile, with substantial spice and a moderately persistent dry ending. His strong recommendation does not establish a native-category maximum.

Scope: July 13, 2013, explicit Rare 18 / 40%, Valleyfield-era account.

Blair Phillips · Distiller

Phillips describes a coherent progression from fruit and vanilla toward assertive oak and citrus dryness, with wood providing the continuing framework.

Scope: Exact Rare 18 / 40% specialist review; Venerable is not substituted.

Jason Debly · Jason’s Scotch Whisky Reviews

Debly praises the inviting delivery and sustained transition from sweetness to dryness, while finding the nose restrained. His favorable conclusion is more measured on aromatic complexity.

Scope: June 14, 2010, historical Rare 18 review.
