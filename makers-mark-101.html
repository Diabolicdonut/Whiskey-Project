The short version

An accessible strength increase, still limited by tannin and texture.

Bourbon Obsessed finds fruit followed by oak and pepper; Bourbon Culture finds an enjoyable but somewhat thin and tannic bourbon. Good bourbon above the standard Maker’s 5.0 midpoint fits the added presence. Maker’s 46 and the stronger cask-strength samples offer more developed texture and character.

At $39.95 NC, the strength and familiar style support good value. Maker’s 46 costs the same with stronger reviewed quality, and Cask Strength costs $10 more; both constrain the buying case.

History & identity

The name and the identified release

Maker’s Mark’s wheated bourbon is the base for its standard strength, cask-strength and stave-finished ranges. Cellar Aged extends maturation in the limestone cellar, while Star Hill Farm is a separate wheat-whisky project. Shared branding does not transfer a bourbon score to that new category.

Production

Specifications without invented precision

The same corn, soft red winter wheat and malted barley recipe is matured before bottling at 101 proof. No finishing staves are added.

Recipe: C70 W16 MB14. Age: NAS. Strength: 101 proof / 50.5% ABV. 1

C corn · R rye · W wheat · MB malted barley. Undisclosed proportions are not estimated from neighboring labels. A component’s oldest age is not the age of a blend. Proof is not a quality multiplier.
