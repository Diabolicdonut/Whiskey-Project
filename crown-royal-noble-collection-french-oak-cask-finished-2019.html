The short version

A French-oak-treated version of Fine De Luxe with conflicting independent outcomes. The documented two-year seasoning concerns the wood, not a two-year whisky finish.

Exact identity: Crown Royal Noble Collection French Oak Cask Finished, fourth annual edition, 2019, 40% ABV / 80 proof, NAS.

Background and history

The producer announced the fourth Noble Collection release on June 6, 2019, at USD 59.99 / 750 mL. It is a distinct cask-treated release, not an age-stated replacement for Fine De Luxe.

Substantive reviews range from rich and persistent to underdeveloped and grassy. The production novelty alone does not settle the quality assessment.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The June 2019 producer release specifies new French oak from the Vosges, open-air seasoned for two years before a two-step fire toast. Fine De Luxe then receives the secondary treatment. The source does not publish the time the whisky spent in those casks or a numeric age.

Critical review synthesis

Christopher Null · Drinkhacker

Null finds a cereal-heavy, underdeveloped profile with little useful oak transformation and limited aromatic interest.

Scope: November 12, 2019; exact French Oak Cask Finished at 80 proof.

Bobby · Adventures in Whiskey

Bobby finds a pleasant increase in spice and dark sweetness, but less transformation than expected; he calls it nice rather than compelling.

Scope: September 14, 2019; producer-provided exact 40% sample.

WhiskyCast · WhiskyCast

The June tasting is much more positive, finding substantial texture, layered dark fruit and a persistent spiced ending.

Scope: June 2019; exact fourth Noble Collection edition; full source page inspected directly after web retrieval errors.
