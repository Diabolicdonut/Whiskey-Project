Background and history

The Vaults 2025 belongs to the annual old-and-rare series begun in 2019. Its documented finishing wood and minimum age define this edition, independently of handwritten ordinary batch numbers.

This 2025 release uses greek oak harvested near amfilochia; one-to-two-year air-seasoned wood. Wood-air-seasoning time describes the staves before barrel assembly; it is not extra years of whiskey age.

Critical review synthesis

Jordan Moskal · Breaking Bourbon

Moskal describes an unusual musty/fruity aroma, then an old-oak-led palate with berry and brown sugar. The ending is dry, mature and only subtly sweet/spicy; he recommends tasting before purchase.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Rocks / water

Old Fashioned

Use a small, low-syrup trial if mixing; the finishing treatment deserves a neat assessment first.

Manhattan

Try a restrained vermouth measure so the older wood and finish retain their distinction.
