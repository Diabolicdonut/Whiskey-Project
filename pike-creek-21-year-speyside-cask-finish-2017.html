Critical review synthesis

Mark Bylok · Whisky Buzz

The rich sweet/spicy palate has a buttery, persistent ending, but initially becomes cloying. Air improves its balance.

Scope: November 2017 exact 21-year Speyside bottle; brand background speculation about direct port addition does not apply here.

Selfbuilt · Selfbuilt’s Whisky Analysis

The bottle is clean, gently balanced and somewhat buttery, with a medium ending; its subtle hints never develop a sharply defined character.

Scope: November 9, 2017 exact 45% Speyside; differs from the very-long-finish account rather than being silently reconciled.
