Background and history

The Vaults 2024 belongs to the annual old-and-rare series begun in 2019. Its documented finishing wood and minimum age define this edition, independently of handwritten ordinary batch numbers.

This 2024 release uses amburana finishing casks. Wood-air-seasoning time describes the staves before barrel assembly; it is not extra years of whiskey age.

Critical review synthesis

Eric Hasman · Breaking Bourbon

Hasman describes cola and confectionery aromas, restrained cinnamon/raisin flavor, and an unusually understated herbal/nutty finish over mature oak. He cautions against expecting a showpiece.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Rocks / water

Old Fashioned
