The short version

Unusually mature all-rye Canadian whisky with deep spice and supple oak in its best showings, complicated by conflicting evidence about solvent-like off-notes and low-proof texture.

Exact identity: Alberta Premium 20 Year, 2019 limited 42% ABV release, not the 25/30-year editions or 2019 Cask Strength edition.

Background and history

The limited 20-year release was marketed in 2019 alongside the separate first Alberta Premium Cask Strength edition.

The 20-year expression is not the brand's historically older 25- or 30-year standard bottles.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The 2019 limited edition was produced from 100% Canadian rye matured for 20 years, bottled at 42% ABV. The launch circulated a CAD $89.95/750 mL price which is not a current USD value reference.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Start neat at room temperature to assess the documented malt/rye character, oak and spirit integration.

Rocks

One large cube can soften alcohol intensity; compare before and after to avoid masking release-specific flavors.

Highball

Use restrained soda and fresh citrus to explore its grain and spice.

Old Fashioned

Use a minimal sugar dose so its distinguishing character remains perceptible.

Manhattan

Try a half-size vermouth build to judge whether the whisky retains sufficient spice and structure.
