How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

1792 Full Proof (2023 Release) is 125 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: dark caramel, oak and char. The key question is whether that character persists as dense sweetness with nuts and modest fruit.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 125 proof, add a few drops to test whether the documented warmth releases more flavor rather than simply flattening it. The relevant finishing behavior is a hot, dry, tannic close. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is dense sweetness with nuts and modest fruit. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. The extra proof leaves room for ice dilution, while bitters can frame the spice.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from dark caramel, oak and char toward a hot, dry, tannic close. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
