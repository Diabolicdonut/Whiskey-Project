How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

1792 Small Batch is 93.7 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: caramel and vanilla with rye spice. The key question is whether that character persists as substantial sweetness with uneven heat.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 93.7 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is a straightforward spicy ending. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is substantial sweetness with uneven heat. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 93.7 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of 1792 Small Batch.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from caramel and vanilla with rye spice toward a straightforward spicy ending. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
