History and line identity

The July 2024 117 Rum Finish is one of Old Forester’s limited finishing experiments. Its appealing rum-like opening does not continue unchanged through the palate. The dossier judges that development rather than treating an unusual barrel as an automatic improvement.

Production and identity

Bourbon spends approximately six months in casks that previously held rum for at least four years, then is bottled at 95 proof. Prior rum maturation is not bourbon age. The base 72/18/10 recipe remains distinct from the added cask influence.
