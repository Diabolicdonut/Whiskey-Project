The short version

An accessible Valleyfield single malt with differing judgments about depth. It is distinct from the Noble Barley Edition and receives Canadian single-malt calibration.

Exact identity: Crown Royal Single Malt Canadian Whisky, introduced June 2024, 100% malted barley, 45% ABV / 90 proof, NAS.

Background and history

The June 4, 2024 producer announcement introduces the brand’s first marketed single malt. This does not claim that no Canadian single malt existed earlier, or that the earlier Barley Edition was a single malt.

The exact August–October 2026 NC listing is 90 proof, 750 mL, USD 59.95, code 18-266. The production location is Valleyfield; Gimli is not substituted from other Crown Royal expressions.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The June 2024 producer release identifies Valleyfield Distillery, Quebec, and copper distillation. De Kergommeaux’s exact-release account reports a short copper column, approximate 66% distillation strength and new white-oak maturation. Those technical particulars remain attributed; other reviewers’ guesses about reused Crown barrels are not promoted to facts. No numeric age or separate finish is authenticated.

Critical review synthesis

Christopher Null · Drinkhacker

Null finds useful texture and a gentle toasty, nutty and fruity progression, though the overall experience remains easygoing rather than especially demanding.

Scope: August 26, 2024; exact NAS single malt at 90 proof; unconfirmed cask speculation excluded.

Richard Thomas · The Whiskey Reviewer

Thomas finds a pleasant creamy palate but a faint aroma and limited definition. He considers it average for the class, and his production guesses are not treated as verified specifications.

Scope: July 2024; exact 90-proof Crown Royal Single Malt, tested neat and on ice.

Davin de Kergommeaux · Canadian Whisky

De Kergommeaux finds much greater layering and integration, with grain, fruit, spice and restrained wood working together; water reveals more detail.

Scope: June 18, 2024; exact Valleyfield single malt; full technical and sensory account.
