How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: test the caramel support

Old Forester The 117 Series (Batch No. 001) is 110 proof. Follow creamy sweetness beneath tobacco and old wood. Its oak-led identity succeeds only while those supporting flavors remain distinct.

Water: compare richness and dryness

Try drops in a second sample. Stop when caramel becomes thinner but dry leather does not soften.

An Old Forester flight

Compare Warehouse K Batch 002 at the same 110 proof. This isolates two wood-and-spice balances within the same series more usefully than a proof contest.

Rocks: keep the tobacco visible

Use a large cube cautiously. Cold may mute creamy caramel and make the dry ending harder-edged; retain a neat pour to judge the tradeoff.
