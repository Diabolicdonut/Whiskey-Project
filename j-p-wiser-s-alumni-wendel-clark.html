Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The producer photograph states 41.6%, eleven years and 100% rye. The shop prose confirms bourbon-cask maturation; its rounded 42% specification does not authenticate a second strength. Selfbuilt describes a predominantly column-distilled bourbon-cask component plus some column/pot-distilled rye in charred virgin oak. Toronto Whisky Society’s listed proportions and narrative do not agree; exact ratios are therefore withheld. Corn-like flavor is not proof of corn grain.
