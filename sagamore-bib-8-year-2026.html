Background and history

Sagamore Spirit revived the Maryland-style rye tradition, using blends of two rye mash bill profiles and gradually transitioning from sourced to Maryland-distilled stock.

September 1, 2026 annual 8-year Bottled-in-Bond Straight Rye, distilled spring 2018, Maryland-made, 100 proof; not the branded Ravens commemorative packaging nor 2024 7-year BiB.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The official 2026 release is spring-2018 distilled, aged eight years, blended from high- and low-rye mash bills, triple distilled, bottled at 100 proof and non-chill filtered. Independent exact-release review supports a bright, friendly sensory profile.

Serving recommendations

Neat

The best first test for fruit, floral spice, and surprising creamy progression.

Rocks

Use a little dilution only after tasting neat; the fruit may become quieter.

Old Fashioned

Excellent plausible fit for a restrained Old Fashioned—pepper and cherry should remain visible.

Manhattan

Balanced spice and approachable proof support a classic Manhattan with limited sweet vermouth.

Highball

A tall rye highball is plausible, but this relatively mature/limited bottle is most informative neat before mixing.
