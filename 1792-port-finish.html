How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

1792 Port Finish is 88.9 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: apple, nuts and spice. The key question is whether that character persists as textured red-fruit sweetness.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 88.9 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is nutty caramel persists. Stop early if the already gentle body becomes less distinct.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is textured red-fruit sweetness. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 88.9 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of 1792 Port Finish.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from apple, nuts and spice toward nutty caramel persists. For a Sour, keep syrup modest so the cask-derived sweetness is not doubled.
