How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Johnny Drum Private Stock Bourbon is 101 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: fruit and vanilla over oak. The key question is whether that character persists as red fruit, honey and caramel.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 101 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is warm apple-pastry flavors resolve cleanly. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is red fruit, honey and caramel. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 101 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Johnny Drum Private Stock Bourbon.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from fruit and vanilla over oak toward warm apple-pastry flavors resolve cleanly. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
