Critical review synthesis

Christopher Null · Drinkhacker

Null finds the peach-candy character overpowering, with almost no discernible whisky. He sees a sweet mixed-drink use rather than satisfying neat-whisky depth.

Scope: May 14, 2019; exact 70-proof Peach sample; original C judgment.

Alex · Intoxicology

Alex instead enjoys the pronounced fruit flavor and softened whisky profile, especially over ice and in iced tea. His account supports a sweet-drink audience, not a general critical consensus.
