The short version

An inexpensive green-apple candy style intended for shots and mixing. A substantive panel record supports competent flavored-whiskey execution, with little basis for a complex neat-sipping claim.

Exact identity: Black Velvet Apple, original Canadian whisky with natural apple flavor, 35% ABV / 70 proof; launched February 2021.

Background and history

Heaven Hill’s acquisition closed in November 2019. Apple followed as a February 2021 flavor extension, joining Toasted Caramel.

The original discovery source was a caramel retailer page. Direct producer documentation and a matched 2023 panel tasting authenticate Apple independently; the ledger is corrected without changing its order.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Critical review synthesis

Beverage Testing Institute panel · Tastings

The panel describes confectionery green-apple sweetness, tang and a syrupy medium body, with a lingering candy-like ending. Its Bronze/Recommended judgment supports the intended flavored mixer rather than subtle mature whisky.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Use a small tasting pour to check tolerance for the candy-like sweetness.

Rocks / water

Serve cold or over a cube if the syrupy texture is too prominent neat.

Old Fashioned

Use it as a small flavored portion alongside ordinary whisky and omit syrup initially.

Highball

Ginger ale and lime work as a starting point; adjust mixer sweetness downward.

Serving suggestions are editorial starting points, grounded in documented strength and style; adjust to your taste.
