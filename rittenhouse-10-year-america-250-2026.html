How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the darker fruit

Rittenhouse 10 Year Bottled-in-Bond — America 250 (2026) is 100 proof. Allow mint and citrus to open into the deeper sweet rye middle. The attraction is balanced development, rather than barrel-proof force.

Water: preserve the composed finish

Compare a few drops only after tasting neat. Stop before the fruit and caramel lose definition against the gentle spice.

Manhattan: use less vermouth first

The corn-rich recipe already supplies rounded sweetness. A modest vermouth dose keeps the aged rye and darker fruit evident.

Rocks: retain the herbal opening

Use one large cube in a separate pour. Cold may make the bright mint and citrus harder to follow, so keep a room-temperature reference.
