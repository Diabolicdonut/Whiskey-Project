Critical review synthesis

Selfbuilt · Selfbuilt’s Whisky Analysis

Selfbuilt finds good balance and a buttery mouthfeel, with fruit and baking spice continuing into a medium-long ending. A modest nose, slight acetone-like note and a persistent tea-like tannin prevent his highest assessment.

Scope: July 29, 2020, exact fourteen-year / 45%; meta-review grades are not extra independent tastings.

The Gentleman’s Flavor panel · The Gentleman’s Flavor

The panel finds layered orchard fruit, pepper and soft char across a coherent palate, followed by a genuinely medium, warming finish. Its favorable balance account supports the sipper emphasis.

Scope: Exact fourteen-year / 45% Keon tasting; Scotch-like description is a flavor analogy, not a change of category.
