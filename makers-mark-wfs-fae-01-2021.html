Critical evidence

Critical synthesis

A synthesis of credited critical accounts. Written notes paired with a YouTube episode and that episode count as one voice. Unavailable transcripts are not inferred from titles.

Nose

Toasted oak, caramel and dark fruit, with warehouse and tobacco impressions.

Palate

Brown sugar, cherry and wheated sweetness, with a viscous texture and prominent oak or heat.

Finish

Fruit and warm spice persist, though heat and wood limit ease and development.

Evidence limits and disagreement

Carin Moonin finds a vivid fruit-led result; Brian Dawson describes a simpler, more wood-centered sip. The source’s printed 2012 review date conflicts with its own March 2021 release identification and is treated as a publication typo.
