Evidence limits and disagreement

VinePair finds a composed rye despite initially lean texture and tannin. Drinkhacker favors its bread, herb and orange character. Bourbon Culture likes its creamy botanical profile but finds the finish short-to-moderate. These are three independent 126-proof accounts, not an inherited score from the standard rye.
