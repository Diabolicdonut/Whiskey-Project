The short version

A mature corn-forward Canadian expression with persuasive fruit, spice and texture evidence. It is separately authenticated from the NAS eighty-proof Blenders’ Series bottle.

Exact identity: Crown Royal Noble Collection 13 Year Old Blenders’ Mash, the third annual edition, 2018, 45% ABV / 90 proof.

Background and history

The producer announced the third Noble Collection edition on June 27, 2018, at USD 59.99 / 750 mL. Its age, strength and recipe distinguish it from the NAS Blenders’ Mash and its earlier naming controversy.

Burke documents an August 2018 Minnesota purchase at USD 57.99 / 750 mL. Bobby’s later approximate USD 100 context is preserved as his price objection, not substituted for the authenticated national launch MSRP.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The June 2018 producer announcement confirms the exact 60/36/4 recipe and at least thirteen years in new charred American white oak. That differs from the reported recipe of the NAS expression and Hand Selected Barrel; their ratios are not transferred. It is Canadian whisky, and this dossier uses the Canadian parent comparison group.
