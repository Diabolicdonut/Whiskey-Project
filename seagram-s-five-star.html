Critical review synthesis

Chip Dykstra · The Rum Howler Blog

Finds an increasingly sweet, sour and pithy flavor profile, coarse astringency and a burning finish. Ginger ale does little to rescue it and cola only partly helps; this is a clear unfavorable sensory account, not simply a low award grade.

Scope: August 20, 2013; one Diageo-era bottle. Current recipe continuity is not demonstrated.
