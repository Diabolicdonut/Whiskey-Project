How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Redwood Empire Pipe Dream Bourbon is 90 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: maple and caramel. The key question is whether that character persists as nutty butter and toasted grain.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 90 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is dry oak, pepper and a little strawberry. Stop early if the already gentle body becomes less distinct.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is nutty butter and toasted grain. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 90 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Redwood Empire Pipe Dream Bourbon.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from maple and caramel toward dry oak, pepper and a little strawberry. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
