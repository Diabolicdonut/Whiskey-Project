Critical review synthesis

Stephen Beaumont · Beaumont Drinks

Beaumont finds an attractive chocolate-and-fruit aroma followed by a coherent transition from sweet entry to a dry, tannic ending. He considers the robust Canadian style very successful.

Scope: November 19, 2025; exact 51% Triple Reserve section; producer-supplied product disclosed.

Patrick · Québec Whisky

Patrick praises aroma and palate but finds the fruit and spice disappear too soon, leaving oak to dominate the long ending.

Scope: September 5, 2026; exact Niagara Triple Reserve / 51%.

Martin and Kim · Québec Whisky

Martin finds strong wine influence compatible with the body and richness. Kim’s blind sample offers interesting savory impressions but loses coherence at the finish. Those impressions do not authenticate smoke processing.

Scope: Two further accounts on the same page, including explicitly blind Kim sample; one publisher.
