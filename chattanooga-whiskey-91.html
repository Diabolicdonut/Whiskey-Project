How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Chattanooga Whiskey 91 is 91 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: malt bread, cherry and oak. The key question is whether that character persists as brown sugar and vanilla over toasted grain.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 91 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is malt, leather and pepper end dry. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is brown sugar and vanilla over toasted grain. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 91 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Chattanooga Whiskey 91.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from malt bread, cherry and oak toward malt, leather and pepper end dry. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
