Critical review synthesis

Eric Hasman · Breaking Bourbon

Hasman finds expressive fruit, well-managed oak and an elegant palate. He regards the ending as somewhat thin and less developed, while still strongly favoring the original purchase proposition.

Scope: March 30, 2021; exact 16-year / 90-proof producer-provided sample; original U.S. MSRP context.

Jerry Jenae Sampson · The Whiskey Wash

Sampson finds mature fruit, gentle spice and a more substantial texture than standard Crown Royal. Her positive account accepts its approachable strength.

Scope: January 5, 2021; exact 16-year / 90-proof edition, USD 70 / 750 mL.
