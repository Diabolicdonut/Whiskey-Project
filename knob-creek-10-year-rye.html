Nose

The aroma is gentle for the strength, moving through grain, sweet corn, herbs and mint toward caramel and orange.

Palate

Caramel and char introduce a wood-and-spice middle. The restrained entrance develops into a more distinctly rye-led sip.

Finish

Dry oak joins coffee-like notes, cinnamon and tobacco. Spice and wood have greater persistence than the quiet opening suggests.
