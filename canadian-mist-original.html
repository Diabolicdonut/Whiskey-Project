The short version

An inexpensive Canadian mixer with sharply divided reviews. Some find clean grain, fruit and spice; others report an industrial aroma, bitterness or a very brief palate. The low price does not settle that disagreement.

Exact identity: Canadian Mist Original blended Canadian whisky, recurring 40% ABV / 80-proof expression; excludes Black Diamond and size-only packaging variants.

Background and history

The brand dates to 1967; Brown-Forman later owned it, and the sale of Canadian Mist, Collingwood and their production assets to Sazerac closed July 31, 2020. Older sources naming Brown-Forman are historical, not current ownership evidence.

Critical review synthesis

Stephanie Moreno · Distiller

Moreno finds a grain/nut-led profile that disappears quickly, leaving little development beyond husk-like and oak-spice impressions.

Scope: Exact 40% ABV Original expert tasting; 70 points. The page’s Brown-Forman ownership line is outdated.

Nick Leghorn · Thirty-One Whiskey

Leghorn reports an acrid rubber-like aroma and bitterness with ice. Fruit appears neat and works in a mule, but he dislikes the overall experience and finds the Old Fashioned bland.

Scope: November 28, 2023; NAS Original at 40% ABV, USD 9.99 / 750 mL; sensory defects are his sample judgment, not a universal batch fact.

Davin de Kergommeaux · Canadian Whisky

De Kergommeaux finds a clean, youthful blend with citrus, sweetness and rye spice, valuing it as a mixer and suggesting a neat trial. His favorable reading conflicts materially with the later negative sample.

Scope: Historical Original 40% ABV review; the migrated page contains older ownership and product-range statements that are not reused as current facts.

Serving recommendations

Neat

A small neat pour can reveal whether your bottle resembles the favorable or harsh review.

Rocks / water

Try a little ice first; the 2023 review found bitterness worse on ice.

Old Fashioned / Manhattan

Expect light definition; test one drink before committing to a full bottle for this use.

Highball

A ginger mule or simple mixed drink is the most defensible use in the matched evidence.
