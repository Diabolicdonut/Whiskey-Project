The short version

Big flavor for little money, with heat and a modest finish.

The matched reviews describe stronger caramel, fruit and spice, but also heat and a finish that does not develop as far as the proof suggests. That supports good bourbon, above the ordinary midpoint and below the better integrated Weller Full Proof. The small lead over Bonded is about presence, not a linear proof rule.

At $27.95, substantial flavor and dilution room make this an excellent-value candidate. Wild Turkey 101 remains a more integrated alternative; price and cocktail utility support Value independently of the fairly modest Quality score.

Scope: The current named expression, rather than old No. 8 at a substituted strength. Proof alone does not establish a Quality advantage. 1

Production

Specifications without invented precision

Full proof returns the bourbon to its 125 barrel-entry proof. It is not a claim that each barrel was dumped at 125 proof.

Recipe: Undisclosed · C / R / MB. Age: NAS. Strength: 125 proof / 62.5% ABV. 1

C corn · R rye · W wheat · MB malted barley. Undisclosed proportions are not estimated from neighboring labels. A component’s oldest age is not the age of a blend. Proof is not a quality multiplier.
