Background and history

The producer introduced the limited release in May 2018 for Texas and named additional U.S. states. The Texas bag has a Lone Star detail; a packaging variation does not create another whisky dossier.

The original U.S. suggested price was USD 24.99 / 750 mL. This is historical context only; exact cessation timing and present stock are not established.

Critical review synthesis

Davin de Kergommeaux · Canadian Whisky

De Kergommeaux finds the smoke controlled enough for sweetness, spice and wood to remain perceptible, though the initial presentation is fairly simple.

Scope: May 26, 2018; exact forty-percent Texas Mesquite; full independent account.

Forrest C. Price · The Whiskey Wash

Price finds a less sweet, more savory and saline profile than the aroma suggests, with useful texture and a lingering smoky-spicy ending. He sees a practical cocktail role.

Scope: October 30, 2018; exact Texas Mesquite, USD 25 / 750 mL reference; the infusion method remains unresolved.
