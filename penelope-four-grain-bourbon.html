How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Penelope Four Grain Bourbon is 80 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: sweet fruit and vanilla. The key question is whether that character persists as gentle melon and honey with a little cream.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 80 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is muted wood and spice. Stop early if the already gentle body becomes less distinct.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is gentle melon and honey with a little cream. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 80 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Penelope Four Grain Bourbon.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from sweet fruit and vanilla toward muted wood and spice. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
