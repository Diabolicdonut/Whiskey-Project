The short version

A documented multi-vintage 100%-rye blend whose independent assessments range from flavorful and balanced to flat/short.

Exact identity: October 2024 Alberta Premium Batch 10 Legacy Reserve, 42% ABV. “10” refers to the selected decade/warehouses, not a label age.

Background and history

The producer announced this one-time release in October 2024 using selected 2001–2010 vintages from ten warehouses. It explores maturation-stock diversity rather than introducing a ten-year minimum age.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

Producer documentation establishes 100% prairie rye, ten selected annual vintages and maturation in new white oak, ex-bourbon and ex-Canadian-whisky barrels. The stocks are blended and bottled at 42% ABV; proportions and an explicit label age remain undisclosed.

Critical review synthesis

André, Patrick, Martin and Kim · Québec Whisky

Patrick praises developed fruit/chocolate and a long finish; Martin finds straightforward balance. André describes flatter texture and a short ending, and Kim finds the profile relatively one-dimensional with astringency.

Scope: Exact Batch 10 Legacy Reserve, 42% ABV; four individually signed opinions within one publication.
