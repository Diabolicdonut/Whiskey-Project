Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The independent production account describes triple distillation and oak aging, followed by a distinct Collingwood blend and resting with toasted maplewood staves in a stainless-steel marrying vat. Maplewood is not maple syrup, and this is not the twenty-one-year all-malted-rye recipe. The source reports no more than a year with maplewood, but a fixed duration and a label age for the recurring Original are not established.
