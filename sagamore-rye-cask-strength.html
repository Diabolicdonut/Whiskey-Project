Nose

Pepper, leather and rye spice dominate, tempered by chocolate and toffee; cherry is a quieter accent.

Palate

Dense honey and caramel soften the initial rye attack. Cherry, leather and further pepper keep the sweetness from becoming the whole story.

Finish

Spice gradually recedes, leaving dark fruit, caramel and seasoned wood. Honeyed accents survive the long close.
