The short version

A lighter, distinctive 90-proof 2020 rye with herbal dill, citrus, floral and caramel notes from a Sierra Nevada red-ale-cask cycle; a matched critic finds it surprisingly fruity, sweet and pleasant.

Exact identity: Sagamore Brewer's Select Sierra Nevada Rye Ale Barrel Finish, 2020, 90 proof: 4- and 6-year ryes rested an additional 10 months in former Sagamore rye barrels that had first been used by Sierra Nevada for rye red ale. Distinct from 98-proof Ale Cask Finish.

Background and history

The cross-industry Sagamore/Sierra Nevada partnership recycled barrel influence in both directions: red ale first into old rye casks, then finished rye whiskey back into the former ale casks.

This 2020 Brewer's Select is not the separately listed 98-proof Ale Cask Finish with a different secondary age and provenance.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The initial rye barrels traveled to Sierra Nevada where a rye-enriched red ale matured in them; returned to Sagamore, those same beer-soaked barrels held a blend of four- and six-year straight ryes for a further 10 months. Bottled at 90 proof in May 2020.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Enjoy neat as a light aromatic rye, judging dill, flowers, tropical citrus and slightly malty sweetness.

Rocks

Ice is acceptable but may blunt an already gentle 90-proof finish.

Old Fashioned

Works with light syrup and citrus peel; avoid intense bitters that dominate its subtle ale character.

Manhattan

Use modest sweet vermouth, or choose a dry style, so the delicate malt/fruit accents are not obscured.

Highball

Possible for mixing, but the limited bottle's special finishing deserves an initial neat comparison before dilution.

Serving suggestions are editorial starting points, grounded in documented strength and style; adjust to your taste.
