The short version

A stronger, sweet-and-spicy look at one component of Crown Royal. It is a single-barrel whisky; published sample impressions cannot promise an identical store selection.

Exact identity: Crown Royal Hand Selected Barrel, the NAS single-barrel Coffey Rye component program at 51.5% ABV / 103 proof; Texas introduction followed by 2015 wider rollout.

Background and history

A collector archive dates the first Texas program to 2014. The producer’s 2015 announcement confirms that Texas came first and announces expansion to select U.S. markets, at USD 54.99 / 750 mL.

Each retailer bought a barrel, with a retailer medallion supplied for its bottles. NAS remains NAS: an informal eight-year rumor is not used as an age statement. The two independent 2015 samples establish a style reference rather than universal barrel identity.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The 2015 producer announcement identifies a single-barrel Coffey Rye component distilled at Gimli. Drinkhacker reports its grain percentages and new-oak maturation. The term Coffey Rye identifies this intended Canadian style; it does not establish an all-rye recipe, American straight-rye status or a blended final liquid.

Critical review synthesis

Christopher Null · Drinkhacker

Null enjoys the ripe sweetness, grain/spice detail and energetic ending, while finding it less complex than some other family releases.

Scope: January 30, 2015; Texas-era Hand Selected Barrel at 103 proof; exact sampled retailer/barrel not identified.

Bobby · Adventures in Whiskey

Bobby finds a medium-bodied combination of sweet grain, fruit and warming spice with good persistence. His producer-provided sample corroborates the general style.

Scope: June 3, 2015; 51.5% Hand Selected Barrel sample; photograph credited to Diageo.
