Critical synthesis

Raisin and cherry give the wood some sweetness, but the central problem is proportion: a slender middle leads into a markedly dry finish. The extra oak treatment is easy to notice; its benefit over standard 1910 is less convincing. The saved restrained Quality and conditional Value therefore remain appropriate.
