The short version

A mature three-grain blend with strong evidence for texture and coherent restrained development. It rewards patience, though some tasters want more aromatic force or a longer ending.

Exact identity: Gooderham & Worts Little Trinity Three Grain, 2017 Rare Release, seventeen-year Canadian blend at 45% ABV / 90 proof, 750 mL.

Background and history

Corby announced the first Northern Border Rare Release on September 19, 2017 for Canadian stores beginning in October. Little Trinity’s church tribute is a historical name, not its distilling site or whisky vintage.

The primary announcement specifies CAD 79.95 / 750 mL. A current unavailable/backordered U.S. marketplace premium was not accepted as a regular consumer Value anchor.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The contemporary detailed account describes five spirits: three double-distilled corn streams matured in different vessel regimes, plus once-distilled rye and wheat streams. Every component is at least seventeen years; one corn component is reported as eighteen. New oak, once-used bourbon barrels and more-used vessels describe component maturation, not three sequential finishes. The eighteen-year subset does not make the whole bottle eighteen years old.

Critical review synthesis

Davin de Kergommeaux · Canadian Whisky

De Kergommeaux finds depth and mature wood structure with well-controlled tannins, allowing the grain voices and creamy sweetness to coexist.

Scope: August 29, 2017 preview, exact Little Trinity / 17 / 45%; quoted pre-release price was then provisional.

Mark Gillespie · WhiskyCast

Gillespie finds fresh tartness and sweet supporting structure that persist through a long finish. His account is more emphatic about length than the Connosr sample.

Scope: November 2017, exact seventeen-year / 45% annual release.

OdysseusUnbound · Whisky Connosr

The taster values the thick texture and developed harmony after resting or slight dilution, yet finds the nose restrained and the ending only medium with some tannic dryness. Personal excitement is separated from perceived execution.

Scope: April 11, 2018, exact 45% Little Trinity; the related blog version is not a second independent vote.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Start with a small rested pour to judge texture, sweetness and the wood/spice ending.

Water / rocks

Add a few drops or a small cube and assess how texture and bitter notes change.

Old Fashioned

Use restrained sugar and bitters; the whisky already contributes sweetness and spice.

Manhattan / highball

Try a small serve before using a costly limited bottle; a recurring Canadian blend is the budget comparison.
