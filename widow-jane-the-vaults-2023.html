Background and history

The Vaults 2023 belongs to the annual old-and-rare series begun in 2019. Its documented finishing wood and minimum age define this edition, independently of handwritten ordinary batch numbers.

This 2023 release uses chinquapin oak finishing casks; four-year air-seasoned staves, three-month finish. Wood-air-seasoning time describes the staves before barrel assembly; it is not extra years of whiskey age.

Critical review synthesis

Nick Beiter · Breaking Bourbon

Beiter finds a light earthy/musty start and a velvety but somewhat tannic oak-led palate. The finish improves substantially, unfolding in longer sweet/spicy waves.
