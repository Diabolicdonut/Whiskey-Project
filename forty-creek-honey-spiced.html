Critical review synthesis

Beverage Testing Institute panel · Tastings

The modern panel finds rounded honey and spice with a medium finish, supporting a useful flavored-drink role. Its bourbon wording is treated as analogy, not production documentation.

Scope: July 18, 2024; exact forty-percent Honey Spiced; full independent sensory account.

Chip Dykstra · The Rum Howler Blog

Dykstra’s older Spike sample is dominated by syrupy sweetness, with a short finish and limited neat appeal. Ice and ginger ale make a more successful drink for him.
