Evidence limits and disagreement

The Whiskey Wash enjoys its fruit-led elegance. Distiller’s named expert is also favorable. Breaking Bourbon considers it enjoyable but comparatively straightforward. Source descriptions reverse component proportions, so this pass does not promote either ratio to a producer fact.

Identified expression or historical release; published ratings are not averaged. No claim of blind head-to-head consensus.
