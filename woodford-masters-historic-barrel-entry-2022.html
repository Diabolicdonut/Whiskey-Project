Evidence limits and disagreement

Drinkhacker finds a lively fruit-forward bourbon. Breaking Bourbon praises its aroma but finds the palate much less engaging. The Daily Pour’s own house tasting is also restrained. Its nominal 2022 edition was physically released in February 2023; both dates remain explicit.
