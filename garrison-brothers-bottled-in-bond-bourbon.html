How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Garrison Brothers Bottled in Bond Bourbon is 100 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: maple, chocolate and char. The key question is whether that character persists as richly layered chocolate, spice and leather.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 100 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is tobacco and toasted wood persist. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is richly layered chocolate, spice and leather. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 100 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Garrison Brothers Bottled in Bond Bourbon.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from maple, chocolate and char toward tobacco and toasted wood persist. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
