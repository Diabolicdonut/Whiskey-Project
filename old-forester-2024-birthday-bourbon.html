History and line identity

The 2024 Birthday Bourbon was Old Forester’s 24th annual edition, selected by Chris Morris, Melissa Rift and Caleb Trigo. Its 107-proof strength and 12-year age distinguish it from the surrounding lower-proof annual samples.

Production and identity

The source reports 209 barrels aged in Warehouses G and L, using the 72/18/10 recipe. It remains an unfinished bourbon. The review’s rum-cake aroma is a sensory description, not evidence of rum-cask finishing.

Critical synthesis

The dark raisin-and-molasses opening gives way to a much drier cinnamon-and-leather middle. Spiced cherry adds some definition late, but does not restore the opening’s richness. The saved good Quality and modest Value preserve that development gap at the higher $200 reference.
