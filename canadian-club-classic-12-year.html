Critical review synthesis

Stephanie Moreno · Distiller

Moreno finds nutty vanilla sweetness and slight char, with an ending she considers excessively sweet. The 74-point record supports a limited neat-sipping proposition.

Scope: Exact Classic 12 / 40% ABV named review.

Josh · Sipology

The direct comparison finds Classic 12 thinner and milder than Reserve 9, with modest sweetness, oak and a fast-fading finish. It is mildly recommended as a first pour rather than a forceful sipper.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Use a small neat pour to judge the limited body and sweet finish before choosing it as a sipping bottle.

Rocks / water

Ice may suit the sweetness, but restrained dilution protects the already light texture.

Old Fashioned

Use little syrup and enough bitters to test whether the modest wood and grain remain distinct.

Highball

A modest ginger-ale or soda ratio fits the soft style; compare with the cheaper core for this use.
