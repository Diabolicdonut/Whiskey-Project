The short version

A savory, earthy experiment with unusually positive matched reviews. The mushroom infusion is disclosed as flavoring, while its wood treatments and thick texture give the concept more than novelty.

Exact identity: Bearface Wilderness Series 01 Matsutake, mushroom-infused Canadian whisky at 42.5% ABV; Canadian 2022 review era and December 2023 U.S. debut.

Background and history

WhiskyCast reviewed the 85-proof expression in August 2022. The producer’s U.S. announcement is dated December 5, 2023; the Canadian review era and later American market launch are kept separate.

This is Wilderness Series 01, not the later coastal-water Release 02. The original discovery’s discontinued label is not supported by a formal end-date; limited/historical is the defensible availability description.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The producer blends whisky matured in French wine-related wood and sherry pipes with a separate mushroom-infused component. Fresh and dried matsutake spend two months in a high-proof cask component. The bottle has no authenticated age statement; it does not inherit Triple Oak’s seven-year label.

Critical review synthesis

Mark Gillespie · WhiskyCast

Gillespie finds earthy and pine-like aromatics, a dense mouthfeel, pepper and citrus, followed by a persistent spiced ending. His 92-point judgment strongly supports the execution.

Scope: August 2022 Matsutake, 42.5% ABV.

Jacob Wirt; republished by Beth Squires · The Whiskey Wash

The original tasting finds restrained earthy aroma, richer sweetness and savory leaf-like character on the palate, with an enduring earthy finish. It treats the experiment as rewarding and worth trying.

Scope: Original 2023 tasting, updated June 2025; republication is not a second independent review.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Try neat first so the savory note can be judged in context.

Rocks / water

A few drops or one large cube can test the reported dense texture.

Old Fashioned

Use little syrup and aromatic bitters; a strong sweet mix may hide the infusion.

Highball

Try plain soda and restrained citrus to keep the earthy character clear.
