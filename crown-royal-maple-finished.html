Critical review synthesis

Christopher Null · Drinkhacker

Null finds the maple so dominant that it obscures the whisky and becomes tiring without a very sweet palate. This is material counterevidence to the more enthusiastic review.

Scope: November 30, 2012; exact Maple Finished, 80 proof; B- judgment.

Geoff Kleinman · Drink Spirits

Kleinman finds the strong sweetness effectively offset by oak and spice, with good persistence and enjoyable flavored-whisky balance.

Scope: November 19, 2012; exact 40% expression; independent tasting plus production description.
