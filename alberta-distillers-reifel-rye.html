The short version

A flavorful Calgary rye-forward blend with good published agreement on spice, fruit and lingering dry character. Its bourbon/sherry additions are identified rather than hidden behind a 100%-rye claim.

Exact identity: Reifel Rye, introduced 2022, 42% ABV. The finished blend is rye-forward, not 100% rye or American straight rye.

Background and history

Alberta Distillers’ postwar Calgary operation is separate from the older Windsor/Detroit Prohibition story. The producer’s 80th anniversary in 2026 places its founding in 1946; these modern expressions are not Prohibition-era brands.

Canadian specialist Davin de Kergommeaux described Alberta-distilled rye behind several U.S. bottlings in 2022. That cross-border sourcing is a meaningful American connection; it does not establish every brand’s present supplier or recipe.

The brand honors founding distiller George H. Reifel. Its 2022 introduction and postwar distillery ancestry must not be confused with direct brand participation in U.S. Prohibition.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The producer documents over 90% rye grain, pot distillation and new charred white-oak / ex-bourbon maturation. Mark Gillespie gives the more specific finished-blend account: 91% pot-still rye, 6% Old Grand-Dad bourbon and 3% Oloroso sherry. That reported blend composition is not a single American-style mash bill.

Critical review synthesis

Mark Gillespie · WhiskyCast

Gillespie finds a thick, spicy palate with dried fruit and a long, dry finish. His account also specifies the bourbon and Oloroso additions.

Scope: November 2022, 42% ABV.

Davin de Kergommeaux · Canadian Whisky

De Kergommeaux finds broad creamy fruit/floral development, pepper and herbal depth, ending with rye’s bitter citrus-pith character.

Scope: October 25, 2022 exact Reifel expression.

Flavor Camp Team · Flavor Camp

The team finds pepper, menthol, dark fruit and toffee, then a zesty, slightly prickly palate with bitter oak/cocoa. They regard it as useful for both sipping and cocktails.

Scope: May 18, 2024, 42% ABV. Its Windsor-origin field conflicts with the documented Calgary producer and is not adopted.

Serving recommendations
