The short version

A distinctive Niagara-inspired blend whose independent sample shows youthful wood and thin spots. Five-year whisky-base disclosure is kept separate from a whole-blend label age.

Exact identity: Forty Creek Cherrywood Reserve, 2023 seventeenth annual release, 45% ABV / 90 proof, pot-distilled whisky with aged brandy and cherrywood-stave treatment.

The producer announced the 2023 release ahead of September 23–24 Whisky Weekend. The 2025 upload date of an archive image does not create a new release.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The September 19, 2023 producer announcement specifies a five-year-old pot-distilled whisky base, aged brandy addition and steeping with kiln-dried cherrywood staves. These are not a brandy-cask finish or a claim of cherry-fruit flavoring. The exact archive photograph authenticates Cherrywood Reserve at 45%. No complete-blend numeric label age or brandy minimum age is established.

Critical review synthesis

Jason Hambrey · Whisky Connosr

Hambrey finds some enjoyable fruit and spice, but also fresh-wood character, thin texture and youthful rawness. He considers it difficult to justify above Barrel Select.
