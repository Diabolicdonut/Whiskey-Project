How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow pastry into char

Old Forester The 117 Series: 1910 Extra Old (Batch No. 001) is 93 proof. Let chocolate and candied nuts develop before the chewy finish. Decide whether the extra secondary-barrel influence remains supported by caramel and berry.

Water: protect the toasted middle

At this strength, make only a small comparison addition. Stop when pastry sweetness loses definition and roasted wood takes over.

Old Fashioned: start nearly dry

Begin with very little syrup. Chocolate, toasted caramel and nuts already supply a dessert impression that can become cloying with a standard sweet recipe.

A 1910 comparison

Use the ordinary 1910 at the same temperature. This makes the extra wood residence audible; a large cube can be tried separately if the long dry close is too insistent.
