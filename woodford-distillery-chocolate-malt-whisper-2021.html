Critical evidence

The flavor evidence, with disagreement intact

A synthesis of credited critical accounts. Written notes paired with a YouTube episode and that episode count as one voice. Unavailable transcripts are not inferred from titles.

Nose

Toasted grain, light cocoa and spice.

Palate

Caramel, baked fruit and chocolate, without the full force of the earlier malted-rye bourbon.

Finish

Roasted nuts, cocoa and spice; the chocolate lingers.

Breaking Bourbon

Gentle but clear roasted flavor, with a persistent cocoa/nut ending. 2

Drinkhacker / Christopher Null

Light cocoa aroma develops into a more pronounced chocolate and cherry palate/finish. 4

Bevvy / Eric Twardzik

Cereal, fudge and spice make an enjoyable, distinctive bourbon. 5

The Bourbon Road

Publisher’s own tasting notes emphasize fruit, silky texture and a cocoa finish. 6

Evidence limits and disagreement

Breaking Bourbon describes an approachable roasted layer over familiar Woodford. Drinkhacker finds more chocolate on palate and finish than the name implies. Bevvy and The Bourbon Road’s own tasting notes also support a flavorful result. These accounts concern Whisper, not the 2019 Chocolate Malted Rye bottle.
