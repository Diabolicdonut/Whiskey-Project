History & identity

Sonoma Triple Finish combines bourbon components treated in Pinot Noir casks, brandy casks and former bourbon casks that later held red wine. It does not imply a Chardonnay component or the earlier single Pinot Noir finish. The original N/A price and Value are preserved.

How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: fruit with a tannic turn

Woodford Reserve Master’s Collection Sonoma Triple Finish (2023) (90.4 proof): Follow pastry and red fruit into spice and the oxidized-nut or tea-like ending. The favorable fruit accounts and the critical finish account both matter.

Water: retain the component balance

Woodford Reserve Master’s Collection Sonoma Triple Finish (2023): At 90.4 proof, begin neat. Extra water may weaken the fruit while leaving tannin and tea bitterness disproportionately prominent.

A finish-sequence comparison

Woodford Reserve Master’s Collection Sonoma Triple Finish (2023): Try a small pour beside the 2016 Brandy Cask expression. Assess actual integration rather than assuming three treatments create greater quality.

Old Fashioned: a small low-sugar trial

Woodford Reserve Master’s Collection Sonoma Triple Finish (2023): Let cask fruit provide most of the sweet impression and use bitters sparingly. More syrup does not repair an oxidized or tannic finish.

Evidence limits and disagreement

Secret Whiskey Society and Whiskey and Horror enjoy the fruit and spice despite tannin or heat. Bourbon Culture is less convinced, finding an oxidized, nutty turn on the finish. The combined picture supports good bourbon with a polarizing wine layer.
