The short version

Ordinary rye overall, with a recurring concern about finish integration.

Breaking Bourbon and Barrel Banter find some appealing fruit and spice but an unrefined or artificial-tasting finish in their samples. ScotchNoob finds a more balanced sweet-bitter profile that improves with water. The ordinary Overholt/Double Rye midpoint fits this spread. Rye Quality remains provisional.

At $39.95 NC, good approaching fair value fits an accessible but contested rye. Sazerac 90 at $30.95 and Wild Turkey 101 Rye at $27.95 provide cheaper reviewed alternatives.

Palate

Sweet-bitter rye with variable integration.

Finish
