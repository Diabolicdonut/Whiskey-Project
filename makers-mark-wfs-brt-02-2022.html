Critical evidence

Critical synthesis

A synthesis of credited critical accounts. Written notes paired with a YouTube episode and that episode count as one voice. Unavailable transcripts are not inferred from titles.

Nose

Chocolate, brown sugar, pastry and dried fruit, with earthy rickhouse or roasted-nut accents.

Palate

Caramel, toffee and fruit meet a rounded texture, with spice and earthy oak preventing a purely sugary profile.

Finish

Warm dessert notes, cocoa, fruit and clove; more composed than BRT-01 in several comparisons.

Drinkhacker / Christopher Null

Prefers BRT-02’s chocolate, brown sugar and fruit/spice balance to BRT-01. 4

The Whiskey Ramble / Xenoraiser

Rich dessert/rickhouse character with balancing earth and spice; stronger finish and a clear preference over BRT-01. 5

Distiller / Amanda Schuster

Named expert tasting: dried fruit, cocoa, nuts and a rich finish balanced by tartness and heat. 6

Whiskey for the Ages / Hannah Dawson

Material dissent: enjoyable but substantially less impressive than BRT-01. 7

Evidence limits and disagreement

Drinkhacker and The Whiskey Ramble prefer BRT-02’s balance. Hannah Dawson is markedly less impressed than with BRT-01. That contrary assessment remains part of the synthesis rather than being omitted.
