Evidence limits and disagreement

InsideHook enjoys the bourbon’s rye spice. Tasting Table likes its body while finding sourness and a long astringent turn. Only two meaningful independent accounts were established, so this is a tentative ‡ assessment, not an elite inference from its proof.
