Critical evidence

The flavor evidence, with disagreement intact

Editorial synthesis of the credited independent tasting narratives. Producer intent and specifications are separate evidence. Paired channel/article notes count once; inaccessible video transcripts are not inferred.

Nose

Soft fruit, bakery sweetness and light wood.

Palate

Vanilla pastry and fruit with limited body and development.

Finish

Modest persistence; bitterness and dryness emerge in some accounts.

Breaking Bourbon / Jordan Moskal

A promising soft nose gives way to a muted palate and unsatisfying finish. 2

Bourbon Culture / Mike & Mike

Finds pleasant bakery and fruit notes, but thin mouthfeel and a medium, sometimes dry or bitter close. 5

Distiller / Jake Emen

Editorial tasting notes describe nutty sweetness before an astringent wood-and-mint ending. 6
