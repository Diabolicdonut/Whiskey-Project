Evidence limits and disagreement

Several accounts praise the mature fruit, pastry and richer texture. Bourbon Culture favors it over 2023 while finding a varnish-like oak note; VinePair is less impressed by the simpler finish, and Bourbon Guy finds the heat easier with water. Those reservations keep the assessment below the category ceiling.
