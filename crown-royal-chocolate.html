Critical review synthesis

Christopher Null · Drinkhacker

Null finds an appealing cocoa-led dessert pour with little discernible whisky character. It succeeds for him as a chocolate drink rather than a spirit-forward whisky.

Scope: November 24, 2025; exact Chocolate 70-proof review, separate from Marquis on the same page.

Jeff Ellingson · Best Tasting Spirits

Ellingson finds chocolate and baking-spice flavors with a less cloying texture than many flavored whiskies, and substantially more audible whisky character than Null does.

Scope: August 27, 2025; exact 35% ABV Chocolate sample; site generally tests chilled unless stated otherwise.
