October 5, 2026. The accepted 94-dossier v5 collection remains the baseline. All 36 previously unscored Quality records were triaged; existing complete research was not restarted. New searches targeted likely resolvable evidence gaps.

**One tentative assessment was added: Jeptha Creed Four Grain — Quality 6.0, Value 5.0 at the unchanged $52.99 producer reference.** It is visibly marked as limited confidence. The modern published sample reports NAS, the panel’s entrant Vintage 4 field is not independently verified age, and one community account identifies a four-year label. Older two-year praise remains context. The new number describes an evidence-weighted middle-scale assessment, not matched-batch consensus.

An educational retailer page listing an incorrect 90 proof was rejected. Source limitations and five conditional shopping bands are in the [updated dossier](jeptha-creed-four-grain.html).

A newly found Honey Bourbon account reports a different 90%-corn recipe; matching proof alone cannot establish current identity. These pages gain context, not inherited scores. A repeated Honey Toasted article is the same publication, not a second independent vote.

The other 35 Quality records remain N/A, and 32 Value records remain N/A. Existing supported Value scores in unanchored categories are retained; unanchored Quality is not borrowed from bourbon or rye. No old numeric Quality or Value score changes, and no price, proof, recipe, age or family expansion is overwritten.
