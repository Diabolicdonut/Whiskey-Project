How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: aroma through finish

Woodford Reserve Master’s Collection Select American Oak (2018) (90.4 proof): Give the sweet oak and fruit time to open, then follow the subdued palate and dry wood. Complexity in the nose does not establish persistence.

Water: a cautious separate sample

Woodford Reserve Master’s Collection Select American Oak (2018): At 90.4 proof, drops may reduce an already timid profile. Stop if fruit weakens while bitter wood remains conspicuous.

An oak-origin comparison

Woodford Reserve Master’s Collection Select American Oak (2018): Use small pours beside another separately identified wood experiment. Keep grain recipe, oak origin and finishing sequence distinct.

Avoid solving bitterness with syrup

Woodford Reserve Master’s Collection Select American Oak (2018): Heavy sugar can inflate the sweet opening while leaving the short dry close unchanged. Judge the neat ending before a cocktail.
