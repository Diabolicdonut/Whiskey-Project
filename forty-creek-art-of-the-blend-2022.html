Background and history

The producer identifies this as a 2022 limited creation marking Bill Ashburn’s thirty-fifth year with the business. The later 2023 independent reviews are review dates, not a second release.

The Ontario producer page lists CAD 89.95; Bradley’s February 2023 review gives approximately CAD 89. These Canadian references do not establish a U.S. market price or Value score.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

The producer describes Niagara whisky with winter-harvested grape influence; its timeline is more specific about a hint of Winter Harvest Wine and lists Lot 16 with 9,600 bottles. This authenticates a blending addition, not a disclosed wine-cask duration. The exact producer photograph carries the Art of the Blend identity and 45% strength, without a numeric age.

Critical review synthesis

Kurt Bradley · The Gentleman’s Flavor

Bradley finds an accessible, delicately layered profile with an appreciable ending, but notes youth and a missing layer of development at the asking price.

Scope: Reviewed February 12, 2023; exact 2022 Art of the Blend at 45%; editorial page posted May 2023.

Chip Dykstra · The Rum Howler Blog

Dykstra finds breadth and complexity but also heat, solvent-like impressions and fruit/grain elements that do not fully cohere. Ice improves his experience.

Scope: June 14, 2023; full exact 45% Art of the Blend review; no producer notes counted as independent tasting.
