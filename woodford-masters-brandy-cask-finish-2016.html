Critical evidence

The flavor evidence, with disagreement intact

A synthesis of credited critical accounts. Written notes paired with a YouTube episode and that episode count as one voice. Unavailable transcripts are not inferred from titles.

Nose

Berry pastry, caramel and gentle spice.

Palate

Sweet fruit and vanilla; texture ranges from creamy to notably thin.

Finish

Oak and dryness; some reviewers find the ending short or astringent.

Breaking Bourbon / Jordan Moskal

Fruit-led aroma, but thin delivery and a drying, astringent finish. 2

The Whiskey Reviewer / Richard Thomas

A favorable account of fruit, caramel and soft, balanced finishing. 4

Adventures in Whiskey / Bobby

Creamy fruit and caramel with a dry oak ending; supplied sample disclosed. 5

Bourbon Guy / Eric Burke

Ripe fruit and oak, with a relatively short finish and less enthusiasm. 6

Evidence limits and disagreement

The Whiskey Reviewer and Adventures in Whiskey enjoy the fruit and balance. Breaking Bourbon finds a thin palate and an astringent ending; Bourbon Guy is also less impressed. This is materially mixed evidence, not a uniformly excellent finished bourbon.
