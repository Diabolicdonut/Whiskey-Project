Background and history

The original Three Grain launched in 2000. This 2020 twentieth-anniversary edition is separately identified from that original and 2015 Three Grain Harmony, with 9,200 bottles announced by the producer.

The July 21, 2020 independent review predates the October 19 producer announcement. Neither date alone proves the first sale. Original Ontario price is retained in CAD; no dated exact U.S. retail price is authenticated.

Critical review synthesis

Davin de Kergommeaux · Canadian Whisky

De Kergommeaux finds generous texture, fruit and barley character with expressive heat and rye spice. His assessment favors the revived profile, without establishing a native reference-class endpoint.

Scope: July 21, 2020; explicit Twentieth Anniversary / 40%, not original Three Grain.

Women’s Whisky World tasting writer · Women’s Whisky World

The writer finds an involved combination of spice, corn sweetness and barley character, with a dry-sweet ending. Water changes the emphasis, and slow assessment is preferred.

Scope: June 14, 2021 feature; exact Three Grain 20th Anniversary / 40% tasting section, separated from the accompanying producer interview.
