Critical review synthesis

Beverage Testing Institute panel · Tastings

The panel finds an animated, grain-led whisky with a pleasant dry texture and refreshing medium ending. Its description remains youthful rather than treating fourteen years as proof of deep maturity.

Scope: Tasted June 18, 2024, exact 43% fourteen-year; panel grade is not mechanically converted.

Bryan Vanderkruk · Toronto Whisky Society

Vanderkruk praises the delicate but coherent interplay of malt and wood, with creamy texture. His listed short ending remains a limitation alongside the favorable conclusion.

Scope: December 19, 2017, exact Rare 14 / 43%; adjacent 40% Ice review is excluded.

Johanne McInnis · Whisky Lassie

McInnis experiences an intensely unpleasant perfume/soap character on repeated attempts. She also reports that other tasters did not share that perception, so it is preserved as a serious personal response rather than a universal contamination claim.

Scope: July 2015, exact fourteen-year experience; contrary reactions are explicit.
