How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: dark rather than bright

Stagg Bourbon Batch 25B (126.9 proof): Let the coffee, molasses and cocoa emerge before deciding whether the dry wood is supporting the fruit or suppressing it. This batch’s straightforward profile is the relevant reference.

Water: make coffee and fruit legible

Stagg Bourbon Batch 25B: At 126.9 proof, compare a few drops with an untouched pour. Stop if the added water leaves dry wood exposed after the sweet middle recedes.

Old Fashioned: a light sugar trial

Stagg Bourbon Batch 25B: The dark sweets already supply weight. Start with little syrup, aromatic bitters and measured dilution; extra sugar need not repair a wood-led finish.

Rocks: monitor the wood

Stagg Bourbon Batch 25B: A large cube can ease the initial strength. Taste before it melts substantially, because cooling may reduce the fruit sooner than the dry oak.
