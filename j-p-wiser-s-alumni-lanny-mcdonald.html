Critical review synthesis

Selfbuilt · Selfbuilt’s Whisky Analysis

Selfbuilt enjoys a soft, distinctive combination of anise, sweet grain, nut and tobacco-like notes, with manageable bitter wood. He also records faint solvent-like and stale-nut impressions, without treating these as chemical findings.

Scope: November 13, 2019, exact nine-year / 40% sample; identifying a wheat component does not prove every flavor originates from wheat.

The Gentleman’s Flavor panel · The Gentleman’s Flavor

The panel finds an agreeable creamy nose but a palate whose pine, fruit and honey do not form a coherent whole, followed by a short and thin finish. This is meaningful dissent from the favorable character assessment.
