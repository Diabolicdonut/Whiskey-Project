History and line identity

Old Forester expanded its retailer single-barrel program to 100-proof and barrel-strength versions after announcing the change in 2019. The barrel-strength selection keeps the Shively bourbon’s concentrated form rather than applying a common retail proof. This 130-proof review is one sample within that program. Other picks can differ substantially, and the more restrained 100-proof single-barrel expression remains a separate purchase proposition.
