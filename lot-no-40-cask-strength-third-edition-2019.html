Critical review synthesis

Selfbuilt · Selfbuilt’s Whisky Analysis

The bottle has a thick, sweet/spicy palate, but oak masks some familiar grain/floral detail. Slight bitterness and a quicker ending improve with a few drops of water; excess dilution quickly thins it.

Scope: November 27, 2019 exact 57% purchased Third Edition; younger-stock speculation is not an age statement.

Bourbon Culture · Bourbon Culture

The 114-proof bottle gives a distinctive creamy coffee-like profile with controlled heat and a softer rye ending. The reviewer likes its execution while finding it unlike the earlier rye-led editions.

Scope: June 9, 2020. Expectations about rye style do not automatically become faults in the Canadian calibration.
