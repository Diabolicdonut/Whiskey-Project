How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Michter’s US*1 Original Sour Mash Whiskey is 86 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: caramel and apple introduce nutmeg. The key question is whether that character persists as toffee and coffee-cake sweetness fill the middle.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 86 proof, taste neat before adding water; dilution is an experiment, not an improvement guaranteed by the label. The relevant finishing behavior is spice and dry warmth rise sharply late. Stop early if the already gentle body becomes less distinct.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is toffee and coffee-cake sweetness fill the middle. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. At 86 proof, restrained dilution preserves the body; the cocktail should still taste of Michter’s US*1 Original Sour Mash Whiskey.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from caramel and apple introduce nutmeg toward spice and dry warmth rise sharply late. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
