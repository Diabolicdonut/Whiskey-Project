Evidence limits and disagreement

Adventures in Whiskey enjoys the creamy sweet profile. The Whiskey Reviewer finds a harsh aromatic edge but a more pleasing sip. Breaking Bourbon calls the palate ordinary and the experiment dull. These are not unanimous high scores, and the bourbon’s rarity does not resolve the disagreement.
