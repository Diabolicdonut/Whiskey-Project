Background and history

The Vaults 2019 belongs to the annual old-and-rare series begun in 2019. Its documented finishing wood and minimum age define this edition, independently of handwritten ordinary batch numbers.

This 2019 release uses new oak finishing casks raised from staves air-seasoned eight years. Wood-air-seasoning time describes the staves before barrel assembly; it is not extra years of whiskey age.

Critical review synthesis

Richard Thomas · The Whiskey Reviewer

Thomas finds vanilla/brown-sugar sweetness with mint and evergreen spice, then nuts/oak. He enjoys the blend but identifies a quick, insubstantial finish as the main limitation.

Serving recommendations

Neat

Rocks / water

Old Fashioned

Use a small, low-syrup trial if mixing; the finishing treatment deserves a neat assessment first.

Manhattan

Try a restrained vermouth measure so the older wood and finish retain their distinction.
