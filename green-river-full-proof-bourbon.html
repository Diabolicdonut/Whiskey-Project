How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: follow the development

Green River Full Proof Bourbon is 117.3 proof. Use a small tulip glass and compare the opening with the middle: candied fruit, caramel and vanilla. The key question is whether that character persists as creamy toffee and seasoned oak.

Water: adjust in small steps

At 117.3 proof, add a few drops to test whether the documented warmth releases more flavor rather than simply flattening it. The relevant finishing behavior is dark fruit and char persist with heat. Stop when the mid-palate loses definition or dry wood begins to dominate.

Old Fashioned: match the sweetness

The documented middle is creamy toffee and seasoned oak. Begin with less sugar than usual and taste before adding more. The extra proof leaves room for ice dilution, while bitters can frame the spice.

Rocks or a restrained Sour

A separate pour over one large cube lets you track the change from candied fruit, caramel and vanilla toward dark fruit and char persist with heat. For a Sour, balance citrus and sugar in a small trial; the documented finish is the part most easily obscured.
