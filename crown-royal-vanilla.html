Critical review synthesis

Christopher Null · Drinkhacker

Null finds overwhelmingly sweet vanilla with only modest additional development. He considers it serviceable for simple mixed drinks, rather than an especially rewarding neat pour.

Scope: December 5, 2016; exact 70-proof Vanilla.

G-LO and Limpd · It’s Just the Booze Dancing

The two tasters find convincing vanilla and useful spice but differ on heat and sweetness. G-LO finds a bitter cocktail improves balance; both place it closer to dessert drinking than bold whisky.

Scope: December 27, 2016; exact 35% Vanilla, disclosed supplied sample; one publisher with two tasters, not two independent publications.
