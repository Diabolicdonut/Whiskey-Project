How to approach this expression

Serving recommendations are editorial inferences from this expression’s documented sensory profile and strength; they are not a claim of first-hand cocktail testing.

Neat: compare aroma with weight

Old Forester 2018 Birthday Bourbon is 101 proof. Follow the deep sweet opening into the lighter palate. Give tobacco and pepper time to arrive before deciding whether the middle carries enough concentration.

Water: preserve the lighter palate

Try a few drops in a second glass rather than proofing down the whole pour. Stop if caramel and tobacco lose definition together.

A vintage flight

Use the 2019 release in an equal small pour. Its extra proof and different age make it a separate comparison, not additional evidence for the 2018 bottle.

Ice or cocktails: buy for the taste

One large cube or a restrained Old Fashioned is reasonable if the bottle is already open. Heavy sweetness can obscure the lighter middle that makes the quality judgment conditional.
