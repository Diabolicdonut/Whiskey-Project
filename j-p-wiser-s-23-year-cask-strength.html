The short version

An excellent provisional Canadian blend built around rich texture, mature sweetness and substantial spice. The final retail strength is 64.3%; stronger draft or single-cask samples are not interchangeable tasting evidence.

Exact identity: J.P. Wiser’s 23 Year Old Cask Strength Blend, 2019 Northern Border / Rare Cask Series final retail bottling, 64.3% ABV / 128.6 proof, 750 mL.

Background and history

The 2019 Northern Border Collection moved the Wiser’s slot from earlier thirty-five-year / 50% releases to a twenty-three-year cask-strength blend. The change in age is a release identity, not evidence of lower quality.

The exact final bottle is documented in Selfbuilt’s December 10, 2019 review. TOModera’s later post explicitly concerns a single-cask sample rather than the standard retail release; its contrary score is not silently pooled with the final blend.

For the shared historical context, read Canadian whisky, cross-border trade and the American market. The release chronology above distinguishes the current bottle from its brand’s earlier history.

Producer, grains, aging and finishing

Selfbuilt’s exact retail bottle and talexander’s matched tasting authenticate a blend of corn and rye aged at least twenty-three years. The photographed label reads 64.3% and 750 mL, correcting the discovery ledger’s 64.5% shorthand. Selfbuilt distinguishes higher-strength draft samples from the final retail blend. His suggestion that re-char casks must be involved is inference, not a confirmed whole-blend cask specification.

Critical review synthesis

Selfbuilt · Selfbuilt’s Whisky Analysis

Selfbuilt finds luxurious texture, sweet spice and a relatively clean, lasting finish. Water exposes fruit but can increase bitterness, so he suggests restraint. He finds it excellent, though short of his highest complexity tier.

Scope: December 10, 2019, purchased final 64.3% / 23-year retail blend; preliminary higher-strength samples explicitly separated.

talexander · Whisky Connosr

talexander enjoys an elegant, forceful nose, thick mouthfeel and long finish, but finds the hot palate less balanced and somewhat one-note. Water brings more spice and wood. He favors the eighteen and earlier thirty-five for balance.

Scope: April 23, 2020, 23 years / 64.3%; independent matched final-blend dissent retained.
