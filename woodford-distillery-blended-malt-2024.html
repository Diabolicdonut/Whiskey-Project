Critical evidence

The flavor evidence, with disagreement intact

Editorial synthesis of the credited independent tasting narratives. Producer intent and specifications are separate evidence. Paired channel/article notes count once; inaccessible video transcripts are not inferred.

Nose

Malty sweet/sour grain, preserved fruit and toffee.

Palate

Fruit gives way to grain, spice and substantial wood; refined integration is incomplete.

Finish

Dry fruit, cocoa and toasted wood; medium length in VinePair.

VinePair / David Thomas Tao

The matched 90.4-proof 2024 blend develops fruit and grain but shows alcohol prominence, drying tannin and a medium-length finish; verifies $65/375 mL. 3

Whisky Advocate / Shane English
